## 1. The reopening question, stated up front

The research prompt for this session named a specific, narrow question. F238 is titled (in full) *"the geon relic abundance is a genuinely free input, and here is exactly why."* The prompt asked: is that "why" itself the reopening condition — i.e., does it leave room for a different production mechanism to sidestep the exclusion — or is it a permanent, channel-independent obstruction?

**Answer: the "why" is channel-specific.** F238's argument is a five-link causal chain:

$$\Omega_\text{DM}\ \longleftarrow\ \beta(M_\text{form})\ \longleftarrow\ \sigma(k_\text{PBH})\ \longleftarrow\ \text{primordial curvature }P(k)\ \longleftarrow\ \text{inflaton potential (absent)}.$$

Every link in that chain is about the **Press–Schechter route**: a primordial black hole forms when a super-horizon curvature perturbation, generated by an inflaton (or an inflaton-adjacent mechanism) and later re-entering the horizon, exceeds a collapse threshold $\delta_c$ on smoothing scale $M$. F238 §6 is explicit about what it searched for and did not find: *"a search of `findings/` and `ca-simulation/` returns no inflaton finding, no preheating finding, and no primordial-power-spectrum module."* Every noun in that sentence is inflaton-adjacent. **Nothing in F238 addresses, or is worded to cover, a PBH-formation mechanism that never invokes $\sigma(k)$ at all.**

## 2. Does F282/F283/F284/F285 close the gap generally?

Before treating this as open, the obvious question is whether the four findings that followed F238 and hardened its conclusion — F282 (no slow-roll inflaton can exist), F283 (the obstruction is scale-invariant, not a spacing artifact), F284 (expansion is the conformal mode on a rigid lattice, not substrate stretching), F285 (no principled measure on the $t=0$ state reproduces $n_s=0.9649$) — close this off too. Reading each with that question in mind:

- **F282** proves no slow-roll inflaton field can exist on this lattice (every compact CA direction has $M_\text{Pl}^2/f^2\ge\sqrt3$). This forecloses *field-dynamical* generation of a primordial spectrum. It says nothing about *defect-network* formation, which needs no rolling field at all — only a potential with degenerate discrete vacua, which the model already has (§3).
- **F283/F284** establish the lattice is rigid and the obstruction is exact and scale-free. Neither touches production mechanisms; they are about whether a slow-roll field or an elastic substrate could exist, not about topological defects.
- **F285** proves no measure on the $t=0$ state gives the *observed* CMB tilt $n_s=0.9649$. Its own generic-measure result is, if anything, evidence *for* this finding's argument rather than against it (§4).

**The one argument close enough to need explicit handling is F282 §6's exclusion of "causal seeds."** F282's escape-route table excludes a "criticality substitute (causal seeds)" — a lattice-native, causal, sub-horizon mechanism generating $P(k)$ — citing that it "produces incoherent, active-source perturbations, excluded by the observed acoustic-peak phase coherence" (Durrer, Kunz & Melchiorri 2002; the classic topological-defect-vs-inflation CMB argument, Albrecht et al., Pen–Seljak–Turok). This is the historical debate over whether cosmic strings or textures could replace inflation as the source of the **CMB temperature power spectrum and acoustic peaks** — and it was won decisively by inflation because causal (sub-horizon-generated, incoherent across super-horizon patches) sources cannot reproduce the observed coherent acoustic-peak structure at degree angular scales.

**This does not apply here, and the reason is scale.** Domain-wall-collapse PBH formation is never asked to explain the CMB power spectrum. It operates entirely at the domain-wall network's own formation scale — for the F228 remnant's required $M_\text{form}\sim10^4$–$10^8$ g, a horizon mass corresponding to a temperature scale of order $10^{10}$–$10^{13}$ GeV, some 20+ orders of magnitude in energy above anything the CMB probes. The model's existing $\Lambda$CDM sector (F182/F188) is untouched: whatever process eventually supplies the CMB's own $P(k)$ (still open, per F285) is unaffected by a completely separate, much-earlier, much-smaller-scale defect-collapse event. **F282 §6 excludes causal seeds from playing the role of explaining large-scale coherent structure. Domain-wall-collapse PBH formation is never cast in that role**, so the exclusion's own stated scope does not reach it.

## 3. The precondition, checked exactly

If a domain-wall-collapse channel is to be model-native rather than a hand-added mechanism, it needs a field with degenerate discrete vacua — the textbook Kibble-mechanism setup. The model already has one, and it costs nothing to check: the $E_g$ second-shell condensate's clock angle $\delta$, whose potential is *already fully derived* (F150 architecture, F175/F234 the $\delta^*=\tfrac29$ weight-as-phase result), reused here verbatim from F282's own `eg_clock_coefficient`:

$$V(\delta) = \lambda_6\, e_\text{sat}^6\, \cos^23\delta = \frac{A}{2}\big(1+\cos6\delta\big),\qquad A=\lambda_6 e_\text{sat}^6 = 0.0376903\ldots$$

Two facts about this potential, checked symbolically (sympy, not a numerical scan) and independently re-derived in the test with a *different* simplification path (`trigsimp` rather than the module's `expand_trig`+`simplify`) so the check is not just re-running the same code:

$$\boxed{\ \cos(6\delta)=-1\ \text{has exactly 6 solutions in }[0,2\pi):\quad \delta = \frac{\pi}{6}+\frac{n\pi}{3},\ n=0,\ldots,5.\ }$$

$$\boxed{\ V(\delta+\tfrac{\pi}{3})-V(\delta)\equiv0\quad\text{identically (symbolic, }A\text{ left free).}\ }$$

The second identity is the stronger statement: it is not "6 minima that happen to coincide numerically," it is an **exact discrete $\mathbb{Z}_6$ symmetry** of the potential — $\delta\to\delta+\pi/3$ leaves $V$ unchanged for *every* value of $\delta$, not merely at the minima. Six exactly-degenerate discrete vacua under an exact discrete symmetry is precisely the setup in which the Kibble mechanism generically produces a domain-wall network: if $\delta$ settles into one of the six wells independently in causally-disconnected patches, walls form at the boundaries between patches that chose different wells, with wall number $N_\text{DW}=6$.

**Nothing here is new physics.** F234 already derived $\lambda_6=0.243$ from $\delta^*=\tfrac29$ (not a fit); F118 already derived $e_\text{sat}$; F282 already wrote down $V(\delta)$ in exactly this form to compute a slow-roll obstruction. This finding asks a different question of an already-existing object — a question F234 never had reason to ask, because F234 was never examining domain-wall formation.

## 4. Why the precondition is expected to be *activated*, not merely available

A potential with degenerate discrete vacua is not automatically a source of domain walls — the field could instead be pinned into one vacuum everywhere from $t=0$ "by fiat," with no wall ever forming. Whether that is what happens is a dynamical question this finding does not resolve (§6). But there is a model-internal reason to expect the opposite, and it comes from a finding that never considered $\delta$ at all.

**F285 §2 (D1)** established that the *generic, natural* measure on the model's own $t=0$ lattice state is **short-range-correlated**: any local functional of independent-per-cell or thermal/gapped fields gives white-noise statistics ($n_s=0$) for the energy density, because a locally-correlated field has no reason to agree with itself across super-horizon distances. F285 used this to argue the density field $\rho$ is generically white noise absent a special mechanism. **The same argument, applied to $\delta$ rather than $\rho$, says $\delta$ has no reason to share the same one of its six vacua across causally disconnected patches** — precisely because F282 and F285 have *already* closed off every mechanism (inflation, causal-seed super-horizon coherence) that would make $\delta$ correlated over cosmological distances. This is not a new derivation; it is F285's own "generic case" result, read for a field F285 never considered, and it points the same direction the Kibble mechanism needs: independent vacuum selection in causally separated regions.

This argument is recorded here, not re-derived numerically — there is nothing further to compute; F285's D1 result is stated in terms that apply to any lattice field with no special correlating mechanism, and $\delta$ is such a field once F282/F285 close off the alternatives.

## 6. What remains open — named, not hand-waved

Turning "the Kibble-mechanism precondition is exactly satisfied" into "an abundance is derived" needs four more pieces, none of which this finding supplies:

1. **A causally-early dynamics for $\delta$** establishing that it actually *settles* into one of the 6 wells via a horizon-limited process (argued from F285's own D1 result in §4, not separately derived — §4's argument is suggestive, not a proof that settling happens at any particular epoch).
2. **Confirmation the settling is a genuine phase transition** capable of leaving a real $\mathbb{Z}_6$ wall network rather than a smooth, wall-free relaxation. No finite-temperature (or other causally-early) effective potential for the $E_g$ condensate exists in the model yet — F282/F118/F234 all work with the zero-temperature potential.
3. **The wall tension $\sigma_\text{wall}$ in physical units.** This needs the $E_g$ doublet's canonical kinetic-term normalisation, which F282's own 2026-09-04 update ([[F363-k4-inflation-reopening-checked-nogo-survives]]) explicitly flags as unresolved: *"whether the $E_g$ doublet's kinetic term is exactly canonical (assumed here, never independently derived)."* Without this, $A=0.0376903$ is a dimensionless lattice number, not an energy density.
4. **A network-collapse-fraction computation** (the wall-collision PBH yield) — standard in the literature cited above but not yet built on this lattice — **and** a resolution of the generic $\mathbb{Z}_6$ domain-wall overclosure problem: a long-lived discrete-symmetry domain-wall network run to today catastrophically overcloses the universe (the textbook "domain wall problem"), so this scenario needs *either* an explicit small bias breaking the exact vacuum degeneracy (new physics not currently in the model — and a bias would need its own derivation, not a free parameter) *or* a demonstration that the network collapses to PBHs within a few Hubble times of formation, before ever reaching the scaling regime that would dominate the energy density. Convenient framing, not a free pass: "the walls must collapse fast" is exactly the same requirement whether read as "avoid the domain-wall problem" or "produce PBHs promptly," but it is a *dynamical criterion that has to be checked*, not something guaranteed by the potential's shape alone.

None of these four is a quick close. Each is a genuine, separate piece of physics — comparable in scope to a full finding on its own (item 3 alone, the kinetic-term normalisation, has been an open thread since F282's own 2026-09-04 update). This finding's honest contribution is narrower: it establishes that the *door is open*, names exactly what is behind it, and confirms — at zero cost, reusing existing constants — that the one precondition checkable without any of the four open pieces is exactly satisfied.

## 7. Verdict

> **The reopening condition is real, but partial.** F238's "here is exactly why" is provably scoped to the standard inflaton/Press–Schechter production route: its own §6 names the missing sector as the inflaton/preheating/primordial-spectrum sector, and every subsequent hardening (F282's proof no inflaton can exist; F285's proof no measure fixes $n_s$) sharpens that same, single route. Neither excludes, or was ever tested against, a topological-defect production channel — and F282 §6's one argument close enough to matter (the acoustic-peak-coherence exclusion of causal seeds) is scoped to explaining the CMB's own large-scale power spectrum, a role this channel is never asked to play.
>
> A structurally distinct channel — $\mathbb{Z}_6$ domain-wall collapse sourced by the model's own already-derived $E_g$ clock potential — is not excluded, and its Kibble-mechanism precondition (exact discrete $\mathbb{Z}_6$ symmetry, 6 exactly-degenerate global minima) is confirmed exact-algebraically here, reusing existing model content at zero cost. **This is not a re-derivation of $\Omega_\text{DM}$, and it does not move K8's grade.** K8 stays EXCLUDED for the inflaton/Press–Schechter route, correctly — F238 is unchanged and remains the right closure of the question it actually asked. What changes is the row's *characterization*: from "no route can ever derive this abundance" (an overreading F238 itself never stated, but that CL209's `no_go` kind invited) to "the standard route is provably and permanently closed; a structurally distinct, non-inflationary route is open in principle, its precondition already satisfied, and its full derivation — four named pieces, none of them small — is untouched." **What promotes the grade, stated precisely (per the session's own acceptance criterion):** nothing in this finding promotes K8. ## 8. Falsifiers

1. **The $E_g$ condensate never passes through a symmetric or undecided phase.** If a future finding shows $\delta$ is dynamically pinned to a single value across the whole lattice from $t=0$ by some mechanism this session did not identify — contradicting §4's application of F285's own generic-measure argument — the Kibble-mechanism setup does not fire and this channel closes.
2. **The wall network cannot avoid the domain-wall problem for any wall tension.** If item 4 of §6, worked out, shows overclosure is unavoidable regardless of the (currently unfixed) tension scale, the channel closes on dynamical grounds even though the precondition holds.
3. **A demonstration that F282 §6's acoustic-peak-coherence argument does, after all, reach sub-horizon defect-collapse mechanisms** (contra §2's reading) — would remove the basis for treating this as un-excluded.
4. **Positive resolution.** If items 1–4 of §6 are computed and $\beta(M_\text{form})$ lands in the F228 band without the vacuum-degeneracy bias or prompt-collapse assumption failing, this becomes the first *derived* geon abundance in the model — the strongest possible outcome, and the one this finding deliberately does not claim.
