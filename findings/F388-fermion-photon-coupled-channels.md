**The answer.** New `core.coupled.EmPhotonChannel` and `FermionEmChannel`. `em_photon`: sources `(E,B)` with the partner fermion's F384 conserved current, free-rotates at `Ω_pair` (`gauge.photon.photon_step_spectral`, decision 5), and publishes `A` via F386's Coulomb-gauge solve (`charge_coupling.solve_A_coulomb_3d`). `fermion_em`: advances the charged Weyl fermion by F385's per-link covariant step (`minimal_coupling.u1_link_weyl_step_3d_bcc`), reading `A` from the partner. Registration order (`em_photon` before `fermion_em`) gives the documented pre-tick/post-tick contract — verified directly (§3). **The wiring is correct and the gate is green, but §2 below is the load-bearing result of this finding, not a footnote to it: this specific loop cannot yet close a genuine two-way radiative back-reaction, and a reader should not take "7/7 PASS" to mean it does.**

**The one deviation from the roadmap's own sketch, and why.** The roadmap's Stage 4 text says `em_photon` sources `E += g·J` from the whole current, applied to the whole `Ω_pair`-rotated field. That is exactly the recipe F386 §5 found broken (a genuine `iĈ·B≠0` "no magnetic monopoles" violation) and F387 fixed with a concrete, machine-precision-verified split-sourcing recipe (`gauge.em_photon_sourcing.split_sourced_scenario`): source only the current's `Ĉ(k)`-transverse part through the `Ω_pair` rotation, and carry the longitudinal (Coulomb) sector algebraically via the model's own continuity/Gauss-law machinery, never populating `B_L`. `EmPhotonChannel` implements F387's fix directly rather than the roadmap's original (superseded) sketch — the first place that fix is exercised under a genuinely **dynamical** current (F387's own harness used a static one, disclosed there as unverified — see §2 for why that verification turns out to be less of a stress test than it first appears).

## 1. The channels

```python
EmPhotonChannel:     type_name="em_photon",    topologies=("bcc",)
FermionEmChannel:    type_name="fermion_em",   topologies=("bcc",)
```

`em_photon` per tick: `J = em_current.conserved_current(partner)`; `J_T, J_L = em_photon_sourcing.split_transverse_longitudinal(J)`; free-rotate `(E,B)` by `Ω_pair`; `E += g_lat·J_T`; advance `ρ` by `∂_tρ=−iĈ·J` (F384's own continuity law) and solve `E_L` algebraically from `iĈ·E_L=ρ`; publish `A = solve_A_coulomb_3d(B)` (F386). `B_L` is never populated, so `iĈ·B=0` holds by construction on every tick (F387 §3–4), not by cancellation. `fermion_em` per tick: `f,g = u1_link_weyl_step_3d_bcc(f, g, A, q)` (F385 fork a — the roadmap's own declared choice for the gate-level coupling; norm drift accepted, bounded, per F385's own adjudication).

**Scope limitation, disclosed not fixed here.** `E_L` is computed and exposed (`observables()`, state key `"E_L"`) but not fed back to the fermion — Stage 2's per-link step is a vector-potential-only (magnetic-type) mechanism; the electrostatic force is the roadmap's own §1.2-diagnosed job for the *site-local* wrap (`minimal_coupling.u1_wrap_weyl_step_3d_bcc`) via a time-varying angle, not this stage's per-link construction. Wiring it in (e.g. via the "Bloch acceleration" mechanism F385 §5b already found exact for a uniform gradient) is future work. **`E_L` is not exactly "the Coulomb field the fermion's own charge sources," corrected in review**: `ρ` is initialized to zero at `t=0` and accumulates only the `g_lat`-scaled *change* in charge density since then (`ρ_tracked(t) ≈ g_lat·(ρ_true(t)−ρ_true(0))`, verified directly), never the fermion's actual static self-charge — so `E_L` is the field sourced by a damped redistribution relative to an arbitrary baseline, not the full instantaneous Coulomb field. This does not affect any gated leg (no leg reads `E_L`/`ρ`), but the earlier, looser phrasing overclaimed what the state key represents.

**The topology fix.** `core.channels.PhotonPairChannel.topologies` is now `("cubic", "bcc")` — the same fix `GravityDielectricChannel` already carries for the identical reason ("the field this channel evolves is topology-agnostic"). `Ω_pair(k)` was already built from the BCC dispersion symbol (roadmap §1.4's own observation); the `"cubic"` tag described the array, not the hop rule. This is backward compatible (every existing `"cubic"`-tagged scenario is untouched) and lets the existing, standalone `photon_pair` channel — not just this stage's new `em_photon` — share a lattice with BCC fermion channels for the first time.

## 2. The structural limitation this stage surfaces: this current cannot radiate, and total momentum is not conserved

This is the load-bearing result of the finding, found by the independent review (both the blind subagent and, more sharply, the referee) rather than anticipated going in — disclosed prominently rather than buried in caveats.

**The current is longitudinal by construction, not merely in practice.** F384's own docstring states its derivation outright: `conserved_current` returns "the unique **longitudinal** solution... the current entirely along `Ĉ(k)`, with no transverse... freedom added." Measured directly across five fermion configurations (widths 1–4, various `k0`, centred and off-centre): `‖J_T‖/‖J‖` (the current's own `Ĉ(k)`-transverse fraction) is **`2.1×10⁻¹⁶` to `2.4×10⁻¹⁶`** in every case — floating-point noise, not a small physical effect. This is not a property of any particular fermion state; it is forced by F384's own minimal-solution construction (its own caveats already say the transverse part is "free, unconstrained gauge freedom" that construction declines to assign a value to, F384 §Caveats).

**Consequence for `em_photon`.** `E`'s only source term is `g_lat·J_T`, and `J_T` is machine-zero always — so **the fermion's current cannot make the photon channel's transverse (radiative) sector nonzero, ever, regardless of coupling strength or how long the loop runs.** Verified directly on the real channels, current-only scenario, 20 ticks: `‖E‖` and `‖B‖` both stay at `1.0–1.6×10⁻¹⁷` (not merely "small" — literally the noise floor) for the entire run, while `‖E_L‖` (the longitudinal/Coulomb sector) climbs steadily to `0.16`. The `self_sourced_field_momentum_trivially_zero` leg (§3) is therefore not testing "a vector crossed with itself vanishes" as a geometric curiosity on a nontrivial field — it is testing a field that is **identically, exactly zero**, for the more fundamental reason given here, and the leg's description below is corrected accordingly.

**Consequence for momentum conservation — a direct threat to Stage 5's first planned claim.** Stage 5 of the roadmap plans, as claim 1, `ΔP_matter + ΔP_field ≈ 0`. Measured on the real seeded-photon scenario (`L=16, g_lat=0.5, q=1, photon_amp=0.15`), tracking both legs of `core.observers.Momentum` tick by tick: `ΔP_field` stays at `~10⁻²⁰`–`10⁻²¹` (machine noise around exactly zero) for the full run, while `ΔP_matter` grows to `~1.9×10⁻⁵` by tick 20 and further (see §4's 100-tick extension). **Total momentum is not conserved by this loop, at all, to any precision tested.** The field pushes the fermion (through `A`, which does carry genuine transverse content when seeded) but the fermion never pushes back on the field, because the only channel available for that — the current's transverse part — is structurally zero. This is not a bug in `EmPhotonChannel`'s implementation; it is a direct, load-bearing consequence of F384's own current definition, now made concrete for the first time in a live coupled loop. **Whoever picks up Stage 5 needs to know this before attempting claim 1**, either by deriving a genuine transverse (radiative) current from the fermion's own acceleration/scattering (F384's declared-open gauge freedom would need to be *fixed*, not left free, for this), or by scoping Stage 5's momentum claim to acknowledge that this specific loop only demonstrates field→matter momentum transfer, not a closed two-way exchange.

**What this does *not* undermine.** F385's per-link step (the field→fermion direction) is unaffected — it is exactly what §4's seeded-photon push demonstrates working. F386's solve decision is unaffected — `solve_A_coulomb_3d` correctly returns exactly zero `A` when there is genuinely no transverse field to represent (§3), which is itself a correct, desired behavior, not a symptom of the limitation. F387's split-sourcing fix is unaffected in its own right — it correctly keeps `iĈ·B=0`; §3 below narrows what its dynamical-current test actually stress-tests, but does not call its correctness into question.

## 4. What "sustained push, within the tested window," means here — and what a longer run shows

F385 §5 measured a genuinely **unboundedly-growing** secular push for its own isolated-fork test, against a *static* test field held fixed tick after tick. That is not what this closed loop shows, and describing it as literal, indefinite secularity would overclaim. Measured directly (`L=16`, `g_lat=0.5`, `q=1`, `photon_amp=0.15`) over the gate's own 20-tick window: `‖Δp‖` rises quickly over the first ~4 ticks (`6.9×10⁻⁶ → 2.8×10⁻⁵`) then holds in a narrow band (`2.6–3.1×10⁻⁵`) through tick 20, never reverting toward zero or oscillating with a visible period — the gate leg's claim, as stated and as gated, is accurate for the window it checks.

**Extended to 100 ticks (found in review, not gated — the photon field itself keeps rotating at `Ω_pair`, so a longer run was always going to show more structure):** `‖Δp‖` keeps rising past tick 20, peaks at `~5.6×10⁻⁵` around tick 54, then **declines back to `~2.7×10⁻⁵` by tick 100** — almost exactly its own tick-20 value. This is a slow envelope oscillation (period set by the seeded mode's own `Ω_pair`), not a stable, indefinitely-sustained plateau, and not F385's own unbounded secular growth either. **The "sustained push" language is accurate and gated only for the tested 20-tick window; it should not be read as a claim that the push holds at this level, or continues to grow, indefinitely.** A future session extending this scenario to longer runs or to Stage 5's own physics claims should use an envelope-aware measure (e.g. peak `‖Δp‖` over a full rotation period, or the time-averaged value) rather than a single endpoint comparison.

## 6. Caveats

- **The momentum-push magnitude (`~3×10⁻⁵`) is small relative to the coupling strengths tested (`g_lat=0.5`, `q=1`, `photon_amp=0.15`).** This is a property of this specific, modestly-parameterized configuration (chosen for numerical stability and a clean gate margin, not to maximize signal), not a statement about the coupling's maximum reach — Stage 5's scenario work should feel free to explore stronger regimes; F385's own isolated-fork tests already demonstrate momentum transfers of order `1–10` at larger field amplitudes.
- **`seeded_photon_gives_sustained_push`'s seed pulse is explicitly projected onto the `Ĉ(k)`-transverse subspace before injection** (`em_photon_sourcing.split_transverse_longitudinal`), because `photon.build_pair_mode` (like `build_beam_packet`, F386 §6) polarizes transverse to the Euclidean `k̂`, not `Ĉ(k)`, and would otherwise seed the test with the same disclosed longitudinal contamination F386/F387 already characterized — conflating a test-helper artifact with the sourcing recipe's own behavior. This is a test-construction fix, not a change to the engine; a future session building a genuinely `Ĉ(k)`-transverse beam helper (F386 §6's own open item) would let this projection step be dropped.
- **`E_L` is computed but not coupled back to the fermion this stage, and is a `g_lat`-scaled zero-baseline redistribution tracker, not the full instantaneous Coulomb field** — see §1's corrected description. Stage 5's scenario work should not assume the fermion feels the charge's own electrostatic self-field; it currently does not, by design, not by oversight, and should not read `E_L`'s norm as "the charge's Coulomb field" without accounting for the baseline/scaling caveat.
- **A `g_lat` coupling-constant scaling inconsistency was caught in review and fixed**: an earlier version scaled `E_T`'s current source by `g_lat` but left `ρ`'s continuity update unscaled, sourcing the longitudinal (Coulomb) and transverse (radiative) sectors at inconsistent effective strengths. Fixed by scaling `ρ`'s continuity update by the same `g_lat` (Gauss's law is linear, so this is equivalent to scaling `E_L` after solving it — a self-consistent choice). **F387's own harness (`em_photon_sourcing.split_sourced_scenario`) still uses the old, unscaled convention** — not fixed here (out of this session's scope, since that module belongs to a concurrent session's own claim), so F387's and F388's reported `E_L`/`ρ`-adjacent numbers are not directly comparable; disclosed rather than silently left inconsistent.
- **A pre-existing exchange-bus blind spot, inherited not introduced here, demonstrated live in review.** `EmPhotonChannel`/`FermionEmChannel` (like the pre-existing `WSourcedChannel`/`FermionDoubletChannel`) name their partner via non-standard config keys (`fermion=`, `photon=`) that `Channel.consumes()`'s recognized key set does not include, so the typed exchange bus (P3.3) reports **zero dependency edges** for either channel and therefore **cannot detect a wrong registration order at all**. The referee built both orderings (`[em_photon, fermion_em]` and `[fermion_em, em_photon]`) and found `graph.order_violations` empty in both cases, while the fermion's actual state at tick 5 differs by `4.6×10⁻⁶` between the two orderings on a seeded-photon run — a real, silent, numerically consequential misconfiguration risk with no error raised. Not fixed here (it predates this stage and also affects the W-loop channels); flagged for a future session to either register `fermion=`/`photon=`/`w_field=` in `Channel.consumes()`'s recognized keys or have all four of these channels override `consumes()`/`provides()` explicitly.
- **The topology fix (`PhotonPairChannel.topologies=("cubic","bcc")`) does not itself update `scenarios/photon_beam_all_fields.yaml`**, the scenario the roadmap's own §1.4 diagnosis named as forced into a companion-scenario split by the tag mismatch. Merging that scenario (or leaving the split as a deliberate choice) is Stage 5/scenario-authoring work, not attempted here — the fix supplies the *capability*, not a rewritten scenario file.
- **`norm_drift_bounded`'s `0.5` threshold is generous, not tight** — chosen to catch outright instability (a diverging or NaN-producing loop) rather than to bound F385's own already-quantified `O(|qA|·a)` drift precisely; the measured value (`8.6×10⁻⁶`) is far below it. A tighter, quantitative drift-vs-coupling-strength check is not attempted here (F385 already owns that derivation for the isolated step).
- The `exactness="machine"` tag covers `ordering_pretick_posttick_exact`, `self_sourced_field_momentum_trivially_zero`, and `no_monopole_fix_holds_with_dynamical_current` (all `≤4×10⁻¹⁶`, aside from the intentionally-zero-by-construction `pfield_norm` at `3.9×10⁻³⁶`); `seeded_photon_gives_sustained_push` and `norm_drift_bounded` are threshold/quantitative checks, the same scoping distinction the F384/F385/F386 reviews already established and disclose here proactively.
- Only the Weyl 2-spinor / U(1) sector is covered, matching F384–F387's own scope.
