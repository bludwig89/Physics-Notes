## 1. The question (ledger D4, first suggested check)

F236/F254 close the see-saw *shape*: the $E_g/Z_3$ texture and the F201 cancellation node fix the three light active masses, their hierarchy, and prove the PMNS angles genuinely free. What remains completely open is the *overall* scale of the Majorana matrix, $M_{R0}$ — "nothing in the model fixes $M_R$." D4 names the cheapest attack: check whether either of the model's two existing scale-bearing structures already in play for the same sector — the F183/F107 canonical lattice cutoff, or the $E_g$ condensate itself (already used for the texture) — offers **any** link, even a rough one, before accepting $M_R$ as an irreducibly free second anchor alongside $v$.

This finding runs that check on both candidates explicitly, and adds a third argument the ledger row did not anticipate: that $\nu_R$'s own gauge content forecloses every dynamical scale-generating mechanism the model has elsewhere.

## 2. Leg 1 — the F183/F107 cutoff: no link, and the gap is not a small one

The canonical cell fixes the lattice's one UV scale exactly (F107, F282): $\Lambda = 3^{-1/4}M_\text{Pl} = 9.277\times10^{18}$ GeV. F201's own worked benchmark, chosen to land the $\nu$MSM split, uses $M_{R0}\sim1$ GeV (heavy sterile) down to the keV DM eigenvalue via node proximity. Comparing directly (check **C1**):

$$\frac{\Lambda}{M_{R0}}\Big|_{M_{R0}=1\,\text{GeV}} = 9.28\times10^{18}\quad(\log_{10}=18.97,\ \approx19\text{ decades}).$$

An exhaustive scan (check **C2**) of small integer powers ($\le12$) of every dimensionless ratio already registered in `casim.constants` — $\delta^*=2/9$ (the $E_g$ weight), $1/(72\pi)$ (`G_LATTICE`), $\sqrt2$ (the Fock amplitude), $6\lambda_6=1.46$ (the sextic brake $W$), $8\pi\sqrt3$ (the $a/\ell_P$ prefactor), $1/(a/\ell_P)$ — against the required suppression $M_{R0}/\Lambda\approx10^{-18.97}$ finds a closest approach of $\bigl(1/(72\pi)\bigr)^{8}=10^{-18.836}$, **0.132 dex away (a factor of $1.35$)**. This is flagged, explicitly, as an **unclaimed numerical coincidence** in the project's own established sense (cf. F332's `reciprocal_cube_coincidence`) and *not* adopted as a link: across the $6\times12=72$ (ratio, power) combinations scanned, landing within a few tenths of a dex of a fixed $\sim19$-dex target by chance is not statistically surprising, and no independent reason is offered — or known — for why the eighth power of the lattice's structural gravitational coupling should set a right-handed-neutrino mass scale. A genuine structural link needs a motivated exponent, not the best of 72 scans; none is available. This is the same shape of gap the ledger already carries open for $v/\Lambda$ (E7: $v$ "is an anchor, not an output") and for $m_{E_g}/\Lambda$ (E5: "undetermined, but no longer unconstrained" — only a *lower bound* from BBN, not a value). $M_R$ adds a fourth instance of the identical unresolved problem, not a new one.

**The disanalogy with $G$, made explicit (check C3).** F79 derives $G=a^2c^3/(8\pi\sqrt3\,\hbar)$ from the cutoff with *zero* hierarchy: $G$ is not a second scale relative to $a$, it is the same scale $a$ re-expressed in SI units via an exact, parameter-free dimensionless prefactor — F79 §6 states this plainly ("the model predicts the dimensionless number $a/\ell_P$, not a dimensionful $G$ from pure numbers"). $M_R$ is the opposite kind of object: it is a *second, independent* scale roughly $10^{-19}\Lambda$, which is precisely the class of problem ($v/\Lambda$, quark masses/$\Lambda$, $m_{E_g}/\Lambda$) the model has never yet solved for *any* fermion or condensate. Citing F79 as a precedent for "the cutoff fixes scales" would be a category error; F79 is the one case with no hierarchy to explain.

## 3. Leg 2 — the $E_g$ condensate: excluded by generalizing F253's POSIT-N no-go, and confirmed directly from the module

Two independent arguments, one representation-theoretic and one a direct code check.

**(a) F253 generalizes.** F253 proved, for the charged-lepton angle $\delta^*=2/9$ rad, that turning an $O_h$ representation *weight* (a dimensionless multiplicity, $\dim E_g/\dim(T_{1u}\otimes T_{1u})$) into a physical *radian* requires one additional, non-derivable normalization — POSIT-N, the $E_g$ generator norm $R=1$ — and that the one route that could have supplied it scale-free (a topological holonomy) is excluded outright: the BCC second shell's only genuine $E_g$ holonomy is $2\pi/3$, and $2/9$ enters only as a multiplicity. Nothing in that argument is lepton-specific: it is a statement about what $O_h$ representation theory on the BCC second shell can and cannot supply — a dimensionless ratio or a $2\pi/3$-quantized angle, never an independent absolute scale. $M_{R0}$ is not even an angle; it is a **mass**, one full dimensional category further from anything the $E_g$ machinery produces than the radian F253 already excluded. Check **C4** confirms symbolically (`sympy`, exact) that the texture's own defining relation,
$$\sqrt{M_a}=M_{R0}\bigl[\,1+\sqrt2\cos(\delta_\nu+\tfrac{2\pi a}{3})\,\bigr],$$
factors $M_{R0}$ out of the bracket **identically** — $M_{R0}$ is algebraically a free overall multiplier the $Z_3$ construction never touches, for any $\delta_\nu$.

**(b) Direct confirmation from `ca_majorana.py`/F201.** Reading the module (`z3_sqrt_texture`, `src/casim/engine/particles/majorana.py` — see also F201 §"What is derived vs computed vs accommodated," which already lists "overall scale $M_{R0}\sim$ GeV" as **accommodated**, not derived) shows the $Z_3$ node mechanism (F201) only ever acts on the *ratio* $M_\text{min}/M_\text{max}$ via the angle $\delta_\nu$'s proximity to $135°$ — it rescales relative eigenvalues, never the prefactor multiplying all three. Check **C5** verifies numerically that rescaling $M_{R0}\to\lambda M_{R0}$ at fixed $\delta_\nu$ rescales every eigenvalue by exactly $\lambda^2$ and leaves every ratio, every mixing angle, and the node location untouched — i.e., the condensate mechanism is provably blind to $M_{R0}$, not merely silent about it.

## 4. Leg 3 — the argument the ledger did not name: $\nu_R$'s singlet status forecloses every dynamical scale-generation route the model has

The model has exactly one mechanism anywhere in its tree for turning the Planck-scale cutoff into a hierarchically *small* physical scale without fine-tuning: asymptotic-freedom running of a **non-abelian, confining** gauge coupling, which is how $\Lambda_\text{QCD}$ (and the constituent scale, F123) arise from $\Lambda$. `src/casim/engine/interactions/running_*.py` (`running_alpha_s`, `running_scale_ratio`, `running_qstar_logmoment`, …) implement exactly this for $SU(3)_c$; there is no analogous module for any other sector, because there is no other confining gauge group in the model. $SU(2)_L$ is Higgs-free and non-confining at these scales; $U(1)_Y$ is abelian and IR-**free** (its coupling shrinks, not grows, toward the IR — it cannot dynamically generate a confinement-like scale via dimensional transmutation, structurally, regardless of any running computation one might attempt).

$\nu_R$ is a **total gauge singlet** — no colour, no $SU(2)_L$, $Y=0$ (F47, reaffirmed as structurally forced by F341's hypercharge-closure argument). It therefore couples to **none** of the model's running or confining sectors at all: there is no gauge coupling attached to $\nu_R$ whose running could generate an intermediate scale the way QCD generates $\Lambda_\text{QCD}$. Check **C6** confirms this mechanically, not by argument: `inspect.getsource` on `casim.engine.particles.majorana` shows its only import beyond the standard library is `numpy` — zero references to any gauge, running, or confinement module anywhere in the file. A control check on the one place `y_nu` *is* mentioned elsewhere in the gauge tree (`casim.engine.gauge.derive_ncolour`, the hypercharge anomaly-cancellation system of F165/F279/F341) confirms that mention sits inside the anomaly/mass-step linear system — a different physics question (fixing $N_c$/hypercharge ratios) with no dynamical bearing on a mass scale — not a running or confinement coupling.

This produces a genuine structural tension worth stating plainly: **the same total-singlet property that makes the Majorana term gauge-invariant and therefore possible at all (F47, F341) is exactly the property that removes every dynamical mechanism the model has elsewhere for setting a fermion's mass scale relative to the cutoff.** A charged fermion's mass could in principle inherit a scale from $SU(3)_c$/$SU(2)_L$ dynamics (confinement, or — if the model ever derives one — an EW condensate scale); $\nu_R$, being coupled to nothing, structurally cannot. This is not a gap in the search; it is a consequence of $\nu_R$'s already-established, load-bearing gauge content.

## 5. External context (not model-internal, cited for scope)

The $\nu$MSM literature this model's F201/F202/F266 already adopts does not itself derive $M_R$ from any UV completion; the sub-GeV/GeV window is chosen for phenomenological viability (BBN, X-ray, seesaw, and — as of 2021 — collider search constraints on heavy neutral leptons below the kaon mass), not derived from a deeper principle. See Béthencourt González & Shaposhnikov-type window analyses ([An allowed window for heavy neutral leptons below the kaon mass, JHEP07(2021)193](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/JHEP07(2021)193)) and the general seesaw/GUT context ([PDG 2025 Grand Unified Theories review](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-guts.pdf); [Neutrino Mass and Grand Unification](https://ar5iv.arxiv.org/html/hep-ph/0412050)). A search for a mechanism-specific *lower bound* on $M_R$ (wash-in leptogenesis, [arXiv:2501.07634](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.07634)) returned a TeV-scale bound under a chargegenesis-specific mechanism this model does not build (an unspecified high-temperature electron-asymmetry source) — not applicable to the ARS/$\nu$MSM oscillation leptogenesis this model's F202 already uses, and not cited as a constraint here. So: the model's inability to fix $M_R$ from first principles mirrors a known, stated open problem in the literature it is built on, rather than being a defect unique to this project.

## 7. Verdict and what promotes the ledger

D4 does **not** close. What moves: the row's own suggested first check is now answered, explicitly, for both named candidates — with reasons, not silence — and a third, previously unnamed structural argument ($\nu_R$'s singlet status foreclosing the model's only scale-generating mechanism) is added. $M_R$ is reclassified from an isolated, unexamined residual into the **fifth member of an existing, named cluster** (alongside $v$/E7, quark masses/E6, $m_{E_g}$/E5, and now $M_R$/D4) of "the model derives shapes exactly and has, so far, no mechanism for any absolute mass scale relative to $\Lambda$." That reclassification is itself the promotion: any future session that finds a scale-generation mechanism for *one* member of this cluster should check it against all five before claiming a $M_R$-specific result, and any claim to derive $M_R$ specifically (rather than solving the cluster) should be treated with the same suspicion F253's POSIT-N localizes for the lepton angle.

## 8. What would change this

1. **A genuinely new gauge coupling for $\nu_R$.** If a future finding attaches $\nu_R$ to *any* confining or asymptotically-non-trivial sector (e.g., a gauged $B\!-\!L$ or a composite/technicolor-style completion), leg 3 is void and the RG-running route (leg 1's missing mechanism) becomes available — this would be new physics beyond the model's current construction, not a re-reading of existing structure.
2. **Solving the E5–E7 cluster generally.** Any future derivation of $v$, a quark mass, or $m_{E_g}$ from $\Lambda$ via a lattice-fixed dimensionless factor should be re-run against $M_R$ immediately — leg 1's "no small enough ratio exists" is an empirical statement about currently-known lattice constants, not a theorem, and a genuinely new $O(1)$-exponentiated ratio could revise it.
3. **A structural argument that $M_D$'s texture-sharing (F236) extends to $M_R$'s prefactor**, not just its texture — not attempted here; F236 argues $M_D$ and $M_R$ share the *same texture object* on the same shell, but never claims they share an overall normalization, and this finding did not test whether such a claim is even well-posed.
