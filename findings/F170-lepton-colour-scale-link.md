## 1. The gap this closes

F144 derived the overall fermion-mass scale to a factor ~1.9 with no tuning, but by **identifying** the colourless lepton scale $N$ with the colour transmutation scale $\Lambda_\text{QCD}/\mu_0$. The roadmap (2026-06-29) flagged the one genuine physics question this leaves:

> **Leptons carry no colour, and the lepton mass shape comes from the $E_g$ condensate (F93), not the gluon sector. So *why* does the lepton / $E_g$-condensate scale track $\Lambda_\text{QCD}$ to a factor ~2?** — currently an identification, not a derivation (roadmap §3, R-link).

The external technicolor/ETC literature says the answer must live in *how the colour scale is fed into the colourless order parameter* and in the condensate's anomalous dimension. This finding supplies that mechanism inside the model.

## 2. The derivation

### 2.1 The $E_g$ condensate has no coupling of its own (F145/F150)

The $E_g$ generation order parameter is a fermion bilinear condensate on the BCC second-neighbour shell (F93). Its dynamics is NJL-type, governed by a contact coupling — but that coupling is **not fundamental**. F145 proved the model's contact self-couplings are **induced**: integrate out the heavy dual-Meissner/dielectric gluon (mass $m_D$), Fierz-project, and read off

$$G=\frac{2}{9}\,\frac{g^2}{2 m_D^2}=\frac{g^2}{9\,m_D^2},\qquad c=\tfrac29\ \text{(channel-independent, $8\times10^{-17}$)}.$$

F150 showed the $E_g$ condensate's *higher* self-couplings (the cubic $B$ that F95 derived, the sextic $C=\lambda_6 e^6$) are generated by the **same** integrating-out, one and two orders higher. **So the entire $E_g$ Landau potential is sourced by the strong sector at the scale $m_D\sim\Lambda_\text{QCD}$. There is no independent lepton-sector coupling, hence no independent lepton-sector scale.**

### 2.2 Nothing condenses at the bare coupling; the running switches it on (F145)

At the bare rule coupling $g_s^2=\tfrac14$, *every* channel is subcritical ($R\approx0.10\ll1$, F145 N3). $\chi$SB is switched on only by the running (F145 N4), crossing $\alpha_\text{crit}\approx0.20$ on the way to the IR. The condensate scale is therefore a transmutation scale — the same $\Lambda_\text{QCD}$ ladder.

### 2.3 The lepton channel is near-degenerate with the colour channel (NEW, A1)

Could the $E_g$ (lepton) channel sit at a wildly different rung of that ladder? No. Compare the gap-kernel integrals $I_X=\langle|F_X(\mathbf k)|^2/\sqrt{K(\mathbf k)}\rangle_\text{BZ}$ for the s-wave colour $\chi$SB channel ($F=1$) and the $E_g$ second-shell doublet ($F=\{\cos k_x-\cos k_y,\ (2\cos k_z-\cos k_x-\cos k_y)/\sqrt3\}$, each normalised to $\langle F^2\rangle=1$), with the Wilson kernel $K=\sum_i 2(1-\cos k_i)$:

$$\frac{G_c^{E_g}}{G_c^{\text{s-wave}}}=\frac{I_{\text{s-wave}}}{I_{E_g}}\ \xrightarrow{L\to\infty}\ 1.08\quad(L=24\!:\!1.243\to L=64\!:\!1.083,\ \text{converging}).$$

So $\alpha_\text{crit}^{E_g}\approx0.217$ vs $\alpha_\text{crit}^{\chi\text{SB}}\approx0.200$: the two channels reach criticality at **essentially the same coupling**, i.e. within a scale factor $\sim1.3$ on the running ladder. **There is no kinematic mechanism that separates the lepton and colour condensation scales by more than $O(1)$.**

### 2.4 The link (A2)

Shared induced coupling (§2.1: no independent lepton scale) $+$ near-degenerate criticality (§2.3: no large kinematic separation) $\Rightarrow$

$$\boxed{\;v_{E_g}=O(1)\times\Lambda_\text{QCD}\;}$$

The colourless lepton condensate inherits the colour scale because it possesses **neither a coupling nor a kinematics of its own that could produce a different scale.** This is the model's concrete realisation of the extended-technicolor "feeding": the extended interaction is the induced coupling (integrating out the dielectric gluon), and the link rides — exactly as the TC literature predicts — on the $E_g$ condensate's induced (anomalous-dimension-carrying) self-interaction.
