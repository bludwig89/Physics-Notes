## 1. Why this is not a re-import of an external unitarity-violation coefficient

F357 closed E12's *kinematic* half — a single photon/graviton line cannot carry more than $E_\text{max}=0.8247\,E_\text{Planck}$ — and stated plainly what it left open (Sec.7): a graviton-graviton scattering amplitude, interaction vertex, or partial-wave unitarity bound. The standard continuum EFT-of-gravity literature (Donoghue's own paper, already cited by F357) states that graviton-graviton tree amplitudes grow with energy and formally exceed $|a_J|\le1$ near the Planck scale — but the exact $O(1)$ numerical coefficient of *where* that happens is process-, helicity-, and Planck-mass-convention-dependent across the literature. Grafting one such external coefficient onto this model, dressed as a "prediction," without re-deriving the model's own cubic graviton self-interaction vertex from the induced Einstein–Hilbert action, would be exactly the kind of import-without-derivation this project's exactness discipline exists to prevent.

This finding computes something different, and needs no imported coefficient at all: a plain **relativistic CM-energy threshold comparison** between two numbers this repository has *already* derived, published, and reviewed — F357's $E_\text{max}$ and F228's $M_\text{rem}$ — both of which trace back to the *same* registered structural ruler $a_\text{over\_ellP}$ (F79/F107). The comparison costs zero new free parameters.

## 2. The re-derivation: F228's remnant mass from the same ruler as F357's ceiling (checks A, B)

Write $A\equiv a_\text{over\_ellP}=\sqrt{8\pi}\,3^{1/4}$ (registered, F79/F107). F79/F107's own canonical-cell definition gives the exact identity

$$A^2 = 8\pi\sqrt3 \qquad\text{(check A, sympy, symbolic residual }=0\text{; numeric residual }7\times10^{-15}\text{ against the live registry value).}$$

F228 derives its one-cell remnant by tiling a Schwarzschild horizon of area $16\pi(M/M_\text{Pl})^2\,\ell_P^2$ with F107 cells of area $a^2=A^2\ell_P^2=8\pi\sqrt3\,\ell_P^2$, giving cell count $N(M)=16\pi(M/M_\text{Pl})^2/A^2$, and setting $N=1$ (the horizon cannot tile fewer than one cell — F190's $S=A/4$ area law) fixes

$$\frac{M_\text{rem}}{M_\text{Pl}}=\frac{A}{4\sqrt\pi}.$$

Check B verifies this **symbolically equals** F228's own published closed form $3^{1/4}/\sqrt2$ (sympy residual $=0$) and **numerically matches** F228's published $0.93060$ to $<5\times10^{-5}$. This is not a new derivation of $M_\text{rem}$ — it is the *same* F228 result, rewritten in terms of the *same* ruler $A$ that F357's $E_\text{max}$ already uses, which is what makes the comparison in §3 exact and parameter-free rather than a coincidence between two independently-normalised numbers.

## 3. The headline result (checks C, D)

F357 gives $E_\text{max}/E_\text{Planck}=\pi\sqrt3/A$. Two band-top quanta colliding head-on carry CM energy $\sqrt{s_\text{max}}=2E_\text{max}$ (standard massless two-body kinematics: $s=4E_1E_2$ at $\theta=\pi$, maximal for equal energies). Then

$$\frac{\sqrt{s_\text{max}}}{M_\text{rem}c^2}=\frac{2\pi\sqrt3/A}{A/(4\sqrt\pi)}=\frac{8\pi^{3/2}\sqrt3}{A^2}=\frac{8\pi^{3/2}\sqrt3}{8\pi\sqrt3}=\boxed{\sqrt\pi}\approx1.77245,$$

with $A$ cancelling **completely** — sympy confirms this by direct symbolic simplification (check C), not by substituting a decimal. A single band-top quantum alone falls short of the same threshold:

$$\frac{E_\text{max}}{M_\text{rem}c^2}=\frac{\pi\sqrt3/A}{A/(4\sqrt\pi)}=\frac{4\pi^{3/2}\sqrt3}{A^2}=\frac{\sqrt\pi}{2}\approx0.88623\qquad\text{(check D, exact-algebraic).}$$

**A head-on collision of the model's own two most energetic possible photons or gravitons supplies exactly $\sqrt\pi\approx1.77$ times the rest-mass energy of the model's own minimal, exact, stable black hole — comfortably super-threshold, in closed form, with zero new free parameters. Individually, one such quantum falls short by the same exact factor of two ($\sqrt\pi/2\approx0.89$ vs.\ the pair's $\sqrt\pi\approx1.77$).**

## 4. Physical reading, honestly scoped

Ordinary relativistic two-body kinematics: for two colliding particles, $\sqrt s$ is the CM energy available to produce a state at rest in that frame, and $\sqrt s\ge$(target rest-mass energy) is the ordinary threshold condition — zero leftover kinetic energy exactly at threshold, room for outgoing recoil above it. Checks C/D show the model's own kinematic ceiling *exceeds* its own black-hole-formation threshold, by an exact, closed-form margin.

**What this is not, stated precisely (correcting an earlier draft's overclaim).** Dvali & Gomez's "self-completeness of Einstein gravity" (arXiv:1005.3497) and 't Hooft's trans-Planckian-collision argument (1987) state a **geometric** criterion for black-hole formation — a hoop/impact-parameter condition relating the collision's impact parameter to its Schwarzschild radius, not a bare energy-vs-rest-mass comparison. This finding does **not** compute that geometric criterion (no impact parameter, cross-section, or hoop-conjecture bound appears anywhere in this module). What checks C/D establish is a **necessary, weaker, purely energetic precondition** for that geometric criterion to have any chance of being met at all: a head-on collision cannot begin to approach the hoop condition unless it first clears the ordinary CM-energy-vs-rest-mass threshold, and this model's own kinematic ceiling clears that weaker threshold by an exact factor of $\sqrt\pi$. Establishing the necessary precondition is not the same as establishing the (stronger, geometric) sufficient criterion the classicalization literature actually uses — that calculation remains genuinely open (§7).

**Why $M_\text{rem}$, not bare $M_\text{Pl}$, is the comparison target.** $M_\text{rem}$ is not chosen for the cleanliness of the resulting closed form: it is the model's own *concrete, minimum, stable* black-hole object (F228) — the smallest black hole the model can actually produce — so it is the operationally correct threshold for "can this collision produce a black hole *in this model*," unlike bare $M_\text{Pl}$, which names a unit, not a model-native object. For robustness: the qualitative conclusion (pair super-threshold, single quantum sub-threshold) is unchanged under the naive substitution $M_\text{rem}\to M_\text{Pl}$ ($\sqrt{s_\text{max}}/M_\text{Pl}c^2=2\sqrt{\pi\sqrt3/8}\approx1.6495>1$, still super-threshold) — it is only the *exact closed form* $\sqrt\pi$ that is specific to $M_\text{rem}$.

**Still open — F357 Sec.7's dynamical residual is unchanged by this finding.** No graviton-graviton scattering amplitude, interaction vertex, cross-section, or partial-wave unitarity bound is computed here — that is exactly the gap F357 Sec.7 named, and it remains exactly that size after this finding. No hoop-conjecture / impact-parameter calculation is attempted either (§4); the purely energetic threshold used here is deliberately weaker and needs none. A genuine black-hole-formation cross-section at the lattice scale, and any dynamical content beyond this kinematic threshold, remain fully open — this finding narrows *what kind of question* is left (a geometric/dynamical one, given the energetic precondition is now shown to hold), not the size of the open question itself. The $O(1)$ (rather than exactly $1$) coefficient $\sqrt\pi$ is a genuine, derived model output, not a target tuned toward any particular value.
