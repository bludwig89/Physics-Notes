## The question (audit G2)

The 2026-06-29 audit (§G2) charges that the F69 photon — a bound $(+,-)$ pair with $\Omega_\text{pair}(k)=\omega^+(k/2)+\omega^-(k/2)$ — is built **kinematically** (a sum of constituent rates) with no dynamical reason for *"a bound state at zero binding energy."* F69 §"Open" itself flags two-body binding dynamics as unfinished; [F74](F74-two-constituent-bound-state-binding.md) then built the dynamics for the **spin-0 sibling** and found its mass needs a tuned super-critical contact coupling the model does not supply.

This finding asks the photon-specific question and answers it: **the photon's zero binding energy is not a tuned or assumed number — it is forced by the structure of the walk and by the gauge (U(1) identity) character of the channel.** The F69 "kinematic sum" is therefore the *necessary* form, the symmetry-protected threshold value, not a modelling choice.

## 1. Why the constituents are massless — the pure-hop walk (B1)

The single-tick BCC unitary is a *pure body-diagonal hop* with **no on-site term**, $A_0=0$ (Paper 2; [F51](F51-bipartite-sublattice-hypercharge.md) §2). At $k=0$ the hop amplitudes give

$$u^{\pm}(0)=c_xc_yc_z\pm s_xs_ys_z\big|_{k=0}=1\cdot1\cdot1\pm0=1\quad\Rightarrow\quad \omega^{\pm}(0)=\arccos 1=0.$$

A massless constituent is not an input: it is the absence of a self-loop. If an on-site amplitude $\varepsilon$ were present, $u(0)=1-\varepsilon$ and a gap $\omega(0)=\arccos(1-\varepsilon)\approx\sqrt{2\varepsilon}>0$ would open. So $\omega^{\pm}(0)=0$ is tied structurally to $A_0=0$ (B1: $u^{\pm}(0)=1$, $\omega^{\pm}(0)=0$ exactly). The pair inherits it: $\Omega_\text{pair}(0)=\omega^+(0)+\omega^-(0)=0$. The "zero binding energy" is therefore not a finite cancellation $M=(m_1+m_2)-E_b$ with $M$ small — it is $m_1=m_2=0$, hence $M_\text{pair}=0$ identically.

## 2. What binds the symmetric pair — the vector (identity) channel (B2)

The chirality relation is **algebraically exact** (B2: residual literally $0$ over 5000 random $k$, because $u^-(k)=u^+(-k)$ by the cosine-even/sine-odd structure):

$$\omega^-(k)=\omega^+(-k).$$

Consequently the symmetric split — one constituent on $+$, one on $-$, each at $k/2$ — is exactly the **parity-even** combination $\Omega_\text{even}=\omega^+(k/2)+\omega^-(k/2)$, the U(1) **identity channel** that minimal coupling forces ([F68](F68-minimal-coupling-forces-even-photon.md)). The alternative — a single-branch photon — is the *chiral*, birefringent object excluded by GRB/AGN polarimetry ([F67](F67-even-law-photon-vs-bilinear-mutually-exclusive.md)) and would couple to an axial (chiral) current, not the electromagnetic vector current. So the binding agent is gauge invariance itself: the photon is the quantum of the conserved, parity-even U(1)$_\text{em}$ current, which is *by construction* the symmetric sum of the two chiral branches. The pair "only occurs as a pair" (F69) because a single branch is not a vector-current excitation.

## 3. Why E_b = 0 is protected, not tuned — the gauge contrast with the scalar (B4)

A rest mass in this model is generated by the [F27](F27-complex-mass-chiral-su2.md) step that **couples the two chiral branches** $\eta\leftrightarrow\chi$. At $k=0$ the kinetic step is the identity, so the full step is the mass vertex

$$V(m)=\cos m\,\mathbb{I}+\sin m\,X,\qquad X=\begin{pmatrix}0&i\\ i&0\end{pmatrix}\ (\eta\!\leftrightarrow\!\chi),\qquad \text{gap }\Omega(0)=m$$

(B4: eigenphase $=m$ to $4\times10^{-17}$ for $m=0,0.1,0.3,0.7$). The mass lives **entirely in the branch-coupling component $X$.** The photon's coupling, by F68, is the branch-*diagonal* identity $e^{i\theta}\mathbb{I}$ — its $X$-component is identically zero — so it cannot generate the mass step and $\Omega(0)=0$ is **forced** (B4: identity-channel gap $=0$). 

This is the sharp dividing line the audit was missing:

| Channel | Vertex | Mass term ($X$-component) | Binding gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Photon ([F68](F68-minimal-coupling-forces-even-photon.md)/F69) | $e^{i\theta}\mathbb I$ (identity, vector) | forbidden ($\equiv0$) | $E_b=0$ **protected** |
| Scalar ([F73](F73-spin0-bound-pair-scalar.md)/[F74](F74-two-constituent-bound-state-binding.md)) | $\sigma$-bilinear (branch-coupling) | allowed | $E_b>0$, needs tuned $g>g_c$ |

The scalar sibling's binding gap is unprotected — F74 showed it requires a fine-tuned super-critical contact coupling (the hierarchy problem in the model's language). The photon's vanishing gap requires *nothing tuned*: it is the gauge (Ward/identity) statement that a vector-current quantum stays massless. In continuum terms this is Weinberg's massless-spin-1 theorem realised on the lattice — masslessness ↔ the 2-polarization transverse (PP5) gauge structure.

## 4. Massless dispersion, no mass term in the split (B3)

Along $(1,1,1)$, $\Omega_\text{pair}/|k|\to1/\sqrt3=0.577350$ (B3 slope $0.5773503$): luminal, gapless. The branch split $\Delta\Omega=2\omega^+(k/2)-2\omega^-(k/2)$ has **no constant ($k^0$, mass) or linear term** — it fits a power law with exponent $\approx2$ (B3: $2.0004$), i.e. it enters only as a phase-velocity birefringence $\Delta v\sim k$ (consistent with [F37](F37-rs-bcc-chirality-helicity.md)'s $\Delta v_\phi/c\approx-k/18$). The two constituents carry **no mass difference**, confirming there is no hidden gap for binding to cancel.

## 6. Verdict — what this closes of G2, and what it does not

**Closed.** The paired-photon binding is **not merely kinematic**. The zero binding energy is forced from three structural facts, each verified:

- the constituents are massless because the walk is a pure hop ($A_0=0$) — §1/B1;
- the binding agent is the conserved U(1)$_\text{em}$ **vector current**: the photon is exactly the parity-even $(+,-)$ combination, and a single-branch photon is the excluded chiral object — §2/B2;
- $E_b=0$ is **gauge-protected**: the mass term lives only in the branch-coupling vertex, which the U(1) identity channel does not contain — §3/B4. This is precisely the structural difference from the F74 scalar, whose unprotected gap needs fine-tuning.

So the F69 kinematic sum $\Omega_\text{pair}=\omega^+(k/2)+\omega^-(k/2)$ is the **necessary, symmetry-protected threshold form**, not an assumption.

**Still open (narrower than before).** This finding establishes *why the bound-state pole must sit at threshold*; it does **not** construct the full interacting two-body wavefunction from first-principles couplings. The relativistic Bethe–Salpeter/ladder build flagged in [F74](F74-two-constituent-bound-state-binding.md) §"Scope/next" — exhibiting the marginal pole dynamically with the F46 dispersion — remains the hard outstanding piece. G2 therefore moves from *"binding is kinematic, mechanism unknown"* to *"the zero binding energy is structure- and gauge-forced; only the interacting two-body wavefunction is left to build."*
