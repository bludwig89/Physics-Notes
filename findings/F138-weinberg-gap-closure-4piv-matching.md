## 2. The mechanism: why $1/4$, and why at a low (TeV) scale

F115 CM2 proved the gap is **not** desert running (a Planck-scale anchor overshoots to 0.06) and relocated it to "a few-TeV matching" — but left both the matching scale and its mechanism underived. Both follow from F41:

**Premise (F41).** Hypercharge is not an independent lattice gauge field. $U(1)_Y$ is absorbed as a Stueckelberg wrap on $U(x)$ — "pure gauge, not a boson" — so at the lattice level the abelian factor has **no kinetic term**, i.e. formally **infinite bare abelian coupling**. Its finite low-energy kinetic term must be *induced* (generated by matter loops / matching at the scale where the EW sector's composite structure resolves).

**Consequence (exact algebra, WM1).** In any embedding where the physical hypercharge is a mixture of the non-abelian Cartan direction (here the σ↔τ swap-singlet of F45, embedding ratio $g'^2_\text{embed}/g^2=1/3$) and an external abelian factor with coupling $g_X$, the 331-type relation holds:

$$\frac{g_X^2}{g_L^2}=\frac{\sin^2\theta_W}{1-4\sin^2\theta_W}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \sin^2\theta_W=\frac{t^2}{1+4t^2},\ t^2=\frac{g_X^2}{g_L^2},$$

so $\sin^2\theta_W<\tfrac14$ for all finite $g_X$ and $\sin^2\theta_W\to\tfrac14$ **exactly as $g_X\to\infty$**. The minimal 3-3-1 model exhibits precisely this: its abelian coupling hits a Landau pole where the running SM angle reaches $1/4$, at $\mu\approx3.5$–$4$ TeV (arXiv:hep-ph/0606173, arXiv:2302.08578, arXiv:2404.05821). In our model the "infinite abelian coupling" is not a pathology to be evaded — it is the *structural starting point* (no $Y$ kinetic term on the lattice). Hence:

> $\sin^2\theta_W=\tfrac14$ holds **exactly at the scale where the induced $Y$ kinetic term turns on**, and standard running applies below it. F45's swap value is the cap value of the embedding, reached because F41 deletes the independent abelian stiffness.

This also explains F115 CM2's negative: $1/4$ was never a Planck-scale boundary condition, because between the lattice scale and $\mu_\star$ the hypercharge field does not exist as an independent propagating degree of freedom — there is nothing to run.

**The scale.** The model's EWSB is Higgs-free (F27/F41/F118): the symmetry-breaking sector is composite/strongly-coupled, and the generic (NDA) scale at which such a sector resolves is $\Lambda=4\pi v$. Identifying $\mu_\star=4\pi v=3.094$ TeV requires no new parameter ($v$ is fixed by $G_F$, already an external ruler via the EW magnitude of F115 CM1).

$$\sin^2\theta_W^\text{os}\equiv1-\frac{m_W^2}{m_Z^2}=\frac29\;\Longleftrightarrow\;\frac{m_Z}{m_W}=\frac{3}{\sqrt7}=1.13389,$$

$$\frac{2/9}{1/4}=\frac89,$$

Under F138 the two findings stop competing: $1/4$ is the running angle at $\mu_\star$ (UV cap, derived), $2/9$ is numerically consistent with the chain's on-shell endpoint at $M_Z$ ($\lesssim1\%$).
