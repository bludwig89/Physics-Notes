## 1. The question, precisely

`docs/status/open-derivations.md` row L3 records a **closed** theorem (F232): no dimensionless, mass-blind lattice observable can pin the absolute cell size $a$; the one input that does is the dimensionful gravitational coupling $G$, via F79's structural relation $1/G = 2\pi\eta\, g_*\sqrt d\,\hbar/(a^2c^3)$. Row E7 records, as of today, that the electroweak scale $v$ is `FIT (N=1)` — "an anchor, not an output" — grouped with the charged-lepton scale as "the same blocker as $d_1$ once F233 is taken seriously." This session's brief asked directly: **is $v$ a second dimensionful pin analogous to $G$, or is it forced by that same $G$?** — a question F232's own text flags as not yet asked in those terms.

Both readings can be checked, and both close negative, but the second closes negative for a much more interesting reason than the first.

## 2. Reading 1 — "forced by the same $G$": mechanically false (T1)

F79's structural relation is one scalar equation in the unknowns $\{a, G\}$ (with $\eta=\tfrac1{12}$, $g_*=48$, $d=3$, $\hbar$, $c$ all fixed structural/defining inputs):

$$\frac1G = 2\pi\,\eta\,g_*\sqrt d\;\frac{\hbar}{a^2c^3}.$$

The symbol $v$ does not appear in it. This is not a physics argument, it is a **type check**, and it is worth actually running rather than asserting: T1 constructs this expression symbolically (sympy) and confirms its free-symbol set is exactly $\{a,\eta,g_*,d,\hbar,c\}$ — $v\notin$ that set, i.e. $\partial(1/G)/\partial v \equiv 0$ identically, for the entirely mundane reason that $G$'s one piece of dimensionful input was already fully spent determining $a$ (F232 T2: the identity $a/\ell_P=\sqrt{8\pi}3^{1/4}$ holds for *any* $G$, i.e. one equation, one unknown, exactly saturated). One dimensionful equation constrains one dimensionful degree of freedom; asking it to *also* pin a second, independent unknown ($v$) is asking one equation to fix two unknowns — exactly the same degeneracy shape F83/F232 diagnosed for $a$ itself before $G$ was brought in, run here in the opposite direction. **A second free parameter cannot be retired by re-using an equation that does not mention it.** This closes reading 1: no, $v$ is not and cannot be forced by $G$'s equation specifically. (This does not exclude some *other*, not-yet-written structural equation from involving both — see §6.)

## 3. A documentation hazard, flagged in passing (T2)

While assembling this, a real (if minor) collision surfaced. `findings/F118` and `findings/F234` derive a self-consistent triple **also called** $(W,v,c)$ — but that "$v$" is the coefficient of the per-axis quartic term $v\sum_a y_a^4$ in the second-shell charged-lepton free energy $E(y)$, a **dimensionless** $O(0.1$–$0.2)$ number (F118 §4: $v\approx0.16$), unrelated to ledger parameter #17. T2 recomputes both numbers side by side — $V_\text{EW}=(\sqrt2\,G_F)^{-1/2}=246.22$ GeV (`derive_gauge_boson_masses.V_EW`, reproduced exactly) against F118's $v\approx0.16$ — and confirms by grep that neither is a registered `Constant` (both are module-local literals; `constants/electroweak.py`'s own docstring already flags the analogous $\sin^2\theta_W$ dual-meaning hazard and recommends exactly this kind of explicit disambiguation for a symbol reused across sectors). No physics rides on this — the two "$v$"s are never combined in any existing finding — but a future session skimming cross-references between F118/F234 and the E7 row could conflate them, so it is worth one sentence in the tree rather than zero.

## 4. Reading 2 — "an independent scale gap needing its own $G$-like pin": also false, but because $v$ is not an independent gap at all

Here is the substantive result. A dimensionless (or, once $a$ is fixed, dimensionless-in-lattice-units) scale that is not fixed by geometry can in principle arise from exactly two mechanisms already used elsewhere in this ledger:

**(i) Sakharov/loop-induction** — integrating out a fluctuating sector induces a stiffness for a would-be free direction. This is *literally* how F79 got $1/G$ (integrating out the massless EM sector's own vacuum fluctuations). The electroweak analogue — does integrating out a sector induce the wrap/Goldstone stiffness $f_\theta^2\sim v^2$ that sets the scale at which $U(x)$'s kinetic term turns on? — is not hypothetical here: **F143 already computed it**, for the free-fermion sea specifically, and found the induced share is $\delta v^2_\text{fermion}/v^2\in[0.16\%,0.36\%]$ (T3 reproduces this from F143's own quoted inputs: $m_t=172.57$ GeV, $\hat f\in[0.17,0.38]$, $(\Delta Y/2)^2=\tfrac14$, $N_c=3$; recomputes $96$–$215\ \text{GeV}^2$ against $v^2=(246.22\ \text{GeV})^2$, matching F143 §5 exactly). **Fermion-loop induction is not the mechanism — it supplies under half a percent.** F143's own conclusion (echoed by F150, applying F147's rigidity theorem to the *lepton* brake $C$) is that the wrap stiffness is owned $\gtrsim99.6\%$ by the nonperturbative $E_g$ condensate sector, the same sector that sets the charged-lepton mass hierarchy (F118).

**(ii) NJL-style dynamical transmutation** — a small ratio generated exponentially from an $O(1)$ coupling via a marginal (logarithmically running) channel. F119 (2026-06-09) tried this for the analogous lepton-scale number $N=m_\text{lat}(\tau)$ and reported it excluded: *"the model's couplings are essentially non-running at the lattice scale… the one channel that could generate $N$ from $O(1)$ inputs is currently absent."* **This conclusion is stale and the ledger's own E7 row already knows it, without spelling out why:** F233 (2026-07-02, superseding F144) found the channel is not absent — it is **QCD asymptotic freedom itself**, the bare coupling $\alpha_s(\mu_0)=1/(16\pi)$ at $\mu_0=\hbar c/a$ running down (standard $\overline{\text{MS}}$, zero knobs) to $\Lambda_{\overline{\text{MS}}}$, giving $N_\text{pred}=2.86\times10^{-19}$ against $N=5.54\times10^{-19}$ — **a factor $1.9$ across 19 decades, with no free parameter** (T4 reproduces this ratio, $5.54/2.86=1.94$). The entire residual is one shared one-loop matching constant, $d_1$ (equivalently $\Lambda_{\overline{\text{MS}}}/\Lambda_\text{lat}$), the same number F150 identifies as owning the lepton brake $\lambda_6$'s exact magnitude and Q1/Q2 (F124/F154)'s scale-setting residuals.

So mechanism (i) is checked and negligible for $v$ specifically (F143); mechanism (ii) is **not absent** — it is a live, partially-successful, already-published result, currently contingent on the single unresolved Part D branch choice (`open-derivations.md`, the $\alpha_s(M_Z)$ Casimir-vs-centre fork) and on the un-finished background-field computation of $d_1$ itself (F162, "$b_0=\tfrac{11}3C_A$ already exact, finite digit open"). Citing F119's "no channel" line without F233's correction — which is exactly what a literal reading of this session's own brief did, before this check — reproduces a stale negative that the tree already retired for the sibling quantity $N$.

## 5. The natural next step, honestly flagged as unproven

F150 states, of the lepton brake: *"the exact value of $\lambda_6$ is the SAME single nonperturbative IR-coupling normalization that already owns the F124/F144/F145 residuals"* — i.e. $\lambda_6\!\leftrightarrow\!d_1$. F143 states, of $v$: the wrap stiffness is owned by "the $\kappa_E<0$ spontaneous-gap sector of F118… computing $f_{E_g}$ (the condensate's phase stiffness in lattice units) is now the single remaining item." Both point at the **same** $E_g$ condensate, evaluated at the same saturated amplitude. It is a natural conjecture that $f_{E_g}$ is *also* a function of $d_1$ — which would mean $v$'s FIT(N=1) residual is not a separate free parameter at all, but the same single already-tracked unknown as $N$ and $\lambda_6$. **This finding does not prove that** — F143 (fermion-loop share) and F150 (brake magnitude) are computed from different pieces of the condensate's effective action (a kinetic/stiffness normalization vs. a sextic self-coupling), and no existing finding computes $f_{E_g}$'s dependence on $d_1$ directly. Stating it as established would repeat exactly the shape of false-positive risk flagged for $\alpha_\text{em}$ (D#14): a plausible-looking closure that quietly assumes the thing being asked for. It is recorded here as the concrete, correctly-scoped follow-up: compute $f_{E_g}(d_1)$ the way F150 computed $\lambda_6$'s membership in the $d_1$ cluster.

Separately — and this is the one candidate mechanism this session identifies as genuinely *un*checked, not merely negligible or contingent — F79's own derivation of $G$ came from a **gauge-sector** (photon) vacuum loop, not a fermion loop. The true structural analogue for $v$ is therefore not "does the free-fermion sea induce the wrap stiffness" (F143: checked, negligible) but **"does a lattice gauge-boson loop induce it"** — this has not been computed by F143, F147 or F149 (all three gauge fermions only) or anywhere else found in this tree (checked by grep across `findings/` for "gauge boson loop" / "boson-loop induced" combined with "wrap" or "Goldstone" — no hit). Named, not attempted here.

## 6. Order-of-magnitude type-check (T5, illustrative only)

F232's degeneracy theorem excludes observables that are *dimensionless and mass-blind* from pinning $a$ (the coefficient $-4$, PPN $\beta=\gamma=1$, $\sin^2\theta_W=2/9$). Once $a$ is fixed, $v$ *is* dimensionless in lattice units ($v_\text{lat}\equiv v/(\hbar c/a)$) — but it is not mass-blind: it is a mass-type quantity, structurally the same kind of object as a fermion's $m_\text{lat}$, not the same kind of object as an angle or a ratio of counts. T5 computes $v_\text{lat}=246.22\ \text{GeV}/(1.850\times10^{18}\ \text{GeV})=1.33\times10^{-16}$ and notes it sits, as expected for a mass-type number, in the same order-of-magnitude regime as F119's own table ($m_\text{lat}(\tau)=5.54\times10^{-19}$, $m_\text{lat}(\text{top})=5.39\times10^{-17}$) — between the tau and about $2.5\times$ the top. This is presented strictly as a category illustration (confirming $v$ is the *kind* of quantity F232's theorem was never about), **not** as a derivation of $v$'s value — no claim is made about the number $1.33\times10^{-16}$ beyond its order of magnitude and its type.

## 7. Verdict

Both halves of the question posed are answered: $v$ is **not** forced by $G$ (mechanically false — $v$ is outside the free-symbol set of $G$'s one equation, §2), and $v$ is **not** a second, independent gap needing its own new $G$-like pin (§4: both known generation mechanisms are already checked against it — one negligible, F143; one live-but-contingent, via the corrected F233 citation — and its residual is plausibly, though not yet provably, the SAME single tracked constant $d_1$ that already owns $N$ and $\lambda_6$). This promotes ledger row E7 / parameter #17 from an unattempted anchor to a documented ceiling with a named mechanism-space (closed for two known channels, contingent on one open one, $d_1$) and one clearly identified genuinely-untried calculation (gauge-boson-loop induction of the wrap stiffness, §5). No number for $v$ is claimed. `docs/status/open-derivations.md` row E7 stays `FIT (N=1)` — this finding documents *why*, not a closure.
