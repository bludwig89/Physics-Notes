Design rule (max efficiency): largest gap and lowest voltage that still sustain a stable saturated corona without arc-over, driven from the sharpest practical emitter into a smooth large collector. Efficiency ∝ d/V.

Honesty boundary. μ, corona onset, and the Townsend coefficient are continuum gas-kinetics inputs — not derived from the lattice. The model fixes the mechanism and the efficiency form, not these transport constants. In true high vacuum the model predicts zero net steady-DC thrust (no gas to ionise, no momentum reservoir); the "anti-gravity" reading is excluded. See Applied Note 1.
