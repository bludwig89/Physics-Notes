# Appendix A1 — No-Go Ledger

*Every excluded route in the model, collected from each chapter's own §6 ("What was excluded, and
why") and cross-referenced. This appendix does not re-derive anything; it collects and classifies.
Organized by physics sector, following the monograph's own chapter order. Each entry: what was
proposed, what excluded it, the argument in brief, the chapter/finding, and whether the exclusion is
**structural** (a theorem — cannot be reopened without new mathematics) or **conditional** (could be
reopened, with the reopening condition stated explicitly, following the model of
`findings/F339-alpha-em-nogo-reopening-conditions.md`). Entries that are merely "not yet attempted"
or "an open question, not resolved" are NOT included here — only routes a chapter's own text states
were actually excluded, with an argument. Built incrementally; chapters not yet incorporated are
listed at the bottom.*
