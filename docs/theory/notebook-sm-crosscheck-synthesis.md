## 2. What the field has since vindicated

Not full confirmation of any notebook claim as originally stated — in every case below, the
notebook's *specific* 2007 execution needed at least one correction from the reconstruction before
landing on the vindicated idea — but the underlying intuition or mathematical object turns out to
matter to the field, independently and often unknowingly to the 2007 author.

- **pp.15-29, NB-037/038 (Sachs Lagrangian, torsion discrepancy).** The author's own self-flagged
  "Sachs gets 0, but apparently this doesn't hold!" discrepancy, once the reconstruction supplied
  the missing traceless projection, resolves exactly to the Einstein–Cartan–Sciama–Kibble equation:
  torsion sourced algebraically by fermion spin density. A 2023/2024 published paper proposes a
  concrete tabletop test for exactly this effect.
- **pp.35-36, NB-043/044 (CA lattice speculation).** "The rule's dependency structure defines the
  geometry" and "coordination number determines dimensionality" are, independently of this
  notebook, the explicit organizing theses of at least three active 2020s research programs: causal
  set theory's Myrheim–Meyer dimension estimator, the Wolfram Physics Project (since April 2020),
  and 't Hooft/Elze's cellular-automaton interpretation of quantum mechanics (continuing through
  2025).
- **pp.5-6, NB-007 (paired-fermion photon).** The "two spin-½ objects behaving as one photon that
  only occurs as a pair" idea is not a 2007 original invention — it is, independently, the
  pre-existing "neutrino theory of light" (de Broglie 1932, Jordan, revived by W. A. Perkins through
  the 2010s) — a genuine, if now dormant, minority literature, not a fringe notion invented for this
  notebook.
- **pp.9-11, NB-013 (mass from field-energy divergence).** The deeper intuition — that a particle's
  mass can arise from field/binding energy rather than being a bare input — is exactly how most of
  the *proton's* mass is now known to arise: a 2018 lattice-QCD decomposition found only ~9% of the
  proton's mass comes from quark (Higgs-Yukawa) masses, with the rest from field energy and the
  trace anomaly. (This vindicates the intuition for composite hadrons; it does not rescue the
  notebook's own specific electromagnetic-self-energy mechanism, which is separately excluded for
  the elementary-lepton mass hierarchy — see §3.)
- **pp.110-124, NB-149 (light cone via a stereographic "celestial" coordinate).** Representing a
  null (light-ray) direction by a stereographic complex coordinate on a sphere is, independently,
  exactly the central object of celestial holography — an active 2020s quantum-gravity program (a
  dedicated Simons Collaboration held its first annual meeting in April 2024) attempting to build a
  holographic description of gravity for realistic, asymptotically flat spacetimes.
- **pp.57-76, NB-081 (critique of dropping $\nu_R$).** The author's objection that the minimal SM's
  asymmetric treatment of left- and right-handed neutrinos "destroys the basic symmetry" reads, with
  hindsight, as an early instance of exactly the structural argument that motivates every serious
  modern neutrino-mass extension of the SM (seesaw models, left-right symmetric models) — sharpened
  since 2007 by continuing null results from neutrinoless double-beta decay searches (LEGEND-200,
  2025) that keep the Dirac-vs-Majorana question open.
- **pp.73-74, NB-093–095 (helical-motion mass model).** Once a dropped factor of $c^2$ is corrected,
  the kinematics reduces to the standard relativistic group-velocity formula — and the broader
  "mass from internal helical motion at $c$" picture is the zitterbewegung family of electron
  models (Schrödinger 1930, Hestenes), assessed in a genuine February 2025 peer-reviewed critical
  review, not excluded.

## 3. What the field has since excluded

- **pp.62-72 and pp.108-109, NB-080/NB-142 (Weinberg–Salam without a Higgs field).** The section's
  explicit, stated premise — build electroweak gauge-boson and fermion masses with no dynamical
  scalar at all — is the one place in the whole notebook where a specific physical premise has been
  tested directly by data and found not to hold. The 2012 Higgs discovery, and especially the 2022
  confirmation that its couplings track particle mass across three orders of magnitude (*Nature*
  607, 52-59), is exactly the signature a dynamical Higgs mechanism predicts and a purely
  explicit-mass-term construction does not. "Higgsless" model-building was a real, named,
  contemporaneous research program (roughly 2003-2012); it has not recovered a compelling candidate
  since. The internal gauge-theory algebra explored along the way (the Weinberg-angle relations, the
  two-mass-formula reconciliation) is itself correct and standard — only the section's organizing
  premise is excluded, not its mathematics.
- **pp.9-11, NB-013 (mass-hierarchy-from-interaction-count, the specific mechanism).** The narrower
  claim that a particle's mass is set by *how many kinds* of interaction it has is cleanly falsified
  by the charged-lepton sector alone: electron, muon, and tau share identical SM gauge interactions
  yet span a factor of ~3477 in mass. (The broader "mass from field energy" intuition behind this
  claim is separately vindicated for hadrons — see §2 — but this specific counting mechanism is not
  rescued by that.)

## 4. What remains genuinely open

- **pp.5-6, NB-009 (Higgs as a composite "Cooper pair").** Lands on the real, live pseudo-Nambu-
  Goldstone-boson composite-Higgs research program (addressing the naturalness/hierarchy problem),
  constrained by LHC data to a compositeness scale $f\gtrsim0.6$–$1.3\,\text{TeV}$ but not excluded;
  continuing published work through late 2023.
- **pp.15-29 (the rest of the Sachs program).** Where independently checked, Sachs' quaternionic
  gravity reduces to ordinary GR (the Schwarzschild case) — not contradicted, but also not shown to
  predict anything beyond it. His further, distinguishing claim — that the same field genuinely
  unifies electromagnetism with gravity — has a direct, published outside critique disputing it, and
  essentially no continuation specific to Sachs' own program has appeared since the mid-2010s.
- **pp.35-36 (discrete/emergent-geometry programs generally).** None of causal sets, the Wolfram
  Physics Project, or the 't Hooft/Elze cellular-automaton program has a near-term distinguishing
  experimental test; all are assessed on internal consistency and their ability to reproduce known
  GR/QM/SM physics in a continuum limit.
- **pp.73-74 (zitterbewegung electron-structure models).** Actively debated on internal consistency
  with Dirac theory and SR, not resolved either way; no distinguishing experimental test currently
  proposed.
- **pp.110-124 (celestial holography).** A rapidly growing, well-organized 2020s research community,
  with no proposed experimental test — assessed purely on mathematical/internal consistency and its
  ability to reproduce known flat-space scattering results (soft theorems, BMS symmetry) in
  CFT-like language.
