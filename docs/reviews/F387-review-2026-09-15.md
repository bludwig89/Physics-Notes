## Verdict

Every numeric claim in F387 — the exact direction-anisotropy angles, their `L`-stability, the `Ω_pair(k)`/`|C_odd(k)|` divergence table, the exact commutation of `photon_step_spectral` with the `Ĉ(k)`-transverse/longitudinal split, and the naive-vs-split sourcing comparison — was independently reproduced by a cold subagent that had not read the finding, and separately re-verified by an adversarial referee with full repo access. The fix (`em_photon_sourcing.split_sourced_scenario`) and its module are sound and need no code changes. **But the finding's own causal narrative in §2 overstated the role of the `Ω_pair(k)`/`|C_odd(k)|` *value* mismatch in producing the `iĈ·B≠0` defect.** An elimination experiment (substitute `|C_odd(k)|` for `Ω_pair(k)` exactly, removing the value-mismatch by construction) shows the defect is not reduced — it is marginally *larger* (`2.485` vs `2.415` at tick 6) — proving the dominant, in fact sufficient, cause is structural: *any* scalar per-mode rotation law with no `Ĉ(k)`-projection breaks the invariant when sourced by a longitudinal current, independent of which particular `Ω(k)` it uses. **Narrowed claim: the fix and every quantitative result stand as stated; the mismatch is real and worth recording for other reasons (it rules out "just substitute `|C_odd(k)|` for `Ω_pair(k)`" as an alternative fix), but it is not the mechanism of the no-monopole violation — the mechanism is the total absence of `Ĉ(k)`-awareness in `photon_step_spectral`'s rotation, period.** This correction has been applied in-pass to the finding's §2 heading, its Consequence paragraph, and the module docstring; §3 and §4 (the commutation proof and the fix itself) were already correct and needed no change.

## Independent re-derivation

Subagent A (blind) read `_bcc_uvec`/`bcc_curl_symbol`'s source directly and expanded the small-`q` limit analytically, obtaining `n^+(q) → (q_x,−q_y,q_z)/√3` independently — matching the finding's closed form exactly, by a route that did not require reading F387. It also independently re-ran F87's own `mx1_luminal_curl_rate` and confirmed by direct inspection (not by trusting the finding) that it checks magnitude only, never direction. Per the claim card's own allowed-inputs list, subagent A had legitimate access to F386 (explicit prerequisite context, mirroring the precedent F386's own review set for reading `charge_coupling.py` in full) and disclosed transparently that F386 §6/§5 already stated the target numbers — so TARGET-A/B are independently *re-verified from scratch* rather than *blind-discovered*; the review grades this per-axis below rather than discarding the run, matching the command's own guidance for a partial-independence outcome. The DESIGN-QUESTION harness and fix, however, were built from nothing but the claim card and are genuinely novel to the subagent — it independently arrived at the identical sector-split mechanism (transverse rotates via `Ω_pair`, longitudinal solved algebraically from Gauss's law) that `em_photon_sourcing.py` implements, and it was the first to flag, unprompted, that the value-mismatch might not be the true cause (it measured the defect appearing at tick 2 while `Ω_pair≈|C_odd|` to `0.02%`). The orchestrator's own elimination experiment (run between the two subagents) and the referee's independent re-run of it are what turned that observation into a confirmed, quantified correction.
