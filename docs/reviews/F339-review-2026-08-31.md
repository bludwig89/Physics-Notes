## Verdict

The finding's core conclusion — $\alpha_\text{em}$ stays `OPEN`, with F127's four avenues now individually characterized — holds. One specific claim was overstated: the finding described its own `test_ward_transversality_exact` check as an "independent re-derivation" of the Ward-transversality identity behind avenue A. It is not — it re-verifies a model-independent tensor-algebra tautology (true for *any* scalar $\Pi(q^2)$ times that transverse form), not F251/F277's harder, model-specific result that the fermion loop actually *takes* that transverse form. The finding and claim card CL113 were corrected in place to credit F251/F277 alone for that result and to describe the check accurately. The hypercharge rank-6/7 re-derivation (avenue D) is genuinely independent and survives unchanged.

**Narrowed claim:** avenue A's reopening condition is narrower than F127 stated, but on F251/F277's authority alone — F339 contributes a sanity check on the trivial corollary, not a second independent computation of the hard result.

## The claim, as the reviewer understood it (given to the cold subagent)

F339 is a gap-analysis of an existing no-go (F127, 2026-06-10) against deriving $\alpha_\text{em}$: for each of four avenues, name the one assumption whose failure would reopen it, using findings that post-date F127. No new derivation of $\alpha_\text{em}$ is claimed.
