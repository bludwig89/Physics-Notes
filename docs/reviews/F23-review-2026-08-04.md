## Independent re-derivation

Subagent A read only `bilinear.py`, `bcc.py` and the papers overview — never F21,
F23, F25, any fork, or any review. It confirmed no forbidden file was read.

**A found the exact closed form the finding does not have.** Three identities, each
checked numerically: (I) $E = 2\lvert n\rvert\,\mathrm{Re}\,G_T$ and
$B = 2\lvert n\rvert\,\mathrm{Im}\,G_T$ are real, with $A\perp C$ and $\lvert A\rvert=\lvert C\rvert$
for a helicity eigenspinor, so $\lVert E\rVert = \lVert B\rVert$ *exactly*
(measured 1.0000000000000007); (II) $\Delta E = (\cos\Omega-1)E + \sin\Omega\,B$
exactly ($1.7\times10^{-13}$); (III) $\hat n\times G_T = \pm iG_T$, so
$2n\times B = \pm2\lvert n\rvert E$ ($5\times10^{-16}$). Substituting,
$\lVert\Delta E - i\,2n\times B\rVert^2 = 8N^2\sin^2(\Omega/2)$ with denominator
$2N\lvert k\rvert$, hence

$$R(k) = \frac{\sqrt2\,\lvert n(k/2)\rvert}{\lvert k\rvert} = \frac{\sqrt2\,\sin(\Omega/2)}{\lvert k\rvert}
\quad\text{exactly at all }k,\qquad R(k\to0) = \frac{c_\text{lat}}{\sqrt2} = \frac1{\sqrt6}.$$

**A's mechanism is stronger than F23's.** $\Delta E \parallel B$ and
$2n\times B \parallel E$, with magnitudes *exactly* equal (ratio 1.0000, angle
89.97°) — two equal-magnitude orthogonal vectors, so the residual is $\sqrt2\times$
either side: the maximum mismatch short of anti-parallel. And **dropping the $i$
changes nothing** (0.4078 vs 0.4082) — the 90° *spatial* misalignment alone gives
the $\sqrt2$. F23 attributes the lock to the real/imaginary split alone; that is
half of it.

**A proved the no-go F23 only argues.** Since the LHS is real and $i\,2n\times B$
imaginary for *any* real weighting, $R$ is a strict quadrature, so
$R \ge 2\lvert n\rvert\lVert B\rVert/[(\lVert E\rVert+\lVert B\rVert)\lvert k\rvert]$
and $R=0$ requires $B\equiv0$ — a static, magnetically empty configuration. That is
a theorem over all weightings, not a demonstration on three families.

**A's smearing sweep contradicts the literal headline.** Mean over 5 non-degenerate
directions, stable across $\lvert k\rvert = 2\times10^{-2}\to2\times10^{-4}$:

| class | parameter | $R(k\to0)$ mean | min over dirs | $\lVert E\rVert/\lVert B\rVert$ | $\lVert\text{LHS}\rVert/\lVert\text{RHS}\rVert$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | — | 0.408251 | 0.40820 | 1.0000 | **1.0000** |
| (b) $\sigma=\alpha\lvert k\rvert/2$ | $\alpha=5$ | 0.86887 | **0.079** | 1.72 | 4.59 |
| (c′) shell $\delta=\beta\lvert k\rvert/2$ | $\beta=1$ | 0.39167 | **0.180** | 1.22 | 1.334 |
| (c′) | $\beta=2$ | **0.36369** | **0.202** | 3.67 | 2.326 |
| (c′) | $\beta=5$ | 0.41391 | 0.168 | 8.20 | 5.19 |

So smearing *does* move the coefficient — down to 0.3637 in the mean and 0.079 on a
single direction. A's explanation, which the inline pass accepts: those are
**normaliser artefacts**. Wherever $R$ falls, $\lVert E\rVert/\lVert B\rVert$ has
grown ($3.7\times$, $8.2\times$) — $B$ is suppressed, inflating the denominator —
while $\lVert\text{LHS}\rVert/\lVert\text{RHS}\rVert$ moves *away* from 1. The
defensible negative is A's: **no smearing improves agreement between the two sides;
the unsmeared construction is the unique member of all three families at which
$\lVert\text{LHS}\rVert = \lVert\text{RHS}\rVert$ exactly.**

A also found that the fixed-width families have **no $k\to0$ limit at all** (once
$\sigma\gg\lvert k\rvert$ the coefficient diverges: 0.410 → 0.472 → 1.801 as $k$
falls). They are ill-defined, not merely unhelpful.

**A's own conclusion, verbatim:** *"the $O(k)$ residual is a property of the
residual's definition, not a defect of the bilinear dynamics… the curl structure is
ALREADY PRESENT, hidden in identity (III): $\hat n\times B = \pm E$ is exactly the
helicity relation Maxwell demands. A continuum plane-wave photon with real $E$, $B$
fed through this same residual definition would return the same $1/\sqrt6$."*

## The claim that decides the verdict

F23's `## Root cause` and `## Next fork` are the sections that propagated, and they
are wrong. Verified in this session against the shipped `bilinear` module, BCC walk,
direction $(1,0.4,0.7)$:

- $E = 2\,\mathrm{Re}\,g$ and $B = 2\,\mathrm{Im}\,g$ are **real by construction**
  (measured $\mathrm{Im} = 0.0$ exactly). Therefore $\partial_t E = \Omega B$ is real
  at every $\Delta t$, including the exact derivative. It does not "become complex".
- The residual is $\Delta t$-independent to 7 significant figures across six decades
  of $\Delta t$ (table above).
- The prescribed fork — "run the curl test at $\Delta t = 10^{-3}$… if curl$/\lvert k\rvert \to 0$
  as $\Delta t \to 0$ the $O(k)$ residual is entirely a finite-$\Delta t$ artefact" —
  would have returned **0.4082327**, i.e. a null result, and H3 would have been
  struck off as the last surviving hypothesis on Finding 2's list.
- What *does* work is the analytic-amplitude identification, which F23 does not
  consider: $8.27\times10^{-3} \to 8.33\times10^{-6}$ as $c^2k/4$ at $\Delta t=1$,
  and $c^3k^2/48$ for the exact derivative.

So F23's H3 is the right *hypothesis name* attached to the wrong *mechanism*. The
mismatch is between a real field-space rotation and a complex Fourier curl — a
representation choice — not between discrete and continuous time.
