## Verdict

The core numerical claim survives every attack applied: the calculation is not circular, the model-native input ($\Lambda_\text{model}$) is genuinely free of hand-tuning, the method reproduces the historical literature verdict independently, and the negative conclusion is falsifiable and specific. **Narrowed claim:** this finding shows that RG-improving the F73/F77 compositeness relation from the model's own derived cutoff *specifically excludes minimal single-channel top condensation as the joint origin of $m_t$ and $m_H$*; it does **not** show that no binding-dynamics mechanism in this model can ever reach 125.25 GeV (the Bethe–Salpeter and multi-channel/topcolor-analogue routes remain untested, and the finding body already says so — the narrowing here is about not letting the title or verdict line imply more than that).
