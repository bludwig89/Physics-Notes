## What the blind agent got right, and where its recommendation didn't hold up

The blind subagent's numeric work was accurate and its two "unplanned findings" were real:

- **`bcc_curl_symbol`'s direction is anisotropic**: `Ĉ(k) → (k_x,-k_y,k_z)/|k|`, not `k̂`, as `k→0` off the cubic axes — exact and stable from `L=16` to `L=256` (confirmed independently by the referee with a closed-form derivation from `_bcc_uvec`: `arccos(1/3)=70.53°` on the body diagonal, `90°` on a face diagonal, both exact). Magnitude (`|C|/|k|→1/√3`) is untouched (squaring removes the sign) — every existing speed/`c_lat` check survives.
- **The beam/mode test helpers (`photon.build_beam_packet`, `build_pair_mode`) are not `Ĉ(k)`-transverse** — they carry substantial (tens of percent, growing with lattice size at fixed physical parameters) `C(k)`-longitudinal content, traced to the same anisotropy.
- **`photon_step_spectral` does not preserve `iC·B=0` when sourced with a purely-`C`-longitudinal current** — `B` grows nonzero (not zero, as the finding's first version claimed) and stays purely `C`-longitudinal, because `Ω_pair(k)` (the paired-photon's actual rotation rate) and `|C_odd(k)|` (the vector curl symbol `solve`/`em_current`/`maxwell_curl_step` all use) agree only at small `k`.

But the blind agent's own recommendation (lean accumulate) did not survive the referee's follow-through: accumulate's exact inertness to a *pure* longitudinal current in isolation is true but is a red herring for the case that matters — a *mixed* source (real transverse photon content plus any longitudinal contamination, whether from F384's current or from the `Ω_pair`/`C(k)` mismatch above). `accumulate` has no mechanism to separate the two; `solve` discards the longitudinal part by construction regardless of its origin. The referee's independent adjudication: **the new evidence strengthens solve, it does not overturn the decision.**
