# Independent review — F382: vertex kernel not $G$-periodic; cube domain excluded

**Reviewed:** 2026-09-10 - 16:20
**Verdict:** CONFIRMED-NARROWER
**Mode:** cold subagents (blind re-derivation + adversarial referee)

## Verdict

The core measurement — the vertex-derived numerator $M$ in `pi_loop`'s integrand does not share the propagator's exact invariance under the BCC reciprocal lattice, and swapping the loop's integration domain from the Wigner–Seitz quarter-cube to the full cube makes the anomalous slow convergence *worse*, not better — survives both an independent blind re-derivation and a 13-point adversarial attack pass. The finding is narrowed on three points, all fixed in the finding body and the underlying code as part of this review's close-out (per this project's own established convention, F337 §"Reviewed & corrected" / F350 §9, of folding review fixes back into the finding rather than deferring them): (1) the automated `bad_diverges` criterion in `domain_choice_divergence` used an endpoint-only deviation comparison that a genuine reviewer-found counterexample ($n=(6,8,10)$) flipped to a false "no divergence" reading, because `domain='cube'`'s $b_0$ recovery crosses exactly through 1 between $n{=}8$ and $n{=}10$ and $|1-b_0|$ is non-monotonic through a sign crossing; replaced with a monotonicity test on $b_0$ recovery itself (verified strictly increasing at every consecutive $n=6$–$20$, both branches) plus a healthy final-margin requirement. (2) The sweep range is extended from $n=8$–$16$ to $n=6$–$20$ (cheap, ~60s total), strengthening the "no turnover" claim the original finding could only hedge. (3) The claim that this finding "reinforces F337's L6 decision" is narrowed — domain choice was never one of L6's two litigated legs (propagator scheme; branch fork), only an inherited default from F305's G7 — to "corroborates the WS-cell restriction G7 already licenses," which is the claim the evidence actually supports.

## The claim, as the reviewer understands it

Two internal lattice-numerics measurements on a one-loop LPT vacuum-polarization computation (BCC rhombic gauge action, background-field gauge): (1) does the vertex-derived part of the loop integrand share the propagator's exact invariance under the same BCC reciprocal lattice that licenses restricting the loop sum to one Wigner–Seitz quarter of the sampling cube; (2) if not, does summing over the full cube instead measurably improve the convergence of a $b_0$-recovery diagnostic that is known (prior work) to approach 1 anomalously slowly on the WS-restricted BCC side. Both are meant to be settled by direct machine-precision measurement, not estimate or literature comparison — there is no external experimental value in play.

## Independent re-derivation

Cold subagent A, working from a claim card with no mechanism given, was first run with full read access to the target module and self-disclosed reading the forbidden appended section in full (the run was discarded per protocol). Re-run with a pre-truncated copy of the module (ending immediately after `check_l6_decision`, structurally excluding every F382-era function) and an explicit allow-list of importable-but-not-readable function names. This run reported, unprompted, a clean compliance statement and reached:

- **Q1 (periodicity):** independently derived the structural mechanism (the propagator's `khat_i` is linear in a half-period phase and flips sign under the relevant $G$-shift, but its *square* — the quadratic form — is invariant because the sign flip cancels; the vertex numerator is built from `khat` linearly, not squared, so nothing forces its invariance) and measured it directly: propagator control $3.6\times10^{-15}$, vertex numerator up to $57.1$ (relative to per-component scales of order a few) across 6 random $(k,G)$ pairs. Same qualitative and quantitative conclusion as the finding, via an independently written derivation of *why*.
- **Q2 (domain):** independently swept $n=8$–$16$, `domain='ws'` vs `'cube'`, and reproduced the finding's own $b_0$-recovery numbers to 4 significant figures (e.g. $n{=}12$: $0.4134$ ws / $1.7117$ cube, matching exactly) via its own driver script calling the same production `transverse_B_chunked`.

This is graded **forced-route**, not independent, on Q2: both the blind agent and the finding call the same production integral (`transverse_B_chunked`/`pi_loop_chunked`); an exact numeric match across an independently written driver is a real check that the invocation and interpretation are correct, but it is not a from-scratch reimplementation of the loop integral, which was outside the scope achievable with the tools deliberately made available for this review.

## What would falsify this

If a future sweep at $n>20$ measures `domain='cube'`'s $b_0$ recovery turning over (ceasing to increase monotonically) and settling to a deviation from 1 below `domain='ws'`'s own, that would overturn this finding's exclusion of the domain-choice candidate. No such turnover has been measured across $n=6$–$20$ for either branch (strictly monotonic throughout, in both the original and the extended sweep).

## Recommendations

1. **(Applied during this review's close-out, per house convention.)** Fix `domain_choice_divergence`'s `bad_diverges` criterion to use monotonicity of $b_0$ recovery rather than an endpoint comparison of $|1-b_0\text{ recovery}|$, which is not robust to a sign crossing. Extend the default sweep to $n=6$–$20$. Narrow the "reinforces L6" language. Add a named falsifiability threshold. All done; verified with the fixed test suite (5/5 PASS) and a direct re-check of $ns=(6,8,10)$, `swap_control=True`, and both branches at the extended default range.
2. **Not applied — left for a future session under its own claim, per this project's ledger discipline.** The two remaining untested candidates for d1 leg 3's slow convergence (per F350 §5/§7, now with the domain-choice sub-question of the first candidate closed by F382): the phys-branch projection's specific interaction with the vertex's non-invariance (a narrower question than "which domain," untested), and the $g_s=\tfrac12$ monotonicity assumption (F350's candidate (b), a physics question rather than a numerics one).
3. Consider (not urgent, not done here): identifying *why* the full-cube sum diverges rather than merely fails to help — e.g. whether it is dominated by non-aliased high-$|k|$ vertex content that has no business being summed at all for this observable. Neither reviewer attempted this; it is a distinct question from what F382 set out to answer.
