### The four that mattered

**2 — the $48$ cancels.** F79 gives $a^2/\ell_P^2=2\pi\eta g_*\sqrt d$, so $S_\text{req}=\pi\eta g_*\sqrt d/2$ carries the *same* $g_*$ that multiplies $c_\text{walk}$, and $\text{ratio}=24c/(\pi\sqrt3)$ **for any $g_*$**. The first draft's readings 1 ($N_\text{eff}=15.097$) and 3 (ruler stretch $1.7831$) each vary one side of that identity while freezing the other. Both were withdrawn. B also noticed the required coefficient is the closed form $\pi/(8\sqrt3)$, which the first draft quoted as a decimal without recognising.

**6 — F79's $G$ is itself induced.** F79 §1: the gravity field "carries no bare kinetic term, and its entire stiffness is forced to be the induced response". So §2's punchline — "this model has no such freedom" — was false, and the finding was not testing $S_\text{horizon}=S_\text{ent}$ against an independent $G$ but comparing two of the model's own induced-$1/G$ computations. §2 was rewritten around this; it is the single largest improvement the review produced.

**4 — the $\sigma$ was $4.7\times$ too small.** The hardcoded $0.0015$ was the ball s.e.m. alone. The JSON's own `ball.sd` is $0.0025$; refitting the same four points with a subleading term gives $0.7146$ or $0.7089$ ($\pm0.006$ fit-form systematic); and the gate-scale estimator sits $0.0053$ away. Widened to $0.0070$, which also matches A's independent $\pm0.008$. Consequence: the "$5.7\sigma$ from $\pi$" and "$3.1\sigma$ from 15" exclusions become $1.2\sigma$ and nothing, and were withdrawn.

**5 — look-elsewhere.** $2^{5/3}=3.17480$ is closer to the measured ratio than $\pi$ is ($0.15\%$ vs $1.21\%$); with $\sim$15–30 simple constants in $[2.5,4]$, $p\approx0.3$–$0.5$. Declining to *claim* the proximity was right; asserting a positive *exclusion* on a bad $\sigma$ was not.

### The doubler adjudication — B's central finding

B verified A's four points against the module's own `_uvec` and computed their Berry monopole charges: $-1,+1,+1,-1$, summing to exactly zero (Nielsen–Ninomiya). It found `doubler|doubling|Nielsen|omega = pi|zone corner` returned **nothing** across the finding, module, test and card. It located F278 §6, which puts the $\pi$-mode "exactly on the true zone boundary" and says whether that constitutes a doubler "is **not decided here**" — and observed that F355 works on the crystal, i.e. squarely in the domain where the mode exists, while citing F278 only for the lattice constant.

Effect: the per-cell number is bracketed $8.65$ / $17.30$ / $34.60$, ratios $0.795$ / $1.590$ / $3.179$. The mismatch survives in all three; the magnitude moves by $4\times$ and the **direction flips** at the four-node reading.
