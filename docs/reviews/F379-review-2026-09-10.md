## Verdict

The finding's central structural argument — that Anastopoulos's Postulate 1 is stated at the prequantisation level (a transitive symplectic group action on the classical phase space, entirely prior to any Hamiltonian), so no *dynamical* symmetry fact (this model's emergent continuous $SO(3)$ included, however large or exactly derived) can supply it — is accurate to the primary source and survives all 13 attacks. It is independently corroborated by a blind re-derivation that reached the same conclusion from the primary source alone, and that re-derivation supplied a genuine sharpening (now folded into the finding's §3): even Anastopoulos's own §4.1 worked example has an *exact* transitive symplectic $SO(3)$ action — stronger than any dynamical-covariance fact — and still needs Postulate 1's condition (iii), the prequantising-bundle lift, as a further ad hoc ingredient. **Narrowed for two reasons.** First, the finding's numeric checks (K1, K2) were originally framed as more substantive physics than they are: K1 is a standard, model-independent $SU(2)$-adjoint identity, and K2's continuous-vs-discrete extension is largely an automatic Taylor-consistency corollary of F344's already-established coefficients — both are now stated honestly as such in the finding, with their genuine content (confirming this model's actual rotor/dispersion code realises the identity) kept. Second, two citation errors (wrong journal; a quote misattributed to the paper's Conclusions rather than its Introduction) and one real code bug (an untested branch of `shipped_walk_continuous_covariance_decades` used a sign matrix contradicting F344's own stated convention, failing at $O(1)$) were found and are fixed in the finding, module, and test as of this review.

## The claim, as the reviewer understands it

**TARGET:** whether the model's emergent continuous $SO(3)$ (recovered in its continuum/RG limit) can supply Anastopoulos's Postulate 1, the one non-topological ingredient a topological (belt-trick) derivation of spin-statistics needs, where the model's finite lattice point group $O_h$ (F330) could not.
**VALUE claimed:** (1) numeric — the model's leading-order Hamiltonian/dispersion is exactly covariant under the full continuous $SO(3)$, via the same rotor object used throughout F289/F330; (2) structural — this is nonetheless insufficient, for a reason that generalises beyond "too small/finite": Postulate 1 is architecturally prequantisation-level, logically prior to any dynamics, so no dynamical symmetry fact of any size can supply it.
**CLASS claimed:** the completeness row (A9, spin-statistics) stays at its existing partial grade; this finding does not claim to close it, only to close off one specific route and to sharpen why.
**Ambiguity in the finding's own statement:** none material — the "stated before the results" framing in §1 and the explicit "what this is, honestly stated" caveats added after this review (§4/§5) leave no room to misread K1/K2 as more than they are.

## Independent re-derivation

A cold `general-purpose` subagent was given the claim card, permission to read F289/F330/F344 as allowed prerequisites, explicit prohibition on reading F379 itself or its supporting code/test/results, and told to fetch Anastopoulos's paper directly and reason independently about whether the framing (dynamical vs. kinematic) is right, wrong, or something else.

It reached the same value/conclusion as F379 — categorical mismatch, not a matter of degree — via its own reading of the paper's §1 (prequantisation, "before constructing the physical Hilbert space") and §3 ("Symmetries are generated by a Hamiltonian flow, but this is the case for dynamics only if time-translation is a symmetry"). It reached this by a **different route** than a mechanical confirmation of F330's own wording: it constructed its own decisive comparison ("even an exact transitive symplectic $SO(3)$-on-$S^2$, in the paper's own §4.1 worked example, still needs condition (iii) separately") rather than simply restating F330's "$O_h$ is finite, $\Omega(\mathbf k)$ is dynamical" line. This is graded as strong, genuine support — a different route to the same conclusion — and its novel comparison has been incorporated into the finding's §3 as a second, deeper layer.

It also surfaced, via a brief literature search, that the wider spin-statistics/geometric-quantisation literature (Berry–Robbins and critiques of it) shows the same shape of gap recurring: every successful route constructs an explicit transport/holonomy law rather than merely asserting a larger or more exact symmetry group.
