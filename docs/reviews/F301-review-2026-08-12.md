## What would falsify this

1. **The armed one.** If the physical charged lepton and the neutrino propagate on a single chiral
   branch, the model predicts a helicity-odd dimension-five LIV parameter $\eta\approx1.5$ — already
   excluded by Crab-Nebula electron synchrotron ($\lvert\eta\rvert\lesssim10^{-5}$) and IceCube
   ($\lesssim1.2\times10^{-5}$), with a vacuum-Cherenkov threshold at 12.9 TeV against a $\sim$1 PeV
   requirement. Surviving demands $b_2$ suppressed by $>5$ orders, i.e. even-law fermion propagation.
2. A measurement of photon vacuum dispersion at the $\lvert k\rvert^3$ level inconsistent with
   $-\tfrac1{18}(p+3q)$ (F28's channel, not re-derived here).
3. Any exact finite-$a$ Poincaré realisation on BCC with a **single** momentum map serving photon and
   fermion would refute §3.7 — the referee confirmed both known maps are radial, $\lvert P\rvert=f(\Omega_\text{ch})$,
   and CL263 already flags a non-radial map as the honest gap.
4. A demonstration that a spinor-augmented boost generator cancels $D_i$ would refute §2 fact 3 — not
   tested by either agent, and the strongest untested route.

## Method notes

- **Deviation from the command template.** The claim card's `VALUE` field was deliberately **withheld**
  from subagent A. The claimed orders ($O(k^3)$ photon, $O(k^2)$ chiral, exact on the axes) *are* the
  punchline, and quoting them would have turned the blind run into a confirmation exercise. A derived
  its own numbers and they were compared afterwards. This is recorded as a departure, not hidden.
- **Normalisation mismatch caught late.** A's $D$ differs from F301's by $c^2$ because F301 never
  states its normalisation. Every "match" in this report is after that conversion; had it not been
  caught, the chiral coefficient would have read as a factor-3 discrepancy.
- **A read no forbidden file.** It confirmed this explicitly and its search pattern is consistent with
  the claim (it never grepped `findings-index.md`). No re-run was needed.
- **Attack 6 is the review's own physics** and is the judgement I am least sure of. The conversion
  $\eta=2\sqrt3\,b_2(E_P/E_\text{lat})$ uses the model's F107 cell; a different scale convention moves
  $\eta$ but not by five orders. The escape hatch (even-law fermion propagation) is real and is
  exactly CL263 falsifier 2 — so this is a *conditional* exclusion, and the condition is internal to
  the model, not experimental.
- **Attack 12 was run** (not skipped); no `--fast` flag was used. Nothing was armed and no baseline was
  written; `test-results/F301_boost_covariance.json` retains its 2026-08-06 07:22 mtime.
- The three judgements I am least sure of: the $\eta$ conversion above; whether the $[J,H]$ reduction
  should count as closing attack 10 or as a separate unproved leg; and whether F30's Result 2 is close
  enough to §3.5 to count as internal prior art rather than an independent route.
