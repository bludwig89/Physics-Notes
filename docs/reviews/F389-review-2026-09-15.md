**Reviewed:** 2026-09-15 - 17:15

## Verdict

The core algebraic claim — that a `Ĉ(k)`-transverse addition cannot perturb F384's discrete continuity closure at all — is an exact, forced linear-algebra identity, independently re-derived from a different route than the finding's own presentation and verified bit-identical (not merely small) across a wide sweep of configurations by both the blind agent and the referee. The coupled-loop consequence (genuine radiation, partial but not exact momentum recovery with a robust `O(g)`-vs-`O(g²)` order mismatch) also reproduces independently. Two prose overclaims were found and fixed in this pass: the headline "derives, rather than guesses" language understated that the *specific* transverse current chosen is a disclosed, non-unique constitutive choice (only its continuity-safety is forced), and one sentence describing the Coulomb sector as "unchanged" was ambiguous between a true single-tick statement and a false whole-run-trajectory statement. Narrowed claim: **the transverse addition is provably continuity-safe and lets the loop radiate and partially recoil with a structural, quantified order mismatch — but the specific transverse current is a well-motivated, standard, non-unique choice, and "the Coulomb sector is unchanged" holds only tick-to-tick against the same input, not as an invariance of a closed run's own trajectory.**

## The claim, as the reviewer understands it

**TARGET.** In the BCC Weyl-QCA U(1)-current problem, a prior minimal solution of the discrete continuity equation is purely longitudinal (in a fixed, known curl-symbol direction `Ĉ(k)`) by construction, leaving its transverse projection exactly zero and declared "free, unconstrained gauge freedom." **VALUE.** A construction supplying a nonzero transverse component such that (a) continuity remains exactly closed (claimed exact/bit-identical), (b) the transverse content is generically nonzero for a physically evolving state (claimed, quantitatively, `>60%` of the current's norm on tested configurations), (c) wiring it into a coupled fermion↔photon loop lets the loop radiate and partially recover momentum, with a disclosed, non-exact residual. **INPUTS.** The BCC walk's own unitary, the fixed curl symbol `Ĉ(k)`, and — as given, not re-derived — the model's existing continuum-style Noether bilinear current already used for other internal symmetries, whose longitudinal projection is known to fail continuity at `O(a)`. **CLASS.** `machine` exact for the continuity-closure claim; `QUANT` (measured, not exact) for the momentum-recovery numbers. **FORBIDDEN** (for the blind subagent): F384/F388/F389 finding files, `em_current.py`, `coupled.py`, both F389 test files and result JSONs, the F388 review, and any index line naming F384/F388/F389.

The finding's own statement of the claim was not ambiguous in its numerical content; the one place it was ambiguous is flagged above and fixed (§1's "everything else is unchanged" sentence, and the "derives" verb in the opening paragraph).

## Independent re-derivation

Subagent A (blind, forbidden from every F384/F388/F389-adjacent file) independently reconstructed essentially the same fix by a route that starts from the same place the finding does — "continuity fixes only the `Ĉ(k)`-projection of `J`; the transverse projection is a free choice" — and, given the same one input this finding was also given (the model's existing Noether bilinear, known to fail continuity in its longitudinal sector at `O(a)`), converged on the identical construction: keep only that bilinear's transverse-safe projection and add it to the minimal longitudinal solution. This is graded as **support via the same route, not an independently-discovered different route** — the shared route may simply be forced by how thin the problem's free parameter space actually is once the "reuse the model's existing bilinear" input is handed over; the blind agent explicitly flagged this as its own biggest added assumption. It measured, on its own from-scratch script: continuity residual with/without the transverse addition identical to floating-point round-off; the fix's own orthogonality (`‖Ĉ(k)·J̃_T(k)‖ ≈ 1e-14`) confirmed as an exact identity, not a numerical coincidence; a transverse fraction of `70–92%` across a 27-configuration sweep (`L∈{8,16,24}`, 3 seeds, 3 pre-evolution depths) — a wider, independently-designed sweep than the finding's own single default configuration, and consistent with it. It did not build the full coupled loop, but its qualitative Noether-identity argument for why exact momentum conservation should *not* be expected (the field-source and fermion-recoil constructions are not two halves of one common action) matches the finding's own §4 argument, reached independently.

The referee then verified this against the real repository: re-executing `check_full_current` at 6 further configurations (varied `L`, width, on-axis and off-axis `k0`) found `continuity_diff` exactly `0.0` (via `repr()`, not just under a `<1e-12` threshold) in every case, and the transverse fraction remained substantial (`0.49–0.82`) throughout — the qualitative and quantitative claims are not an artifact of the one config in the finding's own table. The referee independently swept `g_lat` across 6 points (`0.025`→`0.8`, not just the gate's 2 doublings) and reproduced the `O(g)`/`O(g²)` scaling (doubling ratios `1.98`–`2.00` and `3.985`–`4.00`) throughout — a genuine structural law over that range, not a two-point coincidence.

## What would falsify this

A configuration where `continuity_diff` becomes measurably nonzero (not `0.0`) after adding the transverse piece would falsify the core algebraic claim; neither the blind agent's 27-configuration sweep nor the referee's independent 6-configuration sweep found one. A `g_lat` regime where the recovered-momentum fraction stopped growing with coupling strength, or where the matter/field doubling ratios departed from `≈2×`/`≈4×`, would falsify the disclosed order-mismatch argument; the referee's independent 6-point sweep (`0.025`–`0.8`) found neither.

## Recommendations

1. **(Applied in this pass.)** Soften "This finding derives, rather than guesses, a physically motivated transverse component" to make clear that only the construction's *continuity-safety* is forced, not the *choice* of which transverse field to use — the choice is a disclosed, standard, non-unique constitutive one.
2. **(Applied in this pass.)** Qualify the §1 "everything else... is unchanged" sentence to scope it explicitly to the single-tick, same-input level, and note that a closed coupled run's `ρ`/`E_L` trajectory necessarily diverges from the `use_radiative_current=False` case once real backreaction occurs — that divergence is the mechanism of the fix, not a contradiction of the per-tick algebraic claim.
3. **(Not applied, future work, ranked lowest — would not change the verdict.)** A from-scratch derivation of the transverse sector directly from the exact walk unitary `U(k)` (an "improved lattice current" in the same spirit as clover-improved lattice QED currents), rather than reusing the existing continuum bilinear, would remove the one remaining "free input" this review counted — but the blind agent independently converged on the same reuse-based construction given the same inputs, suggesting this is close to the best-motivated option available without a substantially larger derivation effort.
