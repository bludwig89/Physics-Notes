## Verdict

Both agents confirm the central structure. The derived Dirac sea is generation-covariant. The Koide spectrum has three O_h×T-inequivalent [111] circulants, with τ, e and μ respectively on (1,1,1). TM1 requires the e-class, while the δ*-as-T1g/T2g reading is the τ-class. The quadratic crystal field never selects either of those two, and it selects (a) iff α, β > 0.

The as-written claim was too wide in two places. First, the "exact tie at the F118 couplings" holds only for the spectral extension of F118. Read per axis, F118's own couplings pick (a), by E(b) − E(a) = +0.27. Second, (a) is the ground state in one of four quadratic regions, not generically.

**Narrowed claim:** picture (a) is the ground state provided the off-diagonal crystal field is stiff (α, β > 0), which F118's per-axis reading supplies (α = β = −κ_E = 2.16) but no first-principles calculation yet does. Independently of that, no single [111] vacuum carries both the δ* reading and TM1.

## The claim, as the reviewer understands it

The claim card given to the blind agent: which of (a) Y = diag(λ) and (b) Y = FΛF† is the ground state, given F118 extended to Hermitian Y plus the most general O_h×T-invariant anisotropy? What do the sea and the invariant kernel say? And, with charged leptons assigned to eigenvectors by mass, which PMNS columns do O's C2′ residuals give? The mechanism (three branches) was withheld.

## Independent re-derivation

Subagent A reached the same answers by a Reynolds-averaged rank count and a direct orbit minimisation:

- a spectral extension of every F118 term, hence the tie;
- the sea identical at (a) and (b) (−18.8506 on a 12³ grid);
- kernel dimensions 0, 2, 6 at degrees 1, 2, 3;
- the full quadratic phase diagram, with minima −1.5, −2.8678, −3.0 and −1.3678 at test couplings;
- three circulant classes with Σz² = 0.0729, 0.8093, 1.3678;
- TM1 only for the class with e on the symmetric mode, sin²θ12 ∈ [0.3170, 0.3195], 1.3–1.7σ above JUNO.

The route was the same as the author's; it may simply be forced. A added two assumptions of its own: that the C2′ axis is the fixed eigenvector, and that the column label is free. It independently raised the point the referee later quantified: extending F118 through the diagonal entries gives (b) e = 0, so (b) loses the Mexican-hat energy.

## What would falsify this

A first-principles off-diagonal crystal-field stiffness with α ≤ 0 or β ≤ 0 would falsify the frame claim, and one selecting the e-class circulant would revive TM1. On the data side, JUNO measuring sin²θ12 ∈ [0.3170, 0.3195] with DUNE/Hyper-K finding δ_CP ∈ [252°, 293°] would turn the missing TM1 into a failure to explain.

## Recommendations

1. (Done in place.) Add the per-axis evaluation as K1b and base the verdict on it; narrow the claim to α, β > 0.
2. (Done.) Add a K8 control, relabel V, and remove the K9 sample-size floor.
3. (Open, research.) Derive the off-diagonal stiffness from first principles, for example the second-shell E_g vs first-shell T2g susceptibility (F93 O2). This is the only thing that would turn the conditional verdict unconditional.
4. (Open.) Extend the kernel count to degrees 4–6, where F118's Σy⁴, e⁴ and S₃² extensions differ.
