## Verdict

its test, its registry record, and every cited prerequisite: F333's premise (a) ("real baryons
are fermions") is not reducible to F289/F330's own derived spin-statistics connection, and the

gap it would have missed, so the verdict is not downgraded.

## Independent re-derivation

A blind subagent, given only the claim card above plus the four prerequisite findings (F289,
F330, F333, F324) and an explicit forbidden list covering every F381-specific file, worked the
question from scratch and reached the same conclusions on all four sub-questions: (1) premise (a)
is not reducible to F289/F330 alone, because neither contains a colour-rank variable and F330's
own §7 explicitly scopes itself to a single antisymmetric pair; (2) F289+S1+S2 gives only a
conditional ("fermion iff N odd"), not a definite fact, because F333 itself states statistics
alone cannot distinguish N=1 from N=3,5,7,…; (3) using F324 to discharge premise (a) is circular,
because F324's own premise (ii) — "the colour sector exists … F318's standing residual,
unchanged" — presupposes the thing being derived; (4) the substitution costs F324's six premises
against F333's two, a large net loss.

**This agreement should not be oversold as independent confirmation by a different route.** The
claim card necessarily licensed F333's S1/S2 machinery and all four prerequisite findings as
inputs — the blind agent could not have re-derived S1/S2's representation theory from nothing in
the time available — so its route is structurally forced to resemble F381's own. It is graded here
as `forced-route`, consistent with the review skill's own stated reading for this outcome: real
support that the argument is internally sound and that no ambiguity in the claim card produced a
different answer, but not evidence of a genuinely alternate derivation path.
