## Verdict

The no-go is correct. It was re-derived blind by a different route, the NuFIT inputs match the source PDFs exactly, and the test's verdicts move under perturbation exactly where they should. It was stated too broadly: the record and card called a data-dependent verdict "exact", the card and changelog gave the wrong reason for excluding the $C_3$ case, and the finding said $O_h$ cannot fix "the PMNS angles" when its own generalised-CP result fixes θ23. **Narrowed claim:** with an *exactly* cube-diagonal charged-lepton frame, no unitary $O_h$ residual is compatible with NuFIT 6.0 at 3σ and no $O_h$ residual of any kind fixes θ12 or θ13; a charged-lepton rotation of order θ13 would evade this.

## Independent re-derivation

Subagent A read nothing forbidden. Its route was different and stronger: with $M=U^*DU^\dagger$ and $h=U^\dagger gU$, $h^TDh=D$ forces $h=\mathrm{diag}(\pm1)$ for distinct masses, so any residual element is an involution and any residual group is at most $Z_2\times Z_2$. It reached the same classification in every row: every nontrivial cube-frame residual puts a zero in PMNS (excluded, $\lvert U_{e3}\rvert\ge0.142$); in the Fourier frame $C_2\to$ TM2 ($\sin^2\theta_{12}\in[0.3402,0.3415]$) and like-sign $C_2'\to$ TM1 ($[0.3170,0.3195]$), the rest excluded; gCP gives $J=0$ (diagonal) or μ–τ reflection ($\theta_{23}=45°$, $\delta=270°$). It added one result the finding lacked: TM1's δ correlation, $\delta\approx252°$–$293°$. Assumptions it added: free row/column placement, and a Gaussian one-sided JUNO error; both match the finding's.

## What would falsify this

The no-go falls only if θ13 = 0 (excluded), if the model abandons the cube-axis frame, or if it acquires a charged-lepton rotation of order θ13. TM1 (if the trimaximal frame were adopted) dies if JUNO puts $\sin^2\theta_{12}$ outside [0.3170, 0.3195] or DUNE/Hyper-K put δ outside [252.4°, 293.4°] at >3σ.
