## The claim, as the reviewer understands it

**TARGET.** Two extensions of F328's free-sector discrete CPT operator $\Theta=MK$ to an SU(2)-isospin-doublet generalisation of its $4\times4$ massive one-tick unitary $D(\mathbf k)$: (1) grafting a fixed SU(2) matrix $U$ into the kinetic block only, asking whether a fixed isospin operator $\Lambda$ extends $\Theta$ to $\Theta'=(M\otimes\Lambda)K$ satisfying $\Theta' D'(\mathbf k)\Theta'^{-1}=D'(\mathbf k)^{-1}$ for every $\mathbf k,m,U$ simultaneously; (2) the same question with the SU(2) matrix $V$ grafted into the mass (off-diagonal) block instead.

**VALUE.** (1) Yes: $\Lambda=\tau_2$ ($=\sigma_y$ numerically), unique up to phase, works for every $U$ simultaneously, machine precision ($\sim10^{-16}$); $\Theta'^2=+\mathbb1$ (not F328's Kramers $-\mathbb1$). (2) No: no fixed $\Lambda$ can work for every $V$ simultaneously — a proved impossibility from group theory (conjugation is an automorphism, $V\mapsto V^T$ is an anti-automorphism, and the two coincide only on an abelian group).

**INPUTS.** F328's own $\Theta$, $M$, identity (I) taken as given/prerequisite; the SU(2) link(s) $U,V$ are external, fixed, spatially-uniform classical backgrounds, not dynamical fields being solved for.

**CLASS.** EXACT for both the kinetic-sector theorem and the mass-sector impossibility proof; MACHINE for the numerical cross-check against the real module (leg X0); the E1 zero-coupling cross-module mismatch and the D2/D3 no-go residuals are explicitly labelled MEASURED, not exactness targets.

**FORBIDDEN** (respected by the blind-rederivation agent, confirmed): `findings/F378-*.md`, `src/casim/engine/particles/discrete_cpt_gauged.py`, `test-results/F378_discrete_cpt_gauged_theorem.json`, the F378 entry in `tests/registry/particles.yaml`, `docs/claims/CL306-*.md`, and every F378 line in `findings-index.md`/changelog/project-status/open-derivations.

The finding's own statement is not ambiguous relative to this reconstruction — the only place a reviewer could stumble is that the module docstring frames the two questions in the opposite order from how a reader might expect (kinetic first, mass second), which both subagents caught correctly on first read.

## Independent re-derivation

The blind agent, given only F328 (allowed) and the raw codebase modules (`bcc.py`, `weak_wmu.py`, `dirac_bcc.py` — read for orientation on the actual gauge-coupling and mass mechanisms, not as answer keys), derived both results from scratch using a **different intermediate identity** than F378 itself: instead of F378's isospin pseudoreality relation $\tau_2 U\tau_2^{-1}=U^{*}$, it transposed F328's identity (I) directly to get $\sigma_y A^T\sigma_y^{-1}=A^\dagger$ (★) and applied ★ to the isospin factor, landing on the kinetic-sector requirement $\Lambda U^T\Lambda^{-1}=U^\dagger$ — algebraically equivalent to F378's route but reached without ever writing down the conjugate-form pseudoreality relation F378 leads with. Both routes converge on $\Lambda=\tau_2=\sigma_y$, unique up to phase (the blind agent additionally supplied a Schur's-lemma uniqueness proof F378 itself does not include), and on $\Theta'^2=+\mathbb1$ with the identical physical reading (two independent pseudoreal twists cancel the Kramers sign).

For the mass sector, the blind agent derived the identical required condition $\Lambda V^T\Lambda^{-1}=V$ and the identical impossibility argument (conjugation-by-$\Lambda$ is always order-preserving; $V\mapsto V^T$ is always order-reversing; forcing them equal forces $SU(2)$ abelian, which is false) — framed slightly more strongly than F378's own §5, since the blind agent's proof explicitly does not require $\Lambda$ to be unitary, only invertible, while F378's *numerical* Monte-Carlo control (leg D3) restricts its search to unitary $\Lambda$ (though F378's own written algebraic argument, on inspection, already covers the non-unitary case too — this is a case of F378 under-advertising the generality of its own proof, not overstating it).

**Reaches the same value by a genuinely different route → strongest possible support**, per the review rubric's grading table. The one thing the blind agent could not check (having no access to `covariant_dirac_doublet_step`'s exact internals beyond a orientation-only skim) was F378's leg E1 zero-coupling cross-module claim — that was left to the adversarial referee, who verified it directly against the source.

## What would falsify this

For the kinetic-sector theorem: any sampled $(\mathbf k,m,U)$ at which $\Theta' D'(\mathbf k)\Theta'^{-1}\ne D'(\mathbf k)^{-1}$ to machine precision. For the mass-sector no-go (within the same-size, fixed-$\Lambda$ scope actually proved): nothing, since it is a closed theorem — the open, well-posed question is whether a **background-transforming** $\Theta$ (mapping $V\to V'\ne V$ rather than requiring the identity at the same $V$) or a **larger-space** $\Lambda$ closes the residual differently; both are explicitly named as open in ledger row A4r rather than claimed closed.
