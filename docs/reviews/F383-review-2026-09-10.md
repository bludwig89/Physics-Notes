## Verdict

The finding's qualitative conclusion survives: on this model's own BCC dispersion, the static matter-density polarization's $q^4$ Taylor coefficient $\Pi_4$ is robustly nonzero (sign never flips under grid refinement, $q$-window change, direction change, or fit-order change), so lattice locality does not forbid the four-derivative curvature term — it generates it, exactly as ordinary Wilsonian EFT reasoning predicts. **Narrowed claim:** only $\Pi_4$'s *sign and order of magnitude* are numerically supportable at the resolutions used; the specific 3-significant-figure values the finding's M2 leg reports are a fit-order artifact (a bare quartic fit is ill-conditioned at these $q$-scales) that shifts by $\sim2.6$–$3\times$ when a $q^6$ term is added to the same fit. This was not disclosed in the finding's first version and is fixed below.

## The claim, as the reviewer understands it

TARGET: whether a local, finite-range 3D lattice's induced matter-density polarization is forced to have a vanishing four-derivative ($q^4$) Taylor coefficient, or whether locality only ever suppresses (not forbids) such a term.
VALUE: $\Pi_4$, the $q^4$ coefficient of $\Pi(q)=\int_\text{BZ}d^3k/(2\pi)^3\cdot1/(\omega(k)+\omega(k+q))$ over the model's F26/BCC dispersion — forced to zero, or generic and computable?
INPUTS: F57's established $\Pi_2>0$ (the two-derivative sibling term, same mechanism); the F26/BCC dispersion module.
CLASS: quantitative (as claimed in both the module registry and test registry — verified correct, not inflated to exact/machine).

## Independent re-derivation

Cold subagent A (blind — had not read F383, F345, or the module/test files) independently reimplemented F57's exact BZ-cube quadrature convention from just the F57 prerequisite, reproduced F57's own $\Pi_0\approx0.446$ and $\Pi_2\approx0.06$ as a fidelity check, then computed $\Pi_4$ from scratch. Result: $\Pi_4$ positive in every one of ~20 configurations tried (grid 80–250, three directions, multiple $q$-windows), never crossing zero. It independently discovered the same fit-ill-conditioning the adversarial referee later confirmed on the finding's own settings: a bare quartic fit's magnitude swings by up to ~12$\times$ across windows, stabilized to $\Pi_4\approx+0.12$ (order 0.1, same order as $\Pi_2$) only once a $q^6$ correction term is added. Its own conclusion, reached independently: locality guarantees UV-finiteness of the derivative expansion term-by-term, not its truncation — matching the finding's own conclusion by a different implementation, though the same underlying route (F57's mechanism) and the same qualitative finding (fit-order sensitivity of the magnitude).
