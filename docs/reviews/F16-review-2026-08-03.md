## Verdict

What survives is narrow and worth keeping: *under the Paper-6 ansatz the Pound–Rebka factor 2 comes from identifying the clock rate with $c(x)$, and any redefinition of the clock removes it; the photon-sector observables GR-1 and GR-2 are insensitive to that redefinition at $O(\varphi)$.* Everything F16 builds on top of that fails. GR-3 is a **closed-form identity in the clock exponent** — the referee reproduced all 16 GR-3 table entries, plus all four means and standard deviations, from algebra on the committed $\varphi$ values to $\le 1.3\times10^{-12}$, so the Poisson solve cancels and there is no simulation content. And the entire GR-4 section — "Fork C predicts half the advance", "Forks A and B reproduce Mercury", "Forks A and B remain viable", "distinguishing A from B is open" — is not supported by its own harness: the harness never integrates any fork's metric.

**Corrected claim:** the factor-2 redshift is a clock-identification artifact of the single-scalar ansatz, removable by definition; GR-4 as measured in this harness discriminates nothing, because all four `metric()` functions return the same linearised Schwarzschild pair and the perihelion integrator is hard-coded to $\beta=\gamma=1$.

## Method notes

Nothing in the repo was changed by either agent; `git status --porcelain` on the finding and both result artifacts is clean, and no baseline was armed. Scratch work is under the session outputs directory only.

**Not verified.** The requested attack-7 perturbation sweep is structurally impossible: the only F16-linked record is path-only, `_run_result_dump` executes it in the repo root, and the script writes a committed baseline. The referee re-implemented the harness's GR-4 integrator from source, reproduced its JSON bit-for-bit, and perturbed that instead — same evidence, no writes. One array-based orbit integrator exceeded the 45 s shell limit and was rewritten with scalar floats.

**The three judgements the reviewers were least sure of.**

1. **Fork A's $\alpha_B$.** The fork genuinely never specifies matter's spatial metric. The referee judged the harness's $(1,1)$ the better reading (⇒ Fork A ≡ Fork B); the blind agent's literal bimetric reading gives $(1,0)$ (⇒ Fork A ≡ Fork C). Both are defensible and both collapse the trichotomy, so the verdict does not turn on it — but the *reason* Fork A dies does.
2. **The $\beta \leftrightarrow \alpha_A$ identification**, inferred from $K_R=2(\alpha_A+\alpha_B)$ matching $2\beta+2\gamma$. The harness states no mapping, so a different reading would give a different "correct" Fork C ratio. The numerics pin the integrator exactly; the PPN translation carries the inference.
3. **OVERSTATED vs REFUTED.** The qualitative statement "GR-4 discriminates, and Fork C is the one it kills" survives under every reading tested; its stated mechanism, its number and its conclusion about the survivors do not. A stricter referee would grade the GR-4 section REFUTED and the GR-3 section CIRCULAR separately.
