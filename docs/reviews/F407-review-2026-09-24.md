## Verdict

The central results survive an independent re-derivation:

- the minimal ansatz has 9 non-redundant parameters against 10 observables;
- it fits PDG masses and CKM at all three candidate E_g pairs in both schemes, with m_s unpulled and a signed down sector;
- off-band pairs fail by χ² ~ 10³–10⁴;
- lepton-like all-positive amplitudes need m_s ≲ 30 MeV (mixed) or ≲ 13 MeV (M_Z).

The M5 result does not survive. The "soft |V_us| floor near 0.19–0.20" is not a property of the ansatz. It is the unitarity bound |V_us| ≳ J/(|V_cb||V_ub|) ≈ 0.199, which comes from keeping J while holding out |V_us| and |V_td|. The M5 leg as written cannot fail.

**Narrowed claim:** the minimal 9-parameter ansatz, with fit-chosen T₁g slot and sign branch, fits the masses and CKM at every candidate E_g pair with m_s unpulled and predicts no CKM number. With |V_us|, |V_td| and J all held out, |V_us| is unconstrained from ≲0.1 to ~0.8, so GST does not emerge, and the ansatz places no floor on |V_us|.

## The claim, as the reviewer understands it

- **TARGET:** does the report's minimal pseudo-mass ansatz fit the data? Per sector it has an exact-Koide diagonal at angle δ_f, three real symmetric off-diagonals, and one T₁g amplitude in one slot. Sub-questions: how many real degrees of freedom does it have, does GST emerge, and what does the all-positive branch require of m_s?
- **VALUE:** 9 vs 10 (rank 9); candidate pairs Δχ² ≤ 1; all-positive m_s ≈ 27 / 12.6 MeV; GST not emergent with a soft floor ~0.2.
- **CLASS:** numerical / quantitative.
- **Ambiguity found:** "Δχ² ≤ 1 in both schemes" rests on an unrecorded deep scratch search. The gate certificate for δ\* mixed is 3.22.
