## What would falsify this

Named in the regression guard: a future high-precision measurement of the $(c,b,t)$ masses landing close enough to $A_{1g}=\tfrac19$ (or either of the other two candidates) that both the measurement-precision sigma drops below ~5 and the range-based look-elsewhere probability drops below ~$10^{-4}$ would flip this finding's characterization of the phase as "decisively excluded and not unusual." No such measurement exists today. There is no comparable sharp falsifier for the $(u,d,s)$ result, which is already a clean miss on every axis.
