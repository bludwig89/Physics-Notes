reduction, not the model's own BCC lattice or F110's gauge sector), is supported by its own
evidence: the fermionic construction is validated two independent ways to machine precision, the
declared control (`v2_control=True`) genuinely turns exactly the regime-separation checks red, and
the two reference constants (Poisson 0.3863, GOE 0.5307) were verified against live literature

signature; generic_V1V2 separates toward GOE and does) — and are recorded below so a later session
does not have to rediscover them.

## The claim, as the reviewer understands it

TARGET: whether a nearest-neighbour (V1) vs. a nearest+next-nearest-neighbour (V1+V2) interaction
on a quadratic lattice-fermion sector with the same conserved-charge structure as this project's
free sector shows the onset of eigenstate thermalisation (ETH), measured by three independent
diagnostics (level-spacing ratio, entanglement-plateau ceiling fraction, eigenstate-fluctuation
finite-size trend).
VALUE: `integrable_V1` stays statistically consistent with the Poisson value (0.9σ at L=14) and its
ETH-fluctuation diagnostic does not shrink with L (0.300→0.318); `generic_V1V2` separates from
Poisson by 20.6σ, sits 1.3σ from GOE, and its ETH-fluctuation diagnostic shrinks by nearly half
(0.284→0.148). Exactness class: quantitative throughout; two construction checks (fermionic sign,
parity block-diagonalisation) are exact/machine.
INPUTS: F300/F309's conserved-charge/GGE result (used as the motivating structure, not re-derived);
external RMT constants (Poisson/GOE mean r); standard Jordan-Wigner duality of the t-V model to XXZ
(used, not re-derived).
CLASS: QUANT (as claimed).
FORBIDDEN (not applicable to an inline review — noted per the contract, not enforced): none, since
no blind subagent was spawned.
