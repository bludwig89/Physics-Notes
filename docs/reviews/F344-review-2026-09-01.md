## Verdict

The mechanical content is confirmed twice over: the cold agent reproduced every number by a materially different parameterisation and got the same answer, including the counter-intuitive parts. Two narrowings were forced and **have been applied to the finding by the authoring session** (disclosed here rather than left as recommendations, because this was an attack-and-fix pass on the session's own finding, not a third-party review): the covariance group is criterion-dependent (**8 under unitary covariance, 16 once step-reversed covariance is admitted**), and the finding's original falsifier 2 pointed at a **retired** module. One genuine gap remains open: the primary paper's own isotropy group could not be retrieved.

Narrowed claim in one sentence: *within the Eq. 15 real-coefficient monomial form, the adopted BCC free-Weyl walk is exactly covariant under $D_{2h}$ (8 of 48) by unitary conjugation and under $D_{4h}$ (16 of 48) if rotation-with-step-reversal is admitted, never under any body-diagonal $C_3$ for any of the 576 unitary sign conventions, while the $O(k)$ truncation is covariant under all 48 — so $O_h$ is an exact infrared symmetry broken at $O(k^2)$ by a closed-form $T_{2g}$ defect.*

## Independent re-derivation

The cold agent worked in the same 8-monomial basis (that much is forced by the object) but parameterised the family differently — overall component signs $\sigma_a$ factored out, with $u = CCC + \delta\,SSS$, $n_x = \sigma_x(SCC + \alpha\,CSS)$ and cyclic, unitarity reducing to $\delta+\alpha+\beta+\gamma=0$ over 48 sign variants — rather than the finding's 576 branch-dependent assignments. It solved for $R$ in exact `Fraction`s and cross-checked at 400 random $k$ against the real `_bcc_uvec` (agreement $1.8\times10^{-15}$, identical verdicts on all $48\times2$ cases).

It also verified directly, at residual $5.7\times10^{-16}$, that the eight $\det R=-1$ elements realise $A(gk)=U A(k)^\dagger U^\dagger$ while $A(gk)=UA(k)U^\dagger$ fails at residual $1.97$ — the finding's $\varepsilon A^\dagger\varepsilon^{-1}$ characterisation, confirmed by an agent that had not read it.

**Assumptions it had to add that the claim card did not license** (both now written into the finding): $R$ and $U$ must be **$k$-independent**, or the question is vacuous, since $\omega=\arccos u$ *is* fully $O_h$-covariant and a pointwise-diagonalising $U$ exists trivially for all 48; and $u$ must transform too, which is what forces the branch map rather than leaving it a free knob. It got stuck nowhere.

Route grade: **independent** — different parameterisation, exact arithmetic, and it arrived at the 16-vs-8 distinction the finding had buried in a sub-leg.

## What would falsify this

The finding's own three falsifiers, unchanged in substance. The reviewer adds one: a $k$-**dependent** but smooth spin frame $S(k)$ under which the $C_3$ defect vanishes identically would not contradict any leg (every leg assumes $k$-independence, now stated) but would drain the result of physical content. Nobody has looked for one.

## Recommendations

Ranked by whether they change the verdict.

1. **Would change the verdict if it failed — check the five live consumers of the full walk** (falsifier 2). The $D_{2h}$ residual is invisible to $\omega$ and to $\lvert\mathbf n\rvert$ but not obviously to `dirac_bcc`, `blockspin`, `thermodynamics`, `induced_stiffness` or `chiral_core`. If an axis-dependent effect at relative $O(k)$ survives into any of them, F344 acquires a claim card and a bound; if all five are provably blind to it, F344 is a clean internal-structure finding and should say so.
2. **Would narrow the claim — retrieve Paper 1 §III** and confirm (a) which finite group $L$ BDPT actually use and (b) that Eq. 15's two solutions are complete modulo unitary conjugation. Falsifier 1 rests on (b).
3. **Would widen the theorem — settle the genuinely-complex-$\mathbf n$ case** (attack 2). If the rank-8 argument can be extended to $\bar{\mathbf n}\times\mathbf n\neq0$, the $C_3$ exclusion becomes unconditional within the monomial form and stops depending on an imported premise.
4. **Cosmetic — derive C8's slope bracket** instead of bracketing the observed $9.886$. The closed form predicts exactly 10 per decade; assert convergence to 10 rather than a window around it.
