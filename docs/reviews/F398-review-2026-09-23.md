The $L^{-2}$ scaling law is **analytically correct** — the same blind subagent independently derived exponent $-2$ by power counting, matching the finding's own reasoning — but the finding's original single-point numerical demonstration at $(L{=}10,L{=}100)$ was not robust: the referee's neighbour scan found the original band passed only 12 of 30 nearby anchor pairs, and no artifact in the repository backed the finding's claim that these two values had been "verified clean" beforehand. **Narrowed claim:** the identity is exact and the $L^{-2}$ law is real and independently confirmed, but its original single-point numerical demonstration was fragile; fixed in this same session (see "Fix applied" below) with a resonance-robust lower-envelope estimator, now verified stable across 30/30 nearby anchor pairs.

## Independent re-derivation

Subagent A (blind, no access to the forbidden files) derived, from the stated fermion algebra alone via Wick's theorem, cross-checked against an explicit 2-mode Fock-space example:

$$\|(a^\dagger)^2|0\rangle\|^2 = 2\left(1-\sum_p\psi(p)^4\right)$$

— an exact match to the finding's own Result 1, reached by the same general method (Wick contraction) but entirely independently (the agent reports one arithmetic slip on its first pass, caught by its own small-example check, which is itself a point in favor of the method's self-correcting rigor). It further derived the scaling exponent $-2$ by a bulk-vs-innermost-shell power-counting argument (unnormalized $\sum\psi^2\sim L^3$, bulk/density-of-states-dominated; unnormalized $\sum\psi^4\sim L^4$, dominated by the innermost nonzero grid shell because the continuum $\int d^3p/p^4$ integral itself diverges at $p\to0$) — the same argument the finding gives, reached independently, and confirmed by the agent's own separate numerical check (a synthetic isotropic $E_0=c|p|$ model on a plain cubic grid, not this repository's BCC dispersion, $L=8\ldots96$, log-log slopes converging to $3$, $4$, $-2$ to within 1%). It named the coboson/composite-boson literature (Combescot et al.) as consistent with, but not the source of, its derivation, and flagged one honest assumption (that $E_0$ has no additional near-zeros away from $p=0$) the problem statement did not fully specify.

**Reading:** same value, essentially the same general method (Wick's theorem / power counting) but a genuinely independent execution (different code, different concrete numerical model, no access to the finding) — this is the "reaches the same value by the same route... support, but the route may simply be forced" case from the governing protocol, strengthened by the agent's own numerical cross-check landing on a cleaner exponent (its synthetic model has no commensurability noise) than the finding's own noisier real-data measurement, which is itself a useful, unplanned cross-validation: it shows the finding's noisy empirical ratio ($\approx85$–$107$ depending on estimator) is consistent with, and now explained by, a clean independent derivation of exactly $-2$.
