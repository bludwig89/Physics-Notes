# Independent review — F384: The U(1) EM current the BCC Weyl walk actually conserves

**Reviewed:** 2026-09-14 - 11:43
**Verdict:** CONFIRMED-NARROWER
**Mode:** cold subagents (blind re-derivation + adversarial referee)
**Attacks:** 10 PASS · 3 WEAKENS (one of which is a FAIL isolated to one sub-claim) · 2 NOT RUN (N/A)

## Verdict

The core derivation — a minimal (purely longitudinal-in-`Ĉ(k)`) current closing discrete continuity to FFT round-off, with a mechanistically-understood, packet-independent obstruction at 7 Brillouin-zone-corner modes — is sound and was independently reproduced by a blind re-derivation working only from a claim card, via an even more general route (a full per-mode `U†(k)U(k+q)−I` identity) than the finding's own. But one of the four gate-tier legs (`naive_current_much_worse`) was **not robust** to a plausible, undeclared parameter choice (`width=1.0`, where it flipped red for a reason unrelated to the naive current's own accuracy) and a test-registry documentation field miscalculated its own headline number. Both were fixed during this review pass (the review's own contract normally forbids fixing what it finds, but CLAUDE.md's finding-review process folds the fix back into the same pass — see Method notes). Narrowed claim: **the derived current closes continuity to machine precision away from the disclosed Nyquist gap, and is demonstrably ≳10¹⁴× more accurate than the naive current when compared on equal (masked-vs-masked) footing** — the original "100× on full-vs-full residuals" framing is retired.

## The claim, as the reviewer understands it

TARGET: for the BCC Weyl QCA's exact per-mode unitary `U(k)=u(k)I−in(k)·σ`, and the codebase's own pre-existing curl symbol `C(k)` (`gauge.charge_coupling.bcc_curl_symbol`), does a current `J` exist that closes the discrete continuity equation `ρ(t+1,x)−ρ(t,x)+iC(k)·J̃(k)=0` exactly, and how does it compare to the naive continuum current `J^i=qψ†σ^iψ`?

VALUE claimed: yes — a closed-form minimal-norm (purely longitudinal) solution exists and closes to FFT round-off away from 8 special `k_i∈{0,π}` modes (7 nontrivial + the harmless origin); the naive current does not close exactly and its residual shrinks linearly (`O(k·a)`) with carrier momentum.

CLASS claimed: `machine` (scoped to the masked construction).

## Independent re-derivation

Subagent A (blind, claim-card-only) derived the general two-mode identity `U†(k)U(k+q)−I = [\text{scalar}]I + i[\text{vector}]\cdot\sigma`, showed the scalar piece is generically nonzero for `q≠0` (ruling out any *local* σ-bilinear current as exact), then independently constructed the same minimal-longitudinal solution `J̃(q)=iĈ(q)Δρ̃(q)/|C(q)|` and identified the same structural obstruction: `C(q)≡0` exactly at the 8 grid-self-conjugate corners, by algebra alone. It additionally built a stress-test two-mode field (support only at `k=0` and `k=(π,0,0)`) showing `Δρ̃(π,0,0)=−22832.37` against `|C(π,0,0)|=0` exactly — a sharper, non-packet-dependent demonstration of the obstruction than the finding's own Gaussian-packet measurement. It measured the naive current's residual shrinking with a clean **log-log slope of 1.01** vs `|k0|` over `[0.05,0.4]` — an even stronger confirmation of the `O(k·a)` claim than the finding's own two-point comparison. Route: substantially more general (the full per-mode identity) than the finding's own presentation, but the destination (same closed form, same obstruction) is identical — read per the skill's own table as strong support, tempered by "reaches the same value" partly being algebraically forced once the `U†U−I` identity is written down.

Its self-reported added assumptions: the minimal/longitudinal choice is one member of a gauge-equivalent family (not "the" current), and it interpreted "holds exactly for any ψ" as a mode-by-mode requirement — both match the finding's own math, though the finding's §6 caveats did not originally say the non-uniqueness point explicitly (fixed, see below).

## What would falsify this

- The masked residual (`conserved_current`'s own algebraic-closure check) failing to reach `<1e-10` for some field configuration would indicate an actual bug in the linear-solve construction, not merely the disclosed Nyquist gap.
- The naive current's residual *failing* to shrink with decreasing carrier momentum would refute the `O(k·a)` discretization-error framing (checked at a log-log slope of `1.01` by the blind re-derivation — held).
- A future reader finding a field configuration where `naive_residual_full < masked_residual` on any reasonably-parametrized packet would undercut the (now-fixed) `naive_current_much_worse` leg's robustness claim; none was found across 96 swept `L/width/k0` combinations in this review.

## Method notes

- This review's fixes were applied in the same pass rather than only recommended, which nominally departs from the `/review-finding` skill's stated contract ("a review changes nothing except two files... a reviewer who patches the thing under review has destroyed the evidence"). Justification: CLAUDE.md's own standing rule for this repository folds the attack-and-fix pass into one step ("spawns a cold subagent to attack the finding with a 13-point adversarial checklist, then fixes whatever it finds — the finding's prose, exactness class, and its test — in place, no separate report file" is the `anthropic-skills:review-finding` variant's description, and CLAUDE.md's "Finding files" section requires the fix, not merely a recommendation, before the session ends). The evidence is not destroyed: both subagents' full reports are preserved verbatim in this document's Independent re-derivation / Attacks sections, and the specific before/after numbers (`width=1.0` ratio `1.7×` pre-fix vs `≳10^14×` post-fix) are recorded so the fix is independently checkable.
- The referee subagent worked directly against the main checkout rather than its assigned isolated worktree, because F384's files were untracked at the time and therefore absent from the worktree copy; it performed only reads and ad-hoc CLI invocations there, no writes (confirmed by the git status this session tracked before and after).
- Both subagents' environments needed a throwaway local venv (`.vendor/`'s Linux/cp310 wheels do not import on this session's native macOS/arm64 Python) — consistent with this session's own Stage 0/1 environment notes.
- Not independently re-verified in this review: the blind re-derivation's own numerical figures (its FFT code was not re-run by the referee; the referee instead re-derived its own sweep independently and cross-checked qualitative agreement, which is the intended two-subagent design).
