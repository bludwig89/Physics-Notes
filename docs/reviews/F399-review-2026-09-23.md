## Independent re-derivation

Subagent A (blind, no access to the forbidden files) derived, from the stated NJL Lagrangian and bubble formulas alone:

1. The residue-coupling formula $g_{Mqq}^2=[d\Pi_M/dq^2|_{m_M^2}]^{-1}$, resolving a sign ambiguity in the naive expansion by requiring consistency with the exact Goldberger–Treiman identity (flagged honestly as a place it needed an external check rather than a derivation from the given formulas alone).
2. Applied to the pion: $g_{\pi qq}^2=1/(2N_cN_fK(0))$, and — independently — the *exact* algebraic identity $g_{\pi qq}f_\pi=M$ for any $G,\Lambda$ (all $K(0)$-dependence cancels).
3. Applied to the scalar: found, at $q^2=4M^2$, that $4E_p^2-4M^2=4p^2$ holds **identically for every $p$** (not just asymptotically as $p\to0$, which is as far as the finding's original version reasoned), giving the closed form $K(4M^2)=\frac{1}{8\pi^2}\ln[(\Lambda+\sqrt{\Lambda^2+M^2})/M]$ — a genuinely better result than the finding had.
4. Confirmed the scalar's analogous ratio is **not** universal: $(g_{\sigma qq}f_\pi/M)^2=K(0)/K(4M^2)=1-S/L$ (with $S,L$ closed-form functions of $M/\Lambda$), numerically falling from $\sim0.85$ to $\sim0.18$ over its own sweep.
5. Derived the two-flavor generalization independently: the polarization matrix $\Pi_{ab}$ is **exactly diagonal always** (a pure field-content/Wick-theorem fact, true for any $G_{ab}$, not merely $G_{ab}=0$), while the full propagator matrix $D=2G[\mathbb1-2G\Pi]^{-1}$ stays exactly diagonal only when $G_{ab}=0$ too — clarifying precisely where a cross-species vertex would have to enter (the coupling-matrix inversion, not the loop integral).

**Reading:** independent derivation, same method (standard NJL bosonization) but reached without seeing the finding, and it delivered a strictly better result (the exact closed form for $K(4M^2)$, valid at every $p$, not just $p\to0$) than the finding's original quadrature-only treatment — the strongest form of confirmation this protocol looks for, and the direct source of this session's fix.
