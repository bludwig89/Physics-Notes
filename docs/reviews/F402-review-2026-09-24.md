Subagent A worked from a task card that named no mechanism. It reproduced: $\sigma_N=46.2$ MeV (4.95% of $M_N$); the dilatation terms $+0.04951,\,+6.45459,\,-5.50410$ (identical to six digits); Cornell $\langle T\rangle=2.54$, $\langle V_\text{conf}\rangle=4.15$, $\langle V_\text{coul}\rangle=-0.947$, $M=8.09$, quark mass term $3m-\langle T\rangle=-0.184$ ($-2.3\%$ of $M$), virial to $0.3$–$0.5\%$; static-string gluon fraction $0.19$ (with the string trace) or $0.53$ (naive). Route: the same standard derivations (forced route), except the gluon-fraction lower-scale coupling, where it made a different assumption and still found the same $\approx2\times$ deficit.
