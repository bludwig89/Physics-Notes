## Verdict

The core result survives: up-type $(u,c,t)$ is a clean, decisive miss against every single-irrep $O_h$ weight, and down-type $(d,s,b)$'s nominal proximity to $A_{1g}=1/9$ does not support adopting that weight. But the finding's *first-written* statistical justification for "not significant" was wrong in a way Attack 7 (a perturbation sweep) exposed directly: it used the measurement-precision $\sigma$ from the PDG-uncertainty Monte Carlo to answer a look-elsewhere ("is this coincidence common") question that $\sigma$ cannot answer — a sufficiently precise *future* measurement landing exactly on the candidate would have been reported as "not significant" by that formula, which is backwards. **Narrowed claim:** down-type's proximity to $1/9$ is not excluded by current *data* (a measurement-compatibility statement, correctly computed) and is a real but modest (not overwhelming, not negligible) numerical coincidence under a properly-scaled range-based null (corrected from a first-draft $p\approx0.62$ down to $p\approx0.0057$ for "at least one of the two sectors this close by chance") — not the stronger, wrong claim that it is "not significant… more likely than not by chance" the first draft made. The "flagged, not adopted" verdict is unchanged, but now rests on the two reasons that never depended on the broken number: no independent theoretical motivation for $A_{1g}$ in the down-type sector, and no matching behaviour in up-type.

## Independent re-derivation

A fresh `general-purpose` subagent (id `a7a17a2dd6342a827`) was given the claim card above plus permission to read F175, F92, F80 and the PDG source, with the forbidden list enforced, and asked to derive the fitted shape angle for both quark sectors independently. It used direct algebra (Cramer's-rule-style solution of the 3-equation circulant system) rather than the authoring module's DFT-based `fit_circulant`, and searched PDG independently for the same 2024 masses.

Its numbers: up-type $\delta\bmod\tfrac{2\pi}{3}=0.074517$ rad (this review's own module: $0.0745169$), down-type $0.110119$ rad (this review's own module: $0.1101187$) -- matching to 5-6 significant figures by a different numerical route. It independently flagged up-type as "nowhere near any of the three weights" and down-type as "closest to $1/9$, within roughly $1$-$1.5\sigma$ of the propagated PDG uncertainty," without being told the candidate set was restricted to single-irrep weights or which one was nearest. It did not attempt a look-elsewhere calculation (out of scope for the claim card as written) and correctly declined to call the down-type proximity either "confirmed" or "refuted" on its own.

**Reading:** different route, same value -- the strongest support category in the review's own grading table.

## What would falsify this

A future measurement moving the down-type triplet's fitted $\delta\bmod\tfrac{2\pi}3$ decisively *away* from $1/9$ (already weak evidence, so this mostly just closes the "flagged" note rather than falsifying a claim) would remove even the modest coincidence. Conversely, nothing in this finding is falsified by a measurement moving *toward* $1/9$, since it is explicitly not claiming that value's significance is dismissible -- see the narrowed claim above. The finding as narrowed has no single sharp falsifier of its own; it is a leaning no-go, and its falsification is "someone finds a working quark-sector shape mechanism," which is out of scope here by design (§section 7 of the finding).

## Recommendations

Ranked by whether they would change the verdict:

1. **(would change the verdict if it worked)** Test the Harari-et-al.-style *mixed-generation-type* grouping (e.g. $(u,d,s)$ or another cross-type triplet) against the same three single-irrep weights, using the identical circulant/Monte Carlo pipeline. This is a concrete, sourced, previously-unexplored avenue this review surfaced (Attack 11) and is a natural, cheap follow-up to F346 -- but it is a *different* finding (a different candidate grouping), not a fix to this one, and should be its own claim/finding number if attempted.
2. **(would not change the verdict, but tightens the record)** If a future session wants to defend the $A_{1g}$ down-type proximity as more than "flagged," it needs an independent theoretical motivation for that specific weight in the down-type sector (paralleling F80's charge/colour selection story for the lepton's $E_g$) -- not a smaller $p$-value, since C4 already shows the coincidence is modest rather than negligible.
3. **(cosmetic)** Tighten Attack 4's tolerance-shopping note by pre-registering the $\sigma$-bucket boundaries (e.g. via a `MeasuredConstant`-style documented threshold) rather than leaving them as bare literals in the check function, if this module is extended further.
