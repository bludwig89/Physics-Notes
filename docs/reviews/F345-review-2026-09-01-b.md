## Where the two agents disagreed

Recorded because the disagreement is informative, and the referee broke part of the blind agent's case:

- **On (d)'s worst case.** The referee sustains that "$\le1.4\times10^{-76}$" is not a correct upper bound, and reproduces the $\sqrt{12}=3.464$ factor exactly. But it **breaks** the blind agent's choice of worst case: in 4D vacuum the four-derivative sector is Gauss–Bonnet (topological, by F345's own L3) plus $R^2$ and $R_{\mu\nu}R^{\mu\nu}$, which are **removable by the standard EFT field redefinition** $g_{\mu\nu}\to g_{\mu\nu}+\alpha R_{\mu\nu}+\beta Rg_{\mu\nu}$. So the leading genuine correction at a *vacuum* horizon is six-derivative, $\sim(a/L)^4\sim10^{-152}$, and the regimes where four-derivative operators genuinely contribute are the **matter** ones — where the module's own worst row is the NS surface at $5.1\times10^{-77}$, *below* the quoted bound. Both the author and the blind agent were computing a proxy. The referee flags its own field-redefinition sub-point as **asserted from standard EFT lore, with no confirming citation landed in-session**.
- **On $f(R)$.** The referee **rejects the blind agent's premise** that F319 puts curvature operators in the theory with lattice-fixed coefficients — "H3 over-reads F319 in precisely the same way F345 does, and I do not accept it." It also rejects the wording complaint (L6 never claims $f=R$ exactly). It **sustains** H3's real content: neither family is *refuted* — a BD with $\omega=10^{40}$ is observationally identical — so the result is an absence-of-a-dial, and "a different and stronger thing than being disfavoured by a bound" is the wrong comparative: it is *different*, not *stronger*.
- **On the 4D Einstein–Gauss–Bonnet loophole** the blind agent left open, the referee **closed it in F345's favour**: the $\alpha\to\alpha/(D-4)$ rescaling is not a genuine four-dimensional metric theory (Gürses–Şişman–Tekin; Shu arXiv:2004.09339), and its surviving regularizations yield scalar-tensor Horndeski — an extra field, excluded by F345's metric-only premise. **PASS for F345**, and it should cite this because a reviewer will ask.
