## The claim, as the reviewer understands it

Claim card handed to the blind agent (no repo access at all — this target needs none):

```
TARGET   Given the 2D-square QCA Dirac dispersion cos ω(k) = √(1−m²)·cos(ka) with a = 1/√2 = c_lat,
         and the definitions ω_static = ω(0), v_g = dω/dk, ω_moving = ω − k·v_g, R = ω_moving/ω_static,
         β = v_g/c_lat: expand R(β) − √(1−β²) = B(m)β² + C(m)β⁴ + D(m)β⁶ + O(β⁸).
         Derive B(m) in closed form; determine its sign on m ∈ (0,1); give its small-m expansion.
VALUE    a closed-form elementary function of m; sign constant on the interval
INPUTS   the dispersion relation and the four definitions above. Nothing else.
CLASS    EXACT (algebraic, no fitted constants) — as claimed
FORBIDDEN  the entire Physics Notes repo, plus any external source stating a closed form
```

The card is non-leading: it contains the setup and the definitions (without which the target is not
well-posed — $R$ is a bespoke construction) but no mechanism, no series coefficients, no answer. It
is recoverable from Finding 12's own open item *"does not derive $\beta_\text{LV}$ analytically"*,
which is the sanity test the procedure requires.

## Independent re-derivation

The blind agent derived the result **by a different route than the finding** and reached algebraic
identity on all three coefficients.

| | Finding 15 | Blind agent | Agreement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Route | implicit differentiation of $\cos\omega = n\cos u$, term-by-term series, series reversion $u(\beta)$ | exact change of variable $t=\cos u$ giving a **non-perturbative closed form** for $R(\beta)$, then a generating ODE $dR/d\beta = -\tfrac1\theta\arcsin(\beta\gamma\tan\theta)$; brute-force reversion run separately as a cross-check | different, and both done |
| $\beta_\text{LV}$ | $\tfrac12\!\left(1-\dfrac{m}{\sqrt{1-m^2}\arcsin m}\right)$ | $\tfrac12\!\left(1-\dfrac{\tan\theta}{\theta}\right),\ \theta=\arcsin m$ | **identical** |
| $\gamma_\text{LV}$ | $\tfrac18-\dfrac{m(3-2m^2)}{24(1-m^2)^{3/2}\arcsin m}$ | $\tfrac18-\tfrac1\theta\!\left(\tfrac{T}{8}+\tfrac{T^3}{24}\right),\ T=\tan\theta$ | **identical** (verified symbolically) |
| $\delta_\text{LV}$ | $\tfrac1{16}-\dfrac{m(8m^4-20m^2+15)}{240(1-m^2)^{5/2}\arcsin m}$ | $\tfrac1{16}-\tfrac1\theta\!\left(\tfrac{T}{16}+\tfrac{T^3}{24}+\tfrac{T^5}{80}\right)$ | **identical** |
| Sign | $<0$, via $\arcsin m < m/\sqrt{1-m^2}$ | $<0$, via $\tan\theta>\theta$ (same inequality, independently found) | agree |
| Small-$m$ | $-\tfrac{m^2}{6}-\tfrac{11m^4}{90}$ | $-\tfrac{m^2}{6}-\tfrac{11m^4}{90}-\tfrac{191m^6}{1890}-\dots$ | agree, extended |

All fifteen entries of the finding's tabulated-values table reproduce from the independent closed
forms to the quoted digits (orchestrator check, `mpmath`/float64).

**This is the strongest outcome the procedure can produce: same value, different route.** No attack
dented the derivation.

### What the blind agent found that the finding does not contain

These are not corrections — they are gaps the cold view exposed:

1. **It closes the finding's own stated open item.** Finding 15 §"What this does not close" says
   *"A separate calculation would confirm whether $\gamma_\text{LV}$ ever flips sign as $m\to1$."*
   It never does: with $T/\theta>1$, every term of $\gamma_\text{LV}$ and $\delta_\text{LV}$ is
   negative on all of $(0,1)$. Confirmed numerically — $\gamma_\text{LV}(0.999)=-306$,
   $\delta_\text{LV}(0.999)=-4.6\times10^4$, no flip anywhere.
2. **A non-perturbative closed form exists**, stronger than the series:
   $R(\beta) = \left[\arcsin(m\gamma)-\beta\arcsin(\beta\gamma\tan\theta)\right]/\arcsin m$, exact,
   no expansion. The finding only ever produced the truncated series.
3. **$a=1/\sqrt2$ is decorative.** $\omega$ depends on $k$ only through $u=ka$ and $c_\text{lat}=a$,
   so $a$ cancels identically in both $R$ and $\beta$ — before any expansion. The finding lists
   $a=1/\sqrt2=c_\text{lat}$ as a setup input as though it enters; it does not, at any order.
4. **The expansion domain is $\beta\in[0,\sqrt{1-m^2})$, not $[0,1)$.** $\beta$ never reaches 1.
   Unstated in the finding.
5. **$\beta_\text{LV}\to-\infty$ as $m\to1$** while the physical effect $|\beta_\text{LV}|\beta_{\max}^2\to0$.
   Quoting the coefficient alone near $m=1$ is misleading; the finding's table stops at $m=0.5$ and
   never flags this.
6. **The load-bearing unstated choice:** expanding in $\beta$ rather than in $ka$ is an
   $m$-dependent reparametrisation ($\beta\simeq(n/m)ka$) and yields entirely different
   coefficients. And $R=\omega_\text{moving}/\omega_\text{static}$ is *asserted* to be the lattice
   analogue of $1/\gamma$ — it is a definition, never derived to be the rate of a moving clock, and
   rival Legendre-type constructions disagree beyond leading order. The physical interpretation of
   $\beta_\text{LV}$ rests entirely on that unargued choice.

## What would falsify this

For **Finding 15's mathematics**: nothing experimental — it is a theorem about an assumed dispersion.
It is falsified only by an algebra error, and two independent routes plus 60-digit numerics now say
there is none. The honest status is *exact algebraic result, not a physical prediction*.

For **the physical claim built on it**: the finding's own framing makes it unfalsifiable — the effect
is $\propto m^2$ in lattice units and vanishes in the massless limit, so no named experiment has a
reachable threshold. It appears in no entry of `papers/Claims-and-Falsifiers-Summary.md`. This is a
defect in the claim's *status*, not a strength.

The one genuinely falsifiable descendant is the **3D-BCC analog**, which does not exist yet. Since
BCC is the canonical lattice (D1) and the square lattice is explicitly *"reference implementation,
not canonical"*, the currently derived result applies to a non-canonical geometry.

## On the verdict

`UNDER-EVIDENCED` is a verdict on **the artifact**, and the split is sharp enough that flattening it
into one word would be dishonest:

- **The physics is right.** A cold agent re-derived all three closed forms by a different route and
  got algebraic identity; every tabulated value reproduces; at 60 digits the series is accurate to
  $5\times10^{-16}$ — better than the finding claims. Thirteen attacks did not dent the derivation.
  On its own, Finding 15 would grade `CONFIRMED` (narrowing to: 2D-square lattice, $m\in(0,1)$,
  physically unobservable).
- **The artifact is not sound.** The result carries **zero test coverage** while its sole
  implementation is registered `dead_candidate` and backs four "exact algebraic" ledger rows. The
  advertised `4/4 PASS \| 3 test rec` is a regex collision. The finding misdiagnoses its own
  numerical floor, quotes an LIV bound eight orders off for the wrong LIV order when the correct
  value sits in the same file, quotes an anomalous-moment precision as a time-dilation precision, and
  routes its SI conversion through an identification the project replaced in July.
- **And the container defeats the repo's own machinery.** Four superseded findings, three stale
  claims and one hard numeric drift sit inside it where the supersession ledger, the coverage join,
  the drift checker and the duplicate-number check cannot see them — because all four key on the
  file's number, and the file's number is F1.

The single most valuable thing this review surfaced is not a physics error. It is that **a
correct, exact, independently reproduced result has been sitting in the repo with no test record,
credited with someone else's passing tests.**
