## What the blind agent found, independently

Its central, most consequential finding: testing beam axis (`axis=1,2`, not just the finding's own default `axis=0`) and a slightly larger carrier wavenumber (`m_index=4`, not the finding's tested `m_index≤3`) breaks both the direction-alignment claim and the anti-parallel result — the correlation with the beam's own `k̂` collapses to near-zero off-axis and *reverses sign* at `m_index=4`, and (independently, a related but distinct measurement) the field/matter anti-parallel relationship does not merely weaken off-axis, it *inverts to near-parallel*. It also independently found: `g_lat` controls only the field-sourcing half of the loop (the matter push is set by `q`/beam amplitude, invariant under `g_lat` to sub-percent precision); the conservation residual's apparent "sweet spot" near `g_lat≈0.6` is a crossing point between a flat `|ΔP_matter|` curve and a linearly-growing `|ΔP_field|` curve, not evidence of approaching conservation; and claim 3 (`Δp=ΔE/c`) is not well-posed because the field's tracked energy is *pumped* by the sourcing term rather than *drained* by absorption from the beam.

## What the referee added

The referee reproduced every one of the blind agent's numeric claims to the digit against the real repo code (not a reimplementation), then went further on the direction-claim gap specifically: it found the on-axis sign flip occurs at `m_index=4` — not a remote Brillouin-zone-edge effect (`L=16`'s true edge is `m_index=8`) but simply double the finding's own default, well inside any reasonable reading of "swept." It also found the anti-parallel result's off-axis behavior is worse than the blind report characterized: sweeping `axis`/`m_index` more broadly, `cos(ΔP_matter,ΔP_field)` ranges across the *entire* interval from `-1` to `+1`, landing at exactly `+1.0` for several configurations — not merely "breaking down," but inverting outright. The referee traced the likely mechanism to `findings/F387-curl-anisotropy-omega-pair-mismatch.md` §1's already-documented `Ĉ(k)→(k_x,-k_y,k_z)/|k|` axis asymmetry, combined with the beam-construction helper's Euclidean-`k̂` (not `Ĉ(k)`) polarization convention (F386 §6) — both pre-existing, independently-established facts the finding had not connected to this specific failure mode. The referee explicitly noted that CL307's own stated falsifier ("anti-alignment breaks down badly under a wider sweep") was met by data the referee itself produced during the review, using entirely ordinary parameter values.

The referee's line-by-line code check and 13-point checklist found no bugs in `photon_fermion_push.py` or the `beam_amp` seeding addition to `coupled.py`, confirmed both declared controls reproduce exactly what `tests/registry/core.yaml` claims, and confirmed the scenario-YAML seeding path and the harness's direct-state-injection path are bit-for-bit identical.
