## Verdict

The **s = 2 result is correct and was independently re-derived by a different route** — the blind
agent reached $d_\text{time}=1$, $\ker(\det)\cong\mathbb Z$ generated by $A$, and the scalar part
$=U(1)\times\mathbb Z^3$, via a Newton-polytope/support-function descent rather than by citing a
Pell/unit theorem. That is the strongest form of support this command can produce, and it is a
real result.

What is overstated is everything wrapped around it. §8's boxed identity
$(d_\text{space},d_\text{time})=(\dim\mathfrak{su}(s),\operatorname{rank}\mathfrak{su}(s))$ is a
**coincidence of small numbers at $s=2$**, contradicted by the model's own $s=4$ cell; §5's claim
that $d_\text{space}=3$ is "recovered from the commutant rather than imported into it" is
circular (the $\mathbb Z^3$ **is** the three Laurent variables that were put in); and the rank-1
step is attributed to the wrong theorem in the wrong ring.

**Narrowed claim.** *For an $s=2$ QCA on an infinite $\mathbb Z^3$ Cayley graph, the
local-homogeneous commutant of the BCC Weyl update splits under $\det$ into exactly the monomial
shift group and an infinite-cyclic Pell group generated by the update, so the update is the unique
flow up to shifts and powers — $d_\text{time}=1$ at the minimal cell — but the
$(\dim\mathfrak{su}(s),\operatorname{rank}\mathfrak{su}(s))$ identity, the claim that
$d_\text{space}=3$ is recovered rather than assumed, and the attribution of the rank-1 step to
Abel, must all be withdrawn.*

## Independent re-derivation

Subagent A read Paper 1 Eq. 15 itself, cross-checked the sign-corrected $n_y$ against
`lattice.bcc._bcc_uvec`, and confirmed it read none of the forbidden files.

**It reached the same values by a different route.** Where F313 sets up the Pell equation
$a^2-b^2N=1$ and imports the unit theorem, A proved the descent directly with a Newton-polytope
argument: $\operatorname{Newt}(u)$ is the full-dimensional centrally-symmetric cube
$\mathrm{conv}\{(\pm1,\pm1,\pm1)\}$, so its support function is strictly positive in every
direction; unitarity forces $p^*=p$, hence centrally symmetric support, hence $0\in
\operatorname{int}\operatorname{Newt}(p)$; the leading forms then factor as
$\mathrm{LF}_a(p)=\sigma(a)\mathrm{LF}_a(u)\mathrm{LF}_a(\lambda)$ with $\sigma$ globally constant
by connectivity of the normal fan's dual graph; and the width
$\sum_a(h_N(a)+h_N(-a))$ strictly drops each step, terminating at $\lambda=0$.

**What A had to add that the claim card did not license** — and this is the most valuable output
of the blind run:

1. **Infinite volume.** Load-bearing and nowhere stated in F313. On a finite periodic $L^3$
   lattice $\mathbb C[\Lambda/L\Lambda]$ is not a domain, *every* homogeneous operator is
   trivially local, units are not monomials, and the commutant unitary group becomes a torus of
   rank $\sim2\lvert\mathrm{BZ}\rvert$. C3 and C4 both collapse. $d_\text{time}=1$ is an
   infinite-volume statement.
2. **Homogeneity is with respect to $\Lambda$**, the automaton's own BCC group, not the ambient
   $\mathbb Z^3$.
3. **Unitarity, not just $\det=1$.** A's descent genuinely needs $p^*=p$; $\det=1$ alone is
   insufficient. Dropping unitarity leaves a rank-2 *module* and the $d_\text{time}$ question has
   no answer.

**A's own control, which F313 does not have.** In $d=1$ the theorem is **false**:
$\tilde{\mathbf n}=(0,0,\sin k)$, $\gcd=\sin k\neq$ unit, the commutant is *all* diagonal Laurent
matrices, $N=\sin^2 k$ is a perfect square so the extension splits, and
$A=\mathrm{diag}(z^{-1},z)$ is the **square** of $\mathrm{diag}(z^{-1},1)$ times a shift — the tick
is not primitive. The referee reproduced both failures. This is the single strongest piece of
evidence in the review that the $3$D result is not a tautology, and it also *vindicates* F313 §6's
reading of a split $N$: the 1D walk really is two automata (left- and right-movers), each with its
own clock.

**A's verdict on §8, which the referee then adjudicated in A's favour on all three sub-arguments:**
the $(\dim\mathfrak{su}(s),\operatorname{rank}\mathfrak{su}(s))$ framing "reads as a mnemonic
presented as a derivation."

## What would falsify this

Of F313's six falsifiers, **one** is a real two-sided computation (F5: does $V=\mathbb I\otimes A$
survive the model's interactions). The review's own additions, which *are* two-sided:

- **Prove or refute the polynomial→Laurent transfer of Dubickas–Steuding Thm 2.** The blind agent's
  two-sided degree $D(f)=\deg_\lambda f+\deg_{-\lambda}f$ (with $D(f)=0\iff f$ monomial $\iff f$ a
  unit of $R$) is the exact replacement for D–S's "deg 0 ⟹ constant". If it goes through, CL269's
  contingency disappears; if it does not, the headline is unproved.
- **The finite-$L$ statement.** At what $L$, if any, does a rank $>1$ commutant appear, and is the
  infinite-volume limit approached or is it a discontinuity? This is a computation, not an appeal.
- **$d_\text{time}$ at $s=4$ measured rather than asserted.** $2$, $1$ and $\operatorname{rank}
  \mathfrak{su}(4)=3$ are three different answers and the finding's §8 predicts the third.
