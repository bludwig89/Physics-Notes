## Verdict

The exact results hold: the trace identity (P1), the requirement of a $T_{1g}$ component for CP violation (P6), and the Schur–Horn exclusion of a universal δ\* = 2/9 for all-positive down amplitudes (P3). Two quantitative statements were wrong. First, the all-positive "smallest Koide violation" of $1.9\times10^{-3}$ was a penalty-weight artifact: at that point $\lvert V_{cb}\rvert=0.124$. With the CKM held, the minimum is $2.1\times10^{-2}$, so the no-go is about 10× stronger than stated. Second, "every angle pair fits" is false: the signed route fits only in a band $-0.05\lesssim\delta_D-\delta_U\lesssim0.1$.

**Narrowed claim.** With all-positive amplitudes, exact Koide pseudo-diagonals plus the measured CKM fail by about $2\times10^{-2}$ of tr S. With $-\sqrt{m_d}$, they fit at the unitary floor inside a band of correlated E_g angles (which includes universal δ\*). So the route predicts no CKM observable, and its only output is an angle correlation.

## The claim, as the reviewer understands it

TARGET: in a Hermitian-amplitude construction ($M_f=S_f^2$, $V=U_U^\dagger U_D$, shared basis), does exact Koide on the diagonal of $S_f$ in both quark sectors fit the measured CKM? VALUE: the identity $Q_\text{phys}=2/3+\lVert S_\text{off}\rVert_F^2/(\operatorname{tr}S)^2$ (0.427 up, 0.254 down); all positive → no, with min violation $1.9\times10^{-3}$; δ ≤ 0.180 for down all positive; $-\sqrt{m_d}$ → χ² = 0.119 = unitary floor for every pair; real S ⇒ J = 0. CLASS: P1/P6 exact; P3 exact criterion; P4/P7 numerical. The finding's own wording "for every angle pair tested" was literally true; the widening to "every admissible pair" happened in CL316 and the inventory.

## Independent re-derivation

Subagent A read no forbidden files. It worked from PDG MSbar masses and PDG 2024 global-fit CKM values, and reproduced:

- **T1:** 0.4268 / 0.2542 (finding: 0.427 / 0.254). Exact, via $\operatorname{tr}S^2$ split into diagonal and off-diagonal parts.
- **T3:** δ ≤ 0.1800 (finding: 0.180), via majorization.
- **T5:** J = 0 for real symmetric S. A adds that the precise condition is that no joint rephasing makes both matrices real.
- **T2:** the verdict NO agrees, but A's minimum is ≈ $2\times10^{-2}$ of tr S (balanced ε_U = +0.019, ε_D = −0.026), 10× the finding's number.
- **T4:** exact Koide reached at the floor for all tested pairs, agreeing structurally. A's floor was 0.0006 against the finding's 0.119, because the inputs differ.

A's route is independent: a Schur–Horn/majorization argument for T3, and `least_squares` on (ε_U, ε_D) with V fixed for T2/T4. The assumptions it had to add were the χ² definition, the mass scheme, the CKM input set, and the violation metric.

## What would falsify this

The P4a no-go falls if a better optimiser finds exact Koide in both all-positive sectors with CKM χ² ≤ 1: the claim is a demonstration, not a proof. The band result falls if an exact-Koide, CKM-floor solution turns up at (0.24, 0.01). No experiment tests the route, because it reproduces the CKM at the unitary floor.
