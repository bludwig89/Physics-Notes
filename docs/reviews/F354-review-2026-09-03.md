## Verdict

The finding's **central mechanism claim is false**. "The BCC site group contains inversion, therefore
the core density is even in `r`, therefore the metric function is even" does not follow: inversion
symmetry of a configuration on R^3 and evenness of `f` under the formal substitution `r -> -r` are
different properties, and the finding's own headline counterexample separates them. What survives is
a genuinely worthwhile but differently-founded result, plus a recorded no-go and two flagged
inconsistencies in prerequisite findings.

**Narrowed claim.** *The curvature bound alone — with no parity, no point-group input and no
saturation — forces a `C^{1,1}` Lorentzian regular centre with no solid-angle defect, in the full
two-function class; radial and null geodesics therefore pass through the centre rather than
terminating. Upgrading that to the analytic evenness Zhou-Modesto require needs **analyticity of the
coarse-grained core density at the centre**, which the BCC point group does **not** supply. The
associated scales are contingent on a saturation postulate, a curvature-ceiling convention and a
profile choice, and move by factors of 1.7-4.9 under defensible alternatives.*

## The kill

Hayward's density is

    rho_Hay(x) = 3 M L^3 / (4 pi (L^3 + |x|^3)^2)
               = 3M/(4 pi L^3)  -  (3M / (2 pi L^6)) r^3  +  O(r^6)

It is bounded, positive, monotone, and **spherically symmetric — hence invariant under all of O(3),
hence under O_h, hence under -1**. Yet it carries an **odd r^3 term**, `m(r)` is not odd, `f` is not
even, and Zhou-Modesto show the spacetime is geodesically incomplete. So

> **"rho is O_h-invariant => rho is even in r" is FALSE.**

The finding's check B1 establishes the implication only for **polynomials**. But
`r^(2k+1) = (x^2+y^2+z^2)^((2k+1)/2)` is O_h-invariant, non-polynomial, and odd in `r`. Check B2 then
*assumes* the polynomial form `rho = rho0 + rho2 r^2 + rho4 r^4` and verifies that `m` comes out odd
— which is true by ansatz. The gap between B1 and B2 is exactly where the counterexample lives.

The load-bearing hypothesis is therefore **smoothness/analyticity of the coarse-grained density at
the centre in Cartesian coordinates** — which is precisely what is unavailable over a ~2.2-cell
region — and once granted, spherical symmetry alone suffices (Whitney), so O_h is redundant in the
isotropic channel.

**The pincer closes from the other side too.** The declared `point_group=Td` control is physically
vacuous: the T_d invariant ring is `R[r^2, xyz, x^4+y^4+z^4]`, so every odd-degree T_d invariant
carries the factor `xyz`, whose spherical average vanishes identically. The Misner-Sharp `m(r)` is
therefore unchanged, `f` stays even, and a diamond/zincblende lattice gives the **same** regular
centre in the monopole channel. The registry `reason:` asserts the opposite. B1 reddens only on the
`inversion_in_group` bookkeeping flag.

So inversion is **neither sufficient** (Hayward) **nor necessary** (T_d) for the conclusion it is
credited with. This is a clean no-go on the elegant route, and recording it as such is the honest
salvage.

## What survives, and is stronger than what was claimed

Agent A's independent route produced a better argument than the finding's. For the general
**two-function** metric `ds^2 = -A dt^2 + dr^2/B + r^2 dOmega^2`, the Kretschmann scalar is a
**sum of squares** of orthonormal-frame Riemann components:

    K = 4 R_trtr^2 + 8 R_tththt^2 + 8 R_rthrth^2 + 4 R_thphthph^2
    R_thphthph = (1-B)/r^2 ,  R_rthrth = -B'/(2r) ,  R_tththt = B A'/(2 r A)

A global bound `K <= 1/a^4` therefore bounds **each component separately**, forcing

| bound | consequence |
|---|---|
| \|1-B\|/r^2 <= a^-2 / 2 | `B(0)=1` — **no mass defect, no solid-angle deficit** |
| \|B'\|/(2r) bounded | `B'(0)=0` |
| B\|A'\|/(2rA) bounded | `A(0)` finite and nonzero, `A'(0)=0` |

so the metric is `eta_{mu nu} + O(r^2)` in Cartesian coordinates: **`C^{1,1}`, Lorentzian, no defect —
the centre is a manifold interior point, not a boundary.** At `C^{1,1}` geodesics exist and are
unique (Chruściel-Grant), so the extension through the centre is unique and radial/null geodesics
pass through it.

This uses **only the curvature bound**. It needs no parity, no point group, no saturation, no
one-function assumption, and it holds in the two-function class F178 says the interior actually
requires — where the finding's Bardeen representative (`AB = 1`) does not belong. In the one-function
case it collapses to the identity the module already uses,
`K = (f'')^2 + 4(f'/r)^2 + 4((1-f)/r^2)^2`.

**This is the result that closes E10**, and the finding should be rebuilt on it.

## Recommendations

   needs none of the contested inputs, and holds in the two-function class. This is the E10 result.
2. **Record the O_h route as a no-go**, with the Hayward counterexample and the T_d monopole
   argument as its two legs. A closed elegant route is a first-class result in this repo's idiom and
   should not be quietly dropped.
3. **State analyticity of the coarse-grained core density as the real hypothesis**, and make
   falsifier 1 computable: *measure the `r^3` coefficient of the coarse-grained rho from a CA run.*
4. **Reclassify CL295**: `exactness: exact -> contingent`, `tier: headline -> supporting`, drop
   "forced by inversion symmetry" from the title, add the maximal-extension scope boundary.
5. **Adjudicate the curvature-ceiling fork.** F183 uses `K_max = 1/a^4`; F284's `H_max = 1/a`
   implies `K_max = 24/a^4`. Only one can be fundamental, and they differ by `24^(1/4)` in length.
   Under the F284-consistent convention the core radius is `L = a` **exactly** and the e-folding is
   `sqrt(3)` ticks = **exactly one cell crossing = F284's own `t_min`** — which is markedly more
   elegant and may well be the right answer. This deserves its own session and its own decision.
6. **Reconcile the F107/F284 tick duration.** F107: `tick = a/(c sqrt3) = 2.054e-43 s`. F284's SI
   `t_min = 6.161e-43 s` with `t_min = sqrt(3)` ticks implies `tick = a/c = 3.557e-43 s`. These
   differ by `sqrt(3)`. F354's `6^(3/4)` uses only the dimensionless statement and is unaffected,
   but one of the two SI numbers is wrong.
7. **Fix the three D7 violations** (`MSUN_KG`, `M_PLANCK_KG`, `_c_lat_sym`).
8. **Retire the T_d control**; replace with one that perturbs a computed quantity.
9. **Move A1 out of the pass count** — it is a structural declaration, not a check.
10. **Fix the covering radius** `a/4 -> sqrt(3) a/4`, and address Wyckoff 8c/12d.
11. **Cite Antonelli & Sebastianutti arXiv:2509.15477.**
12. **Record `M_ext = (3 sqrt3/4) L ~ 19 m_Pl`**: below it there is no horizon at all, so sub-19-Planck-mass
    black holes do not exist in this model. `M_crit = 0.673 m_Pl` sits a factor ~28 below that, so
    calling it "the black-hole mass below which..." is a misnomer. `M_ext` is the physically
    meaningful threshold and is a new, recordable prediction.
