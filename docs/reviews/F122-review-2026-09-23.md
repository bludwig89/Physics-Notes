## Verdict

The core numerical claim survives independent re-derivation intact: a cold subagent, given only a
neutral physics claim card (Cornell-potential three-body problem, no mechanism disclosed), coded its
own solver from scratch and reproduced every quoted number in the finding to 3–4 significant
figures, via a genuinely different method (uncorrelated Gaussian-expansion + virial-theorem
self-check, vs. the finding's correlated-Gaussian ECG engine with explicit S₃ symmetrization). The
physics — confinement dominance, a discrete spectrum, the NR overshoot factor, the n–p splitting
sign and magnitude, and this session's new calibrated quark-mass fraction (0.068% proton, 0.094%
neutron) — is not fabricated, not hardcoded, and not an artifact of an unconverged basis.

**Narrowed claim:** the finding's numbers are correct, but the finding **overstates the
independence of two of its own inputs** (the F120/F121 quark-mass table is a PDG-value *consistency
readout*, not a model prediction, and F40 is misattributed as the source of the d/u mass ratio —
it isn't), **omits a load-bearing undocumented free parameter** ($\alpha_s=0.5$, unregistered
against D7), **does not disclose that its own test record is `result_dump`/battery-tier** (no hard
gate, no falsifiability control, silent-drift risk), and **does not cross-reference the later
F297/F372 episode** in which this same finding's S8 n–p splitting number was excluded at 36.6σ by
BBN light-element abundances and subsequently narrowed to 0.77σ by a dedicated theory-uncertainty
correction — material context absent from F122 itself. None of these narrow the *numbers*; they
narrow what a reader of F122 alone can safely conclude about their provenance and standing.

```
TARGET      Three-quark relative-coordinate ground state under a Cornell potential
            V(r) = sigma*r - (2*alpha_s/3)/r per pair (baryon 3bar Casimir factor);
            report: discreteness of the spectrum, the quark-mass-sum fraction of the
            total mass at (i) a toy degenerate current mass and (ii) the physical
            calibrated u/d masses, the NR m_p/sqrt(sigma) ratio at constituent mass,
            and the n-p mass splitting sign/magnitude.

            m_n-m_p = +1.5 MeV vs measured +1.293 MeV.               CLASS: quantitative
INPUTS      sigma=1 (units), alpha_s=0.5, m_q values, sqrt(sigma)=0.42 GeV anchor,
            2/3 (or 1/2-rule) colour-Casimir factor.
FORBIDDEN   findings/F122-*.md, baryon_dynamics.py, test_P2_baryon_bound_state.py,
            P2_baryon_bound_state.json, F122's findings-index.md line.
```

The claim card was sufficient for a blind agent to attempt the problem from standard constituent
quark-model knowledge alone; no leakage of the finding's specific ECG/Jacobi-symmetrization
mechanism was needed or given.

## Independent re-derivation

Subagent A built its own Jacobi-coordinate, uncorrelated Gaussian-expansion (Rayleigh-Ritz) solver
— a different basis construction from the finding's correlated, permutation-symmetrized ECG basis —
validated its own matrix elements against `scipy.integrate.quad` and Monte Carlo integration, cross
-checked its generalized-eigenproblem solve against direct `scipy.linalg.eigh`, and added an
independent virial-theorem self-consistency check ($2\langle T\rangle=\langle V_\text{lin}\rangle-
\langle V_\text{coul}\rangle$) not present anywhere in the original finding. Results:

| Quantity | F122 (`baryon_dynamics.py`) | Subagent A (independent code) |
|---|---|---|
| $E_\text{rel}$ at $m_q=0.785$ | 5.7398 | 5.7421 |
| $M/\sqrt\sigma$ at $m_q=0.785$ | 8.095 | 8.097 |
| Toy quark fraction | 0.1059% | 0.1060% |
| Calibrated proton fraction | 0.0675% | 0.0675% |
| Calibrated neutron fraction | 0.0937% | 0.0938% |
| $m_n-m_p$ | +1.51 MeV | +1.51 MeV |
| Overshoot vs. empirical $m_N/\sqrt\sigma$ | 3.62$\times$ | 3.62$\times$ |

Different route, same answer to 3–4 significant figures throughout — the strongest support category
this process distinguishes. Subagent A's added, unlicensed assumptions were purely numerical-method
choices (basis shape, regularization cutoff, and the standard textbook argument that a confining,
permutation-symmetric Hamiltonian's nodeless ground state is automatically totally symmetric, used
in place of the finding's explicit basis symmetrization) — nothing that could have steered it toward
the finding's specific numbers. No forbidden file was read.
