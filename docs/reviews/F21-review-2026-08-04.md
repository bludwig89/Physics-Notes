## Verdict

Every number in F21 is arithmetically correct and was reproduced bit-for-bit. The
finding's **physical conclusion is false**.

The residual F21 measures compares a **real** vector against an **imaginary** one.
`EM_bilinears` returns $E_G = \lvert n\rvert(G_T + G_T^*)$ and
$B_G = i\lvert n\rvert(G_T^* - G_T)$, both of which are *real* 3-vectors; the
harness then forms the right-hand side as `1j * cross(two_n, B0)`, which is *pure
imaginary*. The two sides are orthogonal in $\mathbb C^3$ by construction, and
their magnitudes were measured equal to ten decimal places at every $k$ on every
geometry. So $\lVert\text{LHS}-\text{RHS}\rVert = \sqrt2\,\lVert\text{RHS}\rVert$
identically, and

$$R = \frac{\sqrt2\,\lVert\text{RHS}\rVert}{2\varepsilon\lvert k\rvert}
    = \frac{\sqrt2\,\sin(\Omega/2)}{\lvert k\rvert}
    \;\longrightarrow\; \frac{c_\text{lat}}{\sqrt2},
    \qquad \Omega = 2\omega(k/2).$$

The $\sqrt2$ is $\sqrt{1^2+1^2}$ from adding a real and an imaginary vector in
quadrature. The $c_\text{lat}$ is $c_\text{lat}$ *by its own definition*
($c_\text{lat}\equiv d\Omega/d\lvert k\rvert$, Core Design Decision 2). There is
nothing else in the number.

**With the representation made consistent** — drop the `1j` and use the real-field
curl, or promote $E_G,B_G$ to analytic amplitudes $\hat E = 2g,\ \hat B = 2\hat n\times g$
— the residual collapses to zero. Measured on both geometries, two independent
ways agreeing to 3–4 digits: $O(k)$ at $\Delta t = 1$ with coefficient $c^2/4$, and
$O(k^2)$ for the exact derivative with coefficient $c^3/48$, i.e. the residual
itself is $O(k^3)$. The repo's own recorded pass criterion for this construction
(`references/qca-papers-1-4-overview.md:407`) reads: *"measured violation of the
curl equations decays as $O(k^3)$ as $\lvert k\rvert\to0$."* **The construction
meets it.**

**Corrected claim, one sentence:** *For the helicity-eigenstate pairing the
quantity F21 measures is identically $\sqrt2\sin(\Omega/2)/\lvert k\rvert \to
c_\text{lat}/\sqrt2$ by the definition of $c_\text{lat}$, because `EM_bilinears`
returns real $E_G, B_G$ while the residual's right-hand side carries an explicit
$i$; with the representation made consistent the un-smeared pointwise bilinear
satisfies the lattice curl equation to $O(k^3)$ with coefficient $c_\text{lat}^3/48$,
so geometry, smearing and the bilinear are all exonerated, and the only genuine
discrepancy is the $O(k^3)$ gap between the lattice curl symbol
$2\tilde n = 2\sin(\omega(k/2))$ and the rotation rate $\Omega = 2\omega(k/2)$.*

## Independent re-derivation

Subagent A read only the engine modules and `qca-papers-1-4-overview.md`, never
F21, the forks, or their results. It confirmed no forbidden file was read.

**It got a closed form, valid at all $k$, where the finding has only a fit.** For
any walk $U(k) = uI - i\sigma\cdot n$ with $u^2+\lvert n\rvert^2=1$ — which covers
BCC and every cubic variant — with $\psi$ a helicity eigenvector of $\hat n\cdot\sigma$
at $k/2$:

| Lemma | Statement | Numerical check |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | $M = \psi\varphi^T$ is rank 1, so $\det M = 0 \Rightarrow G\cdot G = (\varphi^T\psi)^2$ | $6.7\times10^{-14}$, 2000 random pairs |
| L2 | $G\cdot\hat n = \varphi^T(\hat n\cdot\sigma)\psi = \lambda(\varphi^T\psi)$, $\lambda=\pm1$ | $1.7\times10^{-15}$ |
| L3 | $G_T\cdot G_T = G\cdot G - (G\cdot\hat n)^2 = 0$ — **$G_T$ is a complex null vector**, exactly circularly polarised, at all $k$, for any $\varphi$ | $5.7\times10^{-15}$ |

Nullity gives $E\perp B$ and $\lVert E\rVert = \lVert B\rVert \equiv \varepsilon$
*exactly*, and $\hat n\times B = \mp E$. Then with $g\to e^{-i\Omega}g$:

$$\lVert\text{LHS}-\text{RHS}\rVert^2 = \varepsilon^2\bigl[4\sin^4\tfrac\Omega2 + 4\sin^2\tfrac\Omega2 + 4\sin^2\tfrac\Omega2\cos^2\tfrac\Omega2\bigr] = 8\varepsilon^2\sin^2\tfrac\Omega2$$

so $R = \sqrt2\sin(\Omega/2)/\lvert k\rvert$. Verified to $10^{-14}$ in float64 and
$\sim10^{-30}$ in mpmath at 60 dps.

**A's own conclusion, verbatim:** *"Universality across geometries is therefore not
a discovery about the lattices — it is **tautological** once the closed form is in
hand."* Because $c_\text{lat}\equiv d\Omega/d\lvert k\rvert$, the limit
$\sin(\Omega/2)/\lvert k\rvert \to c_\text{lat}/2$ holds **by definition**.

A's measurements, four $k$ directions, $k$ from $10^{-1}$ to $10^{-20}$:

**The degeneracy A found.** For unit spinors, exactly:

$$\psi=\varphi=\psi_+:\ \lVert G_T\rVert = \sqrt2\,\lvert\hat n_y\rvert,
\qquad
\psi=\psi_+,\varphi=\psi_-:\ \lVert G_T\rVert = \sqrt{2-2\hat n_y^2}.$$

## The two findings that decide the verdict

Neither is on the attack list; both were raised by the blind agent and independently
verified by the referee.

**(a) The relative mismatch is $\sqrt2 = 141\%$ at every $k$ — so $R$ is not a
small-$k$ discretisation error.** Measured $1.414213562373$ at $k = 0.5,\,0.1,\,10^{-3}$
on both geometries, with $\lVert\text{LHS}-\text{RHS}\rVert^2 = \lVert\text{LHS}\rVert^2+\lVert\text{RHS}\rVert^2$
to $1.7\times10^{-18}$ and $\lVert\text{LHS}\rVert/\lVert\text{RHS}\rVert = 1.0000000000$.
The two sides never approximately agree; $R$ only *looks* like it vanishes because
an already-$O(k)$ numerator is divided by $\lvert k\rvert$. F21's text is
incompatible with this — "the residual stays $O(k)$… only the coefficient changes"
(F21:60), and `bilinear.py:69` "The linearisation error is exactly
$c_\text{lat}/\sqrt2\cdot\lvert k\rvert$ — a structural consequence of operating at
finite $\Delta t = 1$" — both describe a $\Delta t\to0$-convergent linearisation
error. It is neither: it is $\Delta t$-independent and $k$-independent in relative
terms.

**(b) Under a consistent representation the residual goes to zero, so
$c_\text{lat}/\sqrt2$ is a convention constant.** Verified two independent ways:

| geometry | corrected residual$/\lvert k\rvert$, $\Delta t{=}1$ | closed form | exact-derivative residual | closed form |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BCC | $8.408\!\times\!10^{-3}\to8.334\!\times\!10^{-5}\to8.333\!\times\!10^{-6}$ | $c^2k/4 = k/12$ | $4.064\!\times\!10^{-5}\to4.015\!\times\!10^{-7}\to4.010\!\times\!10^{-9}$ | $c^3k^2/48$ |
| cubic | $2.498\!\times\!10^{-2}\to2.500\!\times\!10^{-4}\to2.500\!\times\!10^{-5}$ | $c^2k/4 = k/4$ | $2.082\!\times\!10^{-4}\to2.083\!\times\!10^{-6}\to2.083\!\times\!10^{-8}$ | $c^3k^2/48$ |

The entire genuine lattice discrepancy is
$\lvert\Omega - 2\lvert n\rvert\rvert/(2k)$ — the gap between the lattice curl
symbol $2\tilde n = 2\sin(\omega(k/2))$ and the rotation rate $\Omega = 2\omega(k/2)$
— and it is $O(k^2)$, i.e. the residual is $O(k^3)$.

**The mechanism, stated once.** Bisio Eq. 35 defines $E_G = \lvert n\rvert(G_T + G_T^\dagger)$
with $G_T^\dagger$ the **operator** adjoint. `bilinear.py:254` implements it as
`np.conj(G_T)` on a **c-number** vector, which makes $E_G$ and $B_G$ real and the
$i$ on the right-hand side unpayable. That is precisely **Finding 2's hypothesis
#3** — *"the curl equation as stated holds only as an operator-algebra identity…
not as a pointwise classical identity on c-number bilinears"*
(`F01-F15-findings.md:654`). F21's verdict (F21:71) explicitly deprioritised
hypothesis #3 and directed the next fork at hypothesis #1, smearing. **F23 then
spent a session ruling out smearing, self-checking against F21's constant.** So the
smearing programme was aimed at a convention, and the diagnosis it needed was
already written down in Finding 2 and was steered away from by F21.

1. **Grading attack 12 FAIL rather than WEAKENS on the doubler count.** F21's own
   caveat (line 75) says it "measures doublers *as simulated*, which is the
   physically relevant count" — a legitimate defence if the field really lives on a
   unit-spacing simple-cubic array. The counter is that a symbol with period
   $2\pi\sqrt3$ is not a valid nearest-neighbour walk symbol on such an array at
   all, which the referee believes but did not trace to a design document.
2. **REFUTED rather than OVERSTATED.** The title claim and boxed equation are
   arithmetically correct and every number was reproduced. REFUTED was chosen
   because the finding's *conclusion* — "the curl failure is intrinsic to the
   pointwise un-smeared bilinear", "geometry is ruled out as the cause" — is false
   rather than merely broad, and it is the conclusion that propagated into the
   inventory, the status doc, the reference doc, and the F23 session.
3. **Attack 1 FAIL rather than WEAKENS.** $c_\text{lat}$ is genuinely measured, not
   imported, so the circularity is confined to the $1/\sqrt6$ self-check target. A
   reviewer weighting the constant more heavily than the target could reasonably
   call it WEAKENS.
