# CL269 — One time, from the update's own commutant

## Statement

The commutant of the BCC Weyl update $A = u\mathbb I - i\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\tilde{\mathbf n}$
inside the **local homogeneous unitaries** — $2\times2$ matrices over the Laurent ring
$R=\mathbb C[w_1^{\pm1},w_2^{\pm1},w_3^{\pm1}]$, where "local" *is* "Laurent" — is
2-dimensional and free of rank 2 over $R$, and $\det$ splits it into

- **exactly** the shift lattice $U(1)\times\mathbb Z^{3}$ (the scalar part: $aa^*=1$ forces $a$ to
  be a unit of $R$, and the units of a Laurent ring are the monomials $\zeta w^{\mathbf m}$), and
- **exactly** the powers of the update: $\ker(\det)$ is the Pell group of the quadratic extension
  $R[\sqrt N]$, $N=1-u^2$, which is squarefree, and whose fundamental unit is $A$ itself —
  $(a,b)=(u,-i)$ with $\deg b = 0$, the minimum a nonzero $b$ can have.

Hence $d_\text{time} = 1$ at the model's cell $s=2$, alongside — and **separately from** — F291's
spatial bound $d_\text{space}\le 2\log_2 s+1 = 3$. The two share the $s=2$ premise; neither is an
instance of a single formula in $s$.

Further, $\langle A\rangle\cong\mathbb Z$ and $A$ is **primitive** — no power of the update is a
shift, and there is no local "half tick" — so the model's time is infinite and discrete rather
than a cyclic clock.

**Inputs: three.** $s=2$; **infinite volume**; and the three Cayley generators of the automaton's
own BCC group $\Lambda$. Writing the algebra over the Laurent ring *is* the locality assumption.

## What it extends

Special and general relativity, quantum field theory and the Standard Model all take the Lorentzian
signature $(3,1)$ — three space, **one** time — as an **input**. Tegmark-style arguments for one
time are anthropic and external (ultrahyperbolic equations have no well-posed initial-value
problem); they select the signature by consequence, not by construction. This claim derives the
"one" from the algebraic structure of the automaton's own update, with no appeal to well-posedness
or observers, and from the *same* premise ($s=2$) that F291's spatial bound uses.

**Prior art, and what is actually new** (added 2026-08-13, review attacks 6 and 11). The
mathematics is **rediscovery**: multivariate polynomial Pell, the Chebyshev parametrisation and the
rank-one solution group are in Pastor (2001), Dubickas–Steuding (2004) Thm 2, Avanzi–Zannier (2001),
Webb–Yokota (2002) and Nathanson (1976). The signature-derivation literature did not stop at
Tegmark 1997 either — random-chronon Lorentzian-signature emergence with an exclusivity theorem
(*Rep. Adv. Phys. Sci.* 09, 2025) and arXiv:2510.07891 are live competitors. Most relevantly, in
the **D'Ariano–Perinotti QCA program** "space is the Cayley graph, time is the computational step"
is the *standing construction* — i.e. the very object this claim says is not a choice. What appears
genuinely new is the **QCA-commutant framing**: computing the split rather than declaring it.

It also supersedes-by-narrowing this project's own prior position: F291 §6 recorded the "+1" as
`structural` — *"time is one-dimensional by the automaton's construction"* — and F291 §7 named the
missing step as *"why the Cayley-graph/update split is not itself a choice"*. That step is what
CL269 asserts is now taken.

## Falsifier

Any one of these kills it, and each is a computation rather than an appeal:

1. **A local homogeneous unitary commuting with $A$ that is scalar on the cell and is not a
   monomial.** The scalar part would exceed the shift lattice.
2. **A Pell solution $(a,b)$, $b\ne0$, that is not $\zeta A^{n}$** — equivalently, a $\deg b$ that
   fails to descend. The time rank is then $\ge2$ and the model has two times.
3. **$N=1-u^2$ shown to be a square in $R$.** The extension splits: two automata, each with its own
   clock.
4. **Any $n\ne0$ with $A^{n}=\zeta\,w^{\mathbf m}\mathbb I$.** Time closes into a finite clock and
   becomes a space direction after $n$ ticks.
5. **$V=\mathbb I\otimes A$ shown to survive the model's interactions.** **RUN — did not fire**
   (F315, 2026-08-12): $V$ is broken maximally by contact and photon-exchange kernels and its
   $O(G)$ deformation is obstructed. The live remainder of this falsifier is the **F86 colour
   dielectric**, which F315 did not test, and the all-orders statement.
6. **Evidence for a fundamental cell $s\ne2$.** *(Restated 2026-08-13.)* The $s=2$ premise is
   shared with F291's spatial bound, so this attacks both halves of A1 at once. It does **not**
   hand back a predicted $d_\text{time}$: no $s$-general formula is established, and the free $s=4$
   cell measured **2**.
7. ~~Prove or refute the polynomial→Laurent transfer.~~ **PROVED — F316, 2026-08-13.** The
   contingency is discharged. Its live successor is F316's sharpest falsifier: **exhibit $f$ with
   $D(f)=0$ that is not a monomial**, which would break the replacement for the failing hypothesis.
8. **Break the infinite-volume premise.** At what finite $L$, if any, does a rank $>1$ commutant
   appear? At finite $L$ the ring is not a domain and §§ on the scalar part and the Pell group both
   collapse, so this is a real boundary, not a technicality.

## Status & history

### Reviewed 2026-08-13 — OVERSTATED, and narrowed here

An independent cold review (blind re-derivation + 13-attack referee) graded the finding
**OVERSTATED**: 3 PASS, 8 WEAKENS, 2 FAIL. This card was the object that had to move, and it has.

**What the review CONFIRMED, which is the substance.** A blind agent, reading only Paper 1 Eq. 15,
reproduced $d_\text{time}=1$, $\ker(\det)\cong\mathbb Z$ generated by $A$, and the scalar part
$=U(1)\times\mathbb Z^3$ — by a **different route** (Newton-polytope support-function descent, no
cited theorem at all). It also proved *more*: $A$ is **primitive**, so there is no local "half
tick". That is the strongest support the review process can produce, and the core claim stands.

**What was WITHDRAWN.**

1. **The $(\dim\mathfrak{su}(s),\operatorname{rank}\mathfrak{su}(s))$ identity** — numerology.
   The model's own bound is $2\log_2 s+1$, which equals $s^2-1$ only at $s=2$ (5 vs 15 at $s=4$),
   and ≥25 equally natural function pairs give $(3,1)$. This is CLAUDE.md's constants rule applied
   to functions.
2. **"$d_\text{space}=3$ recovered from the commutant"** — circular. The $\mathbb Z^3$ is the unit
   group of $R$, and $R$ has three variables because three Cayley generators were put in. The
   commutant proves *no extra* translations, not *three*.
3. **The Abel attribution** — wrong paper. Abel 1826 contains no group-structure theorem.

**What was CORRECTED.** The published $\gcd(\tilde n_i)=1+i$ was a sympy `ZZ_I` content artifact;
over $\mathbb Q(i)$ it is **1**. `exactness` drops `exact` → `quantitative`. The check count was
11 in three places and is **12**.

**`contingent`, now on ONE thing, and it is sharper than what it replaces.**

**The polynomial→Laurent transfer.** Dubickas–Steuding Thm 2 runs on $k[x]$ with a degree obeying
"$\deg f=0\Rightarrow f$ constant", which **fails** in $R=\mathbb C[w^{\pm}]$
($\deg(1+w^{-1})=0$, not a unit). That transfer — not "Abel is imported" — is the real gap, and
the review's blind agent supplied a route to closing it in-repo (the two-sided degree). Until that
is proved here, `confidence: high`.

**Inputs were undercounted by two** and are now declared in `## Statement`: **infinite volume** and
the three Cayley generators, alongside $s=2$. Infinite volume is load-bearing — at finite $L$ the
ring is not a domain, units are not monomials, and both halves of the split collapse.

**The composite-cell residual — DISCHARGED 2026-08-12 by F315.** At $s=4$ the model's own massive
Dirac walk carries a **second** dispersive commuting flow $V=\mathbb I_\text{branch}\otimes A$, at
every mass ($\lVert[V,D]\rVert\le2.2\times10^{-16}$ at $m=0,\,0.37,\,0.8$, measured in F313's C7).
When this card was issued that made the rank-1 count a theorem at the **minimal** cell
specifically, and F313 §9's reading — that $V$ is the $s=2$ update lifted branch-blind, the
fundamental clock beside its mass-dressed self rather than a new one — was labelled a **reading**,
not a result, and left as falsifier 5.

**Falsifier 5 was run and did not fire.** F315 puts the model's own walk in a homogeneous,
antisymmetrised two-particle sector at fixed total momentum and finds $V$ broken **maximally**
($\max\lvert\Delta\phi_V\rvert=3.113$, i.e. $\simeq\pi$) by both the contact kernel (Hubbard/NJL,
F217/F77) and photon exchange (F68, $1/q^2$) — and, decisively, finds the $O(G)$ **deformation
obstructed**: 1 340 resonant elements, where the denominator $\Delta\Omega$ of
$Q_2=\Delta q_V W/\Delta\Omega$ vanishes, carry $\Delta q_V$ up to $2.876$. So no *dressed* $V$
survives either, which is the form of the question that actually decides it — an interacting
theory can carry a deformed charge, and that is what integrability is. The test is controlled in
both directions: an integrable mode-diagonal interaction gives **zero** obstructed elements (so the
test can report survival), and on the same resonant set the evolution's own charge is conserved to

**Consequence for this card.** $V$ is an artifact of **freeness**. The rank-1 count is therefore
**not** confined to the minimal cell — it is the count of the *interacting* theory at any cell. One

**What this card does not claim.** The **arrow** of time. $\langle A\rangle\cong\mathbb Z$ has two

That belongs to rubric row A4 (C, P, T separately), which remains PARTIAL with a thin T, and this

## Amendment 2026-08-16 — the criterion this count needs on a cell with an internal factor (F318)

F313 counts time directions by the commutant of the update, on the minimal $s=2$ cell. The model's
quark cell is not minimal, and an internal factor **enlarges** the commutant: measured in
[[F318-cell-carries-the-internal-index]], over generic momenta, it factorises exactly as
$\dim\mathcal C_\text{branch}\times N^2$ — $2\to18$ at $N=3$. A maximal abelian subalgebra of
that would suggest $2N-1=5$ time directions.

It does not, and the reason is one measurement: **a commuting unitary is a clock only if its
eigenphase depends on momentum.** The update disperses at $0.2846$ per unit $k$; every internal
element disperses at literal **`0.0`** and commutes with the hop at literal `0.0`. A
non-dispersive commuting unitary is a global internal symmetry, not a second time direction.

F313 never had to state this criterion because it never had an internal factor. The contrast is
not hypothetical: F313 §9's own second commuting flow on the $s=4$ composite **was** dispersive —
which is exactly why it was a candidate clock, why §9 could only call it a *reading*, and why
[[F315-V-does-not-survive-interaction]] had to kill it with interactions. The colour elements were
never candidates.

**Nothing here is retracted.** $d_\text{time}=1$ stands and now stands on a coloured cell too.
The one scope note: F318 measures the commutant in momentum space over seven probe momenta, not
over F313's Laurent ring; the added elements are constants, hence degree-0 Laurent and local by
F313's own definition, so the extension is expected to be exact but is not re-derived on that
footing. See also **CL272**.
