# CL018 — No absolute neutrino mass is predicted — the see-saw supplies a mechanism, not a scale

## Statement

F47 supplies a Higgs-free see-saw **mechanism**. Nothing in the model fixes $M_R$, so **no absolute neutrino mass is predicted**.

## What it extends

Recorded so that **absence is not read as a prediction**. This is a scope boundary, not a result: the project does not assert it, and `status: not_claimed` is the assertion that it does not assert it.

## Evidence

| Source | What it shows | Exactness |
|---|---|---|
| `findings/F47-majorana-seesaw-higgs-free.md` | The Higgs-free Majorana step and see-saw mechanism; $M_R$ is not fixed by it | external |

The same F47 step is what *closes* charge quantisation (CL010) — the mechanism is load-bearing even though its scale is not predicted.

## Falsifier

None, and the structure is named: F47 supplies the see-saw **operator** $\nu_R^{\mathsf T}C\nu_R$ and shows gauge invariance forces $y_\nu=0$, but the operator's coefficient $M_R$ is not constrained by any lattice quantity. A measured absolute neutrino mass therefore fixes $M_R$ rather than testing the model. **KATRIN, $0\nu\beta\beta$ and cosmological mass bounds are all silent on this card** — that is precisely what it records.
