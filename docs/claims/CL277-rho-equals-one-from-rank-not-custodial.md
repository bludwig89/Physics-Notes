# CL277 — rho = 1 exactly, from the rank of the Higgs-free breaking rather than from custodial SU(2)

## Statement

The $(W^3,B)$ mass matrix generated by the model's Higgs-free breaking is

$$M^2=\frac{v^2}{4}\,w\,w^{\!\top},\qquad w=(g,\,-g'),$$

a **rank-one** form, because F41 absorbs exactly one Stueckelberg direction on $U(x)$ — the
single combination $\Delta Y=Y_L-Y_{e_R}=+1$. Its determinant vanishes *identically* in the
stiffness quantum $u$, not at a tuned point, so the photon is exactly massless and

$$\rho\equiv\frac{m_W^2}{m_Z^2\cos^2\theta_W}=1$$

**exactly, for every $u$ and every $g'/g$**. The massless eigenvector is $(\sqrt2,\sqrt7)/3$ at
the BCC counts.

## What it extends

In the Standard Model $\rho=1$ at tree level follows from the Higgs doublet's custodial $SU(2)$
(Veltman 1977). This model has no Higgs field (F27/F41) and therefore cannot use that argument.
It supplies a different one that imposes nothing: $\rho=1$ is a consequence of the **rank** of a
breaking mechanism that F41 introduced for an unrelated reason (avoiding the Higgs), and it is
established by evaluating a determinant over $\mathbb{Q}$ rather than by assuming a custodial
symmetry. Technicolour and composite-Higgs constructions reach $\rho=1$ by imposing a custodial
$SU(2)\subset SU(4)$; this does not.

Before F320 the tree carried **no** derivation of $\rho$ at all, which completeness row B12 named
in its requirement.

## Falsifier

`none`, and the structure is named: the statement is that a rank-one quadratic form on a
two-dimensional space has a zero eigenvalue. That is linear algebra, and it cannot be falsified by
observation. What *is* falsifiable, and is a different claim, is the **premise** — that the
model's breaking is rank one. If the electroweak condensate were shown to carry a second
independent direction (a second, abelian-charged order parameter), the premise fails and $\rho$
leaves 1. The record's `rank_two_breaking=true` control is exactly that scenario, run: B1a, B1b
and B1c go red and nothing else does.

Measured $\rho$ deviating from 1 beyond the known SM loop corrections would falsify the premise,
not this card's algebra.
