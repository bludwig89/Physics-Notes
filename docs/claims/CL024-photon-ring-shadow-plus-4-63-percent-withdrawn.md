# CL024 — WITHDRAWN — photon-ring imaging at +4.63% shadow radius

## Statement

**RETRACTED.** The shadow radius was predicted at $+4.63\%$ over Schwarzschild ($2e$ against $3\sqrt3$), a target for EHT-class photon-ring imaging. **The model no longer predicts it.**

## What it extends

It asserted an imageable deviation from the GR shadow. It no longer does.

## Falsifier

**Inverted.** An EHT shadow matching Schwarzschild would previously have falsified the model. **It does not.**

## Status & history

**Withdrawn 2026-08-02** (summary revision 2), superseded by F178. The card is kept because **this was published as a live claim between 2026-06-08 and 2026-08-02**, and a withdrawn claim that is deleted is a claim that gets re-made. A consequence of the exponential-metric-as-fundamental reading. The **raytracer** (F186) survives as production code; only the prediction it was pointed at is withdrawn.
