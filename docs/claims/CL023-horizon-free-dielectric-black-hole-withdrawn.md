# CL023 — WITHDRAWN — black holes are horizon-free dielectric condensates

## Statement

**RETRACTED.** The former core claim 9 asserted that black holes are horizon-free dielectric condensates — the exponential metric $K=e^{2GM/rc^2}$ read as fundamental gives a throat rather than a horizon. **The model no longer predicts this.** Under F178 the exact vacuum solution is **Schwarzschild, with a horizon**, and the exponential form is the PPN-order weak-field representation only.

## What it extends

It asserted a departure from general relativity's horizon structure. It no longer does. The model's black holes are GR's black holes.

## Falsifier

**Inverted, and that is the important part.** An observation of a horizon — or of anything matching Schwarzschild — **would previously have been recorded as falsifying the model. It does not.** Anyone holding the pre-2026-08-02 papers should read this card before scoring the model against a horizon observation.

## Status & history

**Withdrawn 2026-08-02** (summary revision 2), superseded by F178. The card is kept because **this was published as a live claim between 2026-06-08 and 2026-08-02**, and a withdrawn claim that is deleted is a claim that gets re-made. F178 demotes the exponential metric; the throat, and every consequence listed in CL024–CL027, followed from treating the exponential form as fundamental. F114 is **not deleted** — it is the record of a derivation that was correct given its premise and wrong about the premise.
