# CL015 — PPN beta = gamma = 1 (Mercury 42.98 arcsec/cy); the linear dielectric is excluded

## Statement

PPN $\beta=\gamma=1$, giving Mercury's perihelion advance as $42.98''$/cy. The naive **linear** dielectric ($\beta=\tfrac12$, $50.1''$/cy) is **excluded**.

## What it extends

Nothing, as of F178 — and that is the point of the narrowing. Before F178 this was read as a distinctive prediction of the exponential metric. It is now a **consistency requirement**: in vacuum the model *is* GR.

## Evidence

| Source | What it shows | Exactness |
|---|---|---|
| `findings/F64-em-connection-gravity.md` | The impedance-matched dielectric gives $\beta=\gamma=1$; the linear form gives $\beta=\tfrac12$ and is excluded | quantitative |
| `findings/F178-gravity-full-tensor-adoption.md` | Reclassification: the exact vacuum solution is Schwarzschild, so PPN agreement is required rather than predicted | — |

## Falsifier

A confirmed PPN deviation falsifies the model **exactly as it would falsify GR** — no more and no less. The genuinely discriminating content is the *exclusion* of the linear dielectric, which was a live alternative inside the model and is dead.

## Status & history

`narrowed` by **F178 / revision 2 (2026-08-02)**. The broad form — "Mercury precession is a distinctive prediction of the lattice dielectric" — is withdrawn: under the full-tensor source the vacuum solution is Schwarzschild and the claim carries no discriminating power against GR. It is retained rather than deleted because the *internal* exclusion of $\beta=\tfrac12$ is real and was earned.
