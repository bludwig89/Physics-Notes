# CL017 — The CKM matrix and measured CP violation are out of scope

## Statement

The construction is **first-generation**. $J=0$ is the **arithmetic consequence of one generation** — $N_\text{phase}=(n-1)(n-2)/2$ — not a prediction that the CKM phase vanishes. Three-generation mixing is out of scope.

## What it extends

Recorded so that **absence is not read as a prediction**. This is a scope boundary, not a result: the project does not assert it, and `status: not_claimed` is the assertion that it does not assert it. The distinction matters: a reader could take the model's vanishing Jarlskog invariant as a prediction contradicted by experiment. It is not a prediction at all.

## Falsifier

None, and the structure is named: the model's $J=0$ is a **counting identity**, $N_\text{phase}=(n-1)(n-2)/2$ evaluated at $n=1$. An identity has no observational content, so no measurement of CP violation bears on it either way. The card is falsified only by the project extending the construction to three generations and *then* predicting a phase — which would be a status change here, not an experiment.
