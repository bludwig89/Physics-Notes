# CL005 — The Koide relation Q = 2/3 is an exact 45-degree equipartition of the cubic amplitude sqrt(m)

## Statement

$Q=\tfrac23$ is an exact $45^\circ$ equipartition of the cubic amplitude $\sqrt m$, with $Q(\phi)=\tfrac13\cos^2\phi$; electromagnetism selects the colour-free charged leptons to sit there.

## What it extends

The Koide relation, an unexplained empirical coincidence among the charged-lepton masses in the Standard Model. Here it is a fixed point of the model's phase structure rather than a numerical accident.

## Evidence

| Source | What it shows | Exactness |
|---|---|---|
| `findings/F92-per-constituent-phase-consistency.md` | The two mass laws are jointly satisfiable only at $45^\circ$ | exact |
| `findings/F93-orthorhombic-Eg-vacuum.md` | The orthorhombic vacuum as an $E_g$ condensate on the second-neighbour shell | exact |

Recorded numbers: model $Q=0.6666605$ against $\tfrac23$, i.e. $0.91\sigma$; predicts $m_\tau=1776.97$ MeV ($6\times10^{-5}$). **Zero free parameters.**

## Falsifier

A charged-lepton mass measurement that moves $Q$ off $\tfrac23$ by more than the quoted $0.91\sigma$ band, or an $m_\tau$ measurement inconsistent with $1776.97$ MeV at the $6\times10^{-5}$ level.

## Status & history

`live`. The *magnitude* of the charged-lepton spectrum is a separate and later claim — the shape angle $\delta^*=\tfrac29$, **CL028**, founding decision 7 — which post-dates revision 3 of the summary and supersedes F179/CN3. CL005 is the $Q=\tfrac23$ equipartition only and does not depend on it.
