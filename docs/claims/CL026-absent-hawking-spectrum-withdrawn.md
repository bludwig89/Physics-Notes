# CL026 — WITHDRAWN — absence of a thermal Hawking spectrum

## Statement

**RETRACTED.** With no horizon there is no Hawking temperature, so the absence of a thermal Hawking spectrum was predicted. **The model no longer predicts it** — with a Schwarzschild horizon the usual Hawking result applies.

## What it extends

It asserted a departure from semiclassical black-hole thermodynamics. It no longer does.

## Evidence

| Source | What it shows | Exactness |
|---|---|---|
| `findings/F114-dielectric-black-hole.md` | The absent-Hawking prediction, as originally derived | quantitative |
| `findings/F190-horizon-entropy-lattice-microstates.md` | Horizon entropy from lattice microstates — a live result that presupposes the horizon this claim denied | quantitative |
| `findings/F178-gravity-full-tensor-adoption.md` | The supersession | — |

## Falsifier

**Inverted.** Detection of a thermal Hawking spectrum would previously have falsified the model. **It does not.**

## Status & history

**Withdrawn 2026-08-02** (summary revision 2), superseded by F178. The card is kept because **this was published as a live claim between 2026-06-08 and 2026-08-02**, and a withdrawn claim that is deleted is a claim that gets re-made. F190's horizon-entropy result is the sharpest internal evidence that this withdrawal was correct: it counts microstates *of a horizon*, which the withdrawn claim said did not exist.
