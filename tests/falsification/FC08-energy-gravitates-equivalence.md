# FC08 — Field/binding energy gravitates per unit energy as rest mass (ratio = 1)

**Tier:** C — consistency regression (with one decisive *internal* discriminator)
**Falsification power:** ★★★★ (kills the rest-mass-sourced gravity fork; confirms the energy-sourced dielectric to 10⁻¹⁵)
**Model element under test:** the F64/F106 sourcing law ∇²ln K = −(8πG/c⁴)T⁰⁰ — gravity couples to **energy density**, not rest-mass density, so every form of energy (rest mass, EM/nuclear binding, gravitational self-energy, field energy, kinetic) gravitates per unit energy identically.

## Hypothesis (parameter-free prediction)
The ratio of the gravitational coupling of any energy form to that of rest mass is **exactly 1**:

  R ≡ (m_grav from field/binding energy) / (m_inertial from the same energy) = 1.

In-simulation realisation: F64 D-EM3 measures radiation-vs-rest-mass deflection ratio = **1.00002** (numerical residual; the prediction is exactly 1). The same statement appears as γ = β = 1 and the factor-2 light bending (FA09).

## Measured targets + sources (hard numbers)
1. **Weak Equivalence Principle, composition independence (binding/field energy gravitates).** MICROSCOPE final result: η(Ti,Pt) = [−1.5 ± 2.3(stat) ± 1.5(syst)]×10⁻¹⁵ (Touboul et al., *PRL* **129**, 121102, 2022). Different test materials carry different fractional nuclear/EM binding energies; equal free-fall ⇒ binding energy gravitates as rest mass to ~10⁻¹⁵.
2. **Direct active-gravitational-mass of binding energy.** Kreuzer 1968 (F, Br binding energy) to ~5×10⁻⁵; Bartlett–Van Buren 1986 (lunar core/crust composition) to ~10⁻⁹ — active = passive gravitational mass of nuclear/EM energy.
3. **Gravitational self-energy gravitates (Nordtvedt effect).** Lunar laser ranging: η_N = (−0.6 ± 5.2)×10⁻⁴ (Hofmann & Müller; Williams–Turyshev–Boggs) ⇒ gravitational binding energy of the Earth gravitates normally to ~5×10⁻⁴.
4. **EM field energy responds to gravity (deflection / Shapiro).** Cassini: |γ−1| < 2.3×10⁻⁵ (Bertotti–Iess–Tortora 2003); solar limb deflection 1.751″ (VLBI). Pure field energy bends by the GR factor-2 (FA09).

## Falsification criterion
Falsified if **R ≠ 1** is detected:
1. a composition-dependent free-fall (η ≠ 0) beyond MICROSCOPE's 10⁻¹⁵, attributable to a binding/field-energy fraction, **or**
2. a Nordtvedt polarisation of the lunar orbit (η_N ≠ 0) beyond LLR's ~10⁻⁴, **or**
3. EM/field energy failing to source or respond to gravity per unit energy as rest mass (γ ≠ 1 beyond Cassini).

## What this brief decisively settles (the internal discriminator)
R = 1 is **shared with general relativity** — so items 1–4 *confirm* the model at extraordinary precision but do **not** distinguish it from GR. Their decisive power is internal to the project:

- **Energy-sourced dielectric (F64, canonical):** R = 1. Massless field energy gravitates and bends light (light-bends-light, D-EM6); binding energy adds weight.
- **Rest-mass-sourced fork (F50/F52/F62, superseded):** R = 0 for pure field energy — massless energy sources nothing.

The hard numbers (light bends by factor-2; binding energy gravitates to 10⁻¹⁵; gravitational self-energy gravitates to 10⁻⁴) lie at **R = 1**, decisively excluding the rest-mass fork and confirming the dielectric. This is the empirical content of CLAUDE.md decision 4.

## Where a *GR-vs-model* discriminator lives (now resolved → FC09)
R = 1 for energy density does not probe the **pressure / stress** components (Tolman active mass ∝ ρ + 3p/c²). This has now been computed (**F173**): the single-scalar dielectric sources the time potential from energy density **only**, omitting GR's 3p Tolman term. It is null in the solar system (∼10⁻¹¹, so β = γ = 1 and every test on this page passes), but predicts a **2–42% interior departure for neutron stars** (s = GM/Rc² = 0.1–0.3). That sharp, two-sided discriminator is carried in **FC09 (neutron-star pressure/redshift)** — the first place the model departs from GR itself.
